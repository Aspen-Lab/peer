## Marks

The margin mark is the app's result (list entry plus route image). **Convention** (so that the count can be compared with P12-39's): any axis title, tick label, legend, panel letter or label line cut by the crop is WRONG REGION (clipped); a hairline of page furniture inside the 10 pt pad is not counted. "Measured" means the scratch prototype (flags in a copy of the engine, never in the repository), run on P4–P6 **and again on P1–P3**, and against copies of `pdf-engine.test.ts`, `pdf-engine.caption-pairing.test.ts`, `pdf-engine.work-budget.test.ts` (61 tests; 61 green with no rule on). ### M9 and M10 (no piece over the floors; the caption-blind sibling)
- **R9a (chosen), with R10a: caption-bounded band growth.** For each typed caption line whose figure is above it: the band runs from the nearest body-like line above it (a line of at least 70 characters and 100 pt, or another typed caption) or the page's top furniture band (top 46 pt) down to the caption. Seed: the captioned region if there is one; otherwise (an orphan caption) the nearest piece within 220 pt. Absorb every piece in the band with no gap limit: sub-floor pieces of at least 3,000 pt^2 and 30 pt on both sides (R9a), and above-floor pieces that carry **no typed caption of their own** (R10a: a sibling the caption is blind to, or a panel too far to be captioned).
