## The rule

1. **The notes window carries "Open as page".** In the header of `notes-window.tsx` (in front only, as Export is; behind another window the header shows the title and the X alone), a link (`<a href="/notes/<id>">`, Next's `Link`, so Tab reaches it and Enter presses it) labelled "Open as page" with the accessible name "Open as page: your notes on this paper". A plain press calls `openAsPage` (exported, pure over handed-in steps): `rememberPlace({ paperId, noteId })` first — the phone row's own record: tab on show, scroll, page height, paragraph at the top of the view — then `onClose` (the window leaves the stack and unmounts as the page opens; the editor flushes its unsaved typing as it unmounts); the link's own navigation follows (the press does not `preventDefault`). The notes page's top-left then reads "← Back to paper" (P11-06) and the paper comes back on the tab and at the place the reader left.
2. **A press the browser keeps for itself** (Ctrl, Meta, Shift, Alt, or a non-primary button) records nothing and leaves the window standing: the paper has not been left. Where storage is blocked the press goes on and the notes page stays "← Saved", as for the phone row.
3. **Narrow card or a notice: the link is an icon** (added by me, not in the brief; flagged below). `pageLabelFits(cardWidth)`, `PAGE_LABEL_MIN_CARD_PX = 352`: from the margin's width (`useMarginRect()`, 360 where none stands), in a card narrower than 352 px — or while an export notice ("Copied") stands in the header — the link is a 32 × 32 icon (a page with a folded corner, `aria-hidden`) with the same `href`, the same accessible name and `title="Open as page"`. At 360 the words stay.
4. **Nothing else changes.** `page.tsx`, `paper-notes.tsx`, `notes-button.tsx`, the sticky, the margin, `return-to-paper.ts`, `back-link.tsx` are byte-identical; the phone's row, the upload-fallback band and Saved behave as before (their tests, `paper-notes.return.test.ts`, `back-link.test.tsx`, `return-to-paper.test.ts`, green and unedited). The window's header gains the link only; Export, the notice, the X, Escape, the stack and the editor are as they were.
