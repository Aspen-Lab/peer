## P3-01 — what was built

Pure core, `web/src/lib/papers/terms.ts` (browser-safe; no `node:` import):

- `paperDefinedTerms(doc, scope?)` and `paperDefinedTermsInReading(reading, scope?)` — one core (`definedTermsIn` over `TermSection[]`), two entrances: the `ExtractedDocument` (tests, any server caller) and the page's `PaperReading` (`body` + the map's pages), because the browser never holds the document (§1f.8 amendment). A test pins that the two agree on the same paper, with the page.
- The five patterns of blueprint §3.5 over each sentence of each rendered paragraph (`splitSentences` from `skim.ts`): `X (ABBR)`, `ABBR (X)`, `X, defined as …` (also "X is then defined as"), `X refers to …`, `we define X as …`. A hit is `{ term, definition, evidence, evidenceWhere, sectionId, page? }` with `definition === evidence` === the defining sentence, verbatim, ≤ 400 characters (= `REPORT_CAPS.evidenceChars`, pinned by a test); `evidenceWhere` is the section heading; `page` only when the source has one.
- Abbreviation logic **imported from `lib/scoring/term-expand.ts`, not copied**: a bracket is accepted when `expandTerm` says the abbreviation and the long form are one group entry ("lithium cobalt oxide (LiCoO2)", whose letters are not its initials) or when its letters are the initials of the long form's words (hyphenated words count as two, of/and/the may be skipped, a plural "s" after capitals is not a letter). `isGenericTerm` rejects generic words; a local stoplist rejects pronouns, determiners and the like; no term under 2 characters. `firstOccurrence` finds a term through the same `expandTerm` ("lithium cobalt oxide" is also "LCO").
- Scope (§1h.1): `scope.sectionIds` when given (the page passes `routeTermScope(route)`: every section some non-vague, non-gist question reads or marks `background`); otherwise the methods and results by `canonical`; otherwise the whole body. ≤ 8, de-duplicated by case and plural, the first occurrence in reading order wins.
- `mergeTerms(tier0, model)`: Tier 0 first, then the model's not already there, ≤ 8 in all; a model term with `evidence` is kept as Pass 2's verifier left it, one without carries `peer: true`.
- `firstOccurrence(sections, term)` → `{ sectionId, sectionIndex, paragraphIndex, offset, length }` (a superset of the brief's `{ sectionId, paragraphIndex, offset }`): whole-word, an acronym only as capitals, a plural by its singular, a hyphen by a space, never starting inside or spanning an inline formula (the span is masked).

UI:

- `components/reader/terms-strip.tsx`: `TermsStrip` — label-face heading through `Band` ("Terms to know", `TERMS.heading` in `copy.ts`), one line per term: the term in the reading face (a button when the body uses it, plain text when it does not), then the definition — a paper-defined (or verified) one as `EvidenceQuote` with its section link and page (`where` from `evidenceWhere`, else the map's heading by `sectionId`, else "abstract" — the answers block's own fallback), a Peer one as reading-face prose with `PEERS_READING`, never italic or `§`-attributed. ## Choices beyond the brief (small readings, consistent with the rulings)

1. **The page cannot hold an `ExtractedDocument`.** The brief says `paperDefinedTerms(doc, …)` computed on the page, but §1f.8's amendment says the browser never holds the document. Smallest reading: `paperDefinedTerms(doc, scope)` exists exactly as briefed, and `paperDefinedTermsInReading(reading, scope)` is the same core over `PaperReading.body` + `map` pages (a test pins that both give the same terms); the page calls the second. `firstOccurrence` likewise takes anything with `{ id, paragraphs }`, so it works on `reading.body` and on `readableSections(doc)`.
2. **An empty `scope.sectionIds`, or one naming no section of the paper, is no scope** (the default applies); and the page passes a scope only when the route has a non-vague, non-gist question that reads or marks `background` at least one section — otherwise methods + results (a gist or only-skim route is not "a route that marks read or background").
3. **"Cleared by the next click":** reading it as the next click on a term — a click on another term moves the mark, a click on the marked term clears it — plus Escape. A click elsewhere on the page does not clear it (that would clear it the moment the reader scrolls by clicking).
4. **Escape:** while a term is marked the strip takes Escape first (a capture listener on `document`, `stopPropagation`) so it does not also mean "Back to the briefing"; with nothing marked there is no listener and Escape is the page's again.
5. **A verified model term shows its evidence quote only** (the model's `definition` is Peer's paraphrase and is not shown beside the paper's own sentence — "a verified one quoting", §1h.1; say it once). A model term without `evidence` (or one whose `evidence` has no attribution to show) is Peer's prose, labelled.
6. **A term the body never uses is plain text, not a button** (nothing to point at). Terms are Tier 0 abbreviations as the abbreviation (`QPU`), phrases as written.
7. **Sentences with a formula (an inline `⟦…⟧` marker) or with an equation's operators (`= ∑ ∈ ∫ ≤ ≥ ≈ ∂ ∇`) are passed over**, since `EvidenceQuote` shows bare characters; found on `arxiv-2609.02113`, whose PDF text produced a "defined as" sentence full of equation debris. A sentence over 400 characters is passed over too.
8. **The pressed term in the strip also takes `--color-term-mark`**, so `globals.css.test.ts` now allows exactly two reads of the token (the body's `<mark>` and the strip's pressed button) and nothing else.
9. **The heading is `Band`**, as briefed, with `gap="none"` and `mt-8`; the map above it keeps its own eyebrow (not changed).
