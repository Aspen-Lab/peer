**2. - not in `read`, not in `rejected`, not in the tallies (`refused`), not in `refusedNumbers`, not in the corrections, and not one of the forty (so 45 edge splits then a real correction: the correction is kept, `rejected` 0);
- it uses up no place among a part's three splits and no repeat memory (it never reaches those checks);
- it is judged against the draft as given, like every other check (a split at the draft's own `to + 1` stays a no-op when a `move_start` moves the next part's start);
- a reply of nothing else is a review that agrees with the draft: `reviewStructure` returns `fellBack: false`, `cause: null`, `answer: { corrections: [], unsure }`, `read: 0`, `rejected: 0`, `parts` = the draft; it is the same value as for `{ corrections: [] }`. The route needs no change: a reviewed answer with 0 corrections already stores the entry, asks no look and logs the `review` line (`corrections: 0, rejected: 0`);
- still refused as `out-of-range`: a split at `to + 2` or beyond, before `from`, in a part that does not exist, and in the **last** part at its `to + 1` (no next part starts there: it is the paragraph after the paper's last);
- still `malformed`: a split at an edge with no role, a role outside the five, or a part or ordinal that is not a whole number.

Because every path reads a reply through `sanitizeReview` (the route's first pass, its second pass after the looks, and `reviewedParts` in the browser), both rules act on all of them; a review kept before this change is judged by them when laid on the map.
