## The rules as built

1. `partitionedMap(map, body, partition)` (reading-partition.ts): the trunk's row replaced in place by one row per part: `id` `<sectionId>~<n>`, `heading` `MAP.parts.labels[role]`, `canonical` the role, `role` `PART_ROLE`, `level` 1, no `parentId`, no `page`, `paragraphs` the trunk's lines for the range (real indices and openings), `words`/`minutes` recomputed from the body's paragraphs (180 wpm, as the map), `part` `{ sectionId, sectionIndex, role, from, to }` (read with `partOf`). Totals unchanged. The same map object for no partition, an unknown section, **and a partition the body does not bear out**: the section must be what `unheadedTrunk(body)` reads as the trunk (at the same index) and the parts must be >= 2, contiguous, from 0 to the last paragraph. That guard is mine beyond the brief: a stale or stray stored partition cannot change a headed paper's map.
2. The view (reading-map.tsx): meta part `MAP.parts.tag` ("Peer's reading") where a headed row reads its role; the legend line also while any group row is on screen; a group row's link `paragraphAnchor(sectionIndex, from)`, its lines' `paragraphAnchor(sectionIndex, line.index)`, a row after the trunk `sectionAnchor(real index)` (the map's row number is no longer the body's section number; `sectionIndexesOf`); gists under the trunk's `sectionId`; the open set is a `Set<string>` of row ids, carried when the map prop changes (`carryOpenRows`, exported: a group opens if its trunk was open; ids no longer in the map go) — done in render (`seenMap`), so a late partition keeps what was open; the summary counts the paper's own sections (`new Set(rows.map(trunk id or row id))`). `openRows` stays a prop of positions (tests), turned to ids at the start.
3. Without a key: today's map. **My call, said:** a map of exactly one row starts open by default (`openRows` absent), so a headless paper's map is its paragraph lines under their one row; a map of several rows starts closed as before; an explicit `openRows` is honoured. Never empty: pinned on B's invented fixture and on a headless `minimalPdf` (one row, 24-paragraph lines, no tag, and the overlay refuses a partition it does not fit).
4. `withPartitionMarks(route, map, body, partition)`: generic over `RouteResult` / `DisplayRoute`. Per question the trunk's entry is untouched (same object); group keys are added beside the real ids. A group's `paragraphs` / `topParagraphs` are the trunk's inside its range; `tier` = what its strongest paragraph wears under the trunk's mark (the trunk's tier if the group holds a top paragraph, `faint` if it only mentions, `none`; a background trunk gives `background` to groups with mentioning paragraphs, as the body paints them); `hits` and `evidence` from `routeByQuestions` over the group rows (counts over the group's paragraphs alone, a paragraph's sentence verbatim); at most `max(1, floor(N/2))` groups strong, the earlier first, the rest `medium`. The gist question: tier by role (`gistRoute` over the overlay). Vague entries and routes with no applicable partition are returned as the same objects. Ranking by score is moot: a section's top paragraphs tie by construction, so document order is the tie-break.
