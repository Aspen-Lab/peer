## 2. The rules as built

**Rule 1 — the notes page's square reaches a Summary block or the answers' block.** On `/notes/<id>` the square navigates to `/papers/<id>#summary-proposal` (or `#answers-block`, or a paragraph's id).
- `use-reader-tab.ts`: `summaryBlockFromHash` (an address naming a Summary block: `summary-` and lower-case words) and a small hook, `useArriveOnSummary`: the first time a body is on the page, if the address names a Summary block, the page's own switch is told once, so the paper opens on the **Summary**, not a pick (no scroll to the tab's start), read after the first commit (in a client-side navigation the address is changed after the render that mounts the page), never again (the reader's pick afterwards stands though the address still names the block). `show` is handed in as `useRestoreReaderPlace` is handed `select`: a direct `setState` in the effect is a lint error on this repo (`react-hooks/set-state-in-effect`). A paper with no body is on the Summary already: nothing changes.
- `sticky-note.tsx`: `blockFromHash` (a Summary block, or `answers-block`) and `followHash`, the arrival's wait extracted from the effect, unchanged for a paragraph (a look every 250 ms, forty looks, then `goToPassage` once), now for a paragraph, a Summary block or the answers' block. `goToPassage` was already right for both blocks (C P11-17b: the Summary host, no tab for the answers); the arrival was the gap. The answers' block stands under both tabs, so it brings no tab.
- A body paragraph's hash: the same wait, the same call: measured identical (§3.1).

**Rule 2 — a briefing paper's byline (BACKLOG-100).** On a window under 700 css px high (`@media (max-height: 699px)`, the one the zone's other short-window rules are for — **that is the height I chose, as the brief says**): the byline and the affiliation are each one line with an ellipsis; the control that opens the rest and its comma (`data-pane-more`) go (they would sit past the ellipsis); the whole of each stays in its `title=` and in the pane's details; the venue line, the title's three lines and 8 px gaps (C P11-17c), the commands, the progress and the cap are untouched. From 700 css px, and in one column (byte for byte, pinned), nothing changes.
- `title-block.tsx`: in the pane only, `data-pane-byline` + `title=` (every author) on the byline, `data-pane-more` around the comma and the control, `data-pane-affiliation` + `title=` on the affiliation.
- `decision-block.tsx` (details only): the details take optional `authors` and `affiliation` and draw them first (before the sentence), hidden (`data-pane-byline-full`); `globals.css` shows them on those windows only. **The details did not hold them (checked): they do now, but `page.tsx` has to hand them over — see §7.2.**
- `globals.css`: the byline's rules in the existing short-window block.
- **What this does not do:** it does not make the fixed zone fit a briefing paper on a short window. §3.2 and §7.1.

**Rule 3 — `PINNED_DEFAULTS_MIN_VIEWPORT_PX` retired.** Removed from `pane-layout.ts`; its tests retired with it (§5); `pane-layout.retired.test.ts` pins its absence beside `defaultsFit`'s, for both names, in the module and in every other file of `src/`.
