## The rule — B's P-full, `globals.css` only

Fit is a CSS `zoom: Z` on the paper's container. The masthead (48 px) is outside it; the article's `1rem` of top padding and `KeyLegend` (`--legend-h`, 28 px) are inside it, so they paint `16 x Z` and `28 x Z`. | rule | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `.reader-left` `top` | `calc(4rem / var(--page-zoom, 1))` | `calc(3rem / var(--page-zoom, 1) + 1rem)` |
