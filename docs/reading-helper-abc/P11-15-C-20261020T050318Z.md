## 0. In five lines

1. `figure-card.tsx`: `FigureCardView` (pure) and `FigureCard` (it owns `useFigureZoom` and stays mounted behind). The card is a `MarginCardShell` of kind `figure` and is non-modal. It has a 40-px header with an accent X at the top right, a zoom row (minus, readout, plus, Fit), the zoom core's surface, and the whole caption, which scrolls and is capped at 30 %.
2. `figure-host.tsx`: listens to the figure store. A press opens or raises the `figure` window, and a second figure replaces the first. Escape is a document listener in the bubble phase, gated on being the front card, and a modal dialog or a handled key wins over it. The X and Escape call `closeFigure()` and close the window. Unmount closes the figure. With no margin rect the host shows today's `FigureViewer` overlay in a portal, and a window that narrows closes an open card.
3. `page.tsx`: `<FigureHost key={`figure:${paper.id}`} />` right after `<StickyNotes …/>`, plus its import. The key is not the brief's bare `paper.id` (see "For the manager").
4. Measured on a harness at 1280 x 720 and 1440 x 1000, light and dark, below 1024, and at a page zoom of 1.1418. Every check passed and the four cells behave the same.
5. `windows.ts` and `margin-card.tsx` are not touched: the `figure` kind, its tall front and its 240 floor were already in C P11-14's contract.

## 5. For the manager

1. **Explain's Escape is not gated on the front card** (`explain-box.tsx`, P11-14's file; B P11-05 §2.4 and A4 ask for a one-line gate). Measured in all four cells: with a sticky in front, the figure and Explain behind, and the focus in the text, one Escape closes **Explain**, a card behind. The figure card's own gate holds, and it never closes from behind. When the figure is in front, Explain stays open, because the host's listener stands from mount, ahead of Explain's, and takes the key first. That ordering would change if the host mounted after an open Explain.
2. **The key** is `figure:${paper.id}`, not the brief's `paper.id`. It is still per paper (the host remounts with the paper and closes its figure); it differs only to avoid React's duplicate-key warning with the sibling `<StickyNotes key={paper.id}>`.
3. **On the real page the card waits for C P11-16.** Until the shell registers the margin, `useMarginRect()` is null there, so a press opens the overlay. Only the harness has shown the card in the margin.
4. **A stacked figure is shorter than B's numbers.** C P11-14's stack starts level with the first card's selection, not at the masthead. With a sticky and Explain as headers, the figure in front is 612 px tall at 1440 x 1000 and 332 px at 1280 x 720, against B's estimates of 832 and 552. Both are above the 240 floor, and the behaviour is P11-14's.
5. **The fit leaves about 17 px each side** at a 400-px margin (366.7 px of picture). The cause is the mat's 8-px padding (`FIGURE_MAT_PAD_PX`, this item's) plus the zoom core's own 0.96 (`clampFigureSize`, not this item's). B's A2 suggested the margin less 16. Lowering the padding to 0 would gain 16 px if wanted.
6. In the modal check the harness's fake dialog does not consume Escape, so the stand-in "back" counted that key. A real modal (the lightbox) takes it in the capture phase, as the below-1024 check shows (back 0).
