## Departures from the brief's wording (each deliberate, each small)

1. **`linesOfPage` keeps its one parameter; the prior-taking form is `linesOfPageWith(page, prior)`.** An existing test calls `pages.flatMap(linesOfPage)`, which hands the array index over as a second argument: a positional `prior` would have broken that unchanged test (the index taken for a gutter; and `tsc` rejects it besides).
2. **The run-in pattern matches the whole first run** (the prototype's form), not the brief's prefix form `/^(abstract|summary|key ?words|highlights)\b/i`, which would also cut "Summary statistics were …" at "Summary statistics" (tested: such a run is left in its line). "synopsis" is in as in the prototype. The face test compares the label with the longest run **of the rest** (the prototype compared with the longest run of the whole row, which is the label itself on a justified line made of short word-runs).
3. **Script offsets.** The brief reads "3.5 pt above to 7.5 pt below a row"; the prototype, and the physics (a superscript is 4.2–4.5 pt above its line), are 7.5 pt above to 3.5 pt below. Built as the prototype.
4. **Margin print is strictly "under 0.8"** as the brief says (the prototype used ≤); the outline JSON is byte-identical to the prototype's on all three multi-feature PDFs I compared.
5. **A wrapped heading re-evaluates "ends the argument"** on the joined heading (the prototype did not), so "Supplementary" + "Materials" over two lines ends the body.
6. **The 16-baseline floor of `gutterOf`** is the prototype's, not in the brief's list: a page of fewer than 16 baselines shows no gutter of its own and takes the prior.
7. **A fourth code-side commit** (`b8571792`): the mutation showed two of my own item-level tests passing without a gutter; hardened.
