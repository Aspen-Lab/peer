## The rules, and what was decided

**Rule 1: the left pane never steps at the page's foot (X-A1).** Cause, as A P11-21 traced it and now pinned: a sticky element stops where its holder ends, plus what its negative bottom margin lends it. The holder ends 4rem above the window's bottom at the page's end (the paper page's own bottom room, `md:pb-16` on its container in `papers/[id]/page.tsx`); the window-high pane, stuck at 4rem, would end the legend (1.75rem) and 1rem above the window's bottom, 44 px. A pane that may hang less than 20 px (1.25rem) below its holder is pushed up by the rest; P10-21's `-0.75rem` lent 12 px, so the whole pane stepped up 7.6 to 8.3 px. Fix: `margin-bottom: -4rem`, the whole of the room. The holder's end can then never stop the pane, at any window, tab or Fit zoom (the lengths are declared in the zoomed ancestor's local space and the room scales with it, as `top` and `max-height` do); the pane still ends 16 px above the legend (its own `max-height`) and never under the masthead; the peek and the rail are `position: fixed` with `margin-bottom: 0` and were never affected. Test: `left-pane.foot.test.ts` reads the pane's margin, `top`, `max-height`, the legend's height and the paper page's `md:pb-16` and checks overhang >= room - (legend + clearance) / zoom for zoom 0.75 to 2, and overhang <= room (red on `-0.75rem`: "needs 1.250rem of overhang, has 0.75rem").

**Rule 2: the map's meta never opens a line with a dot (X-A2).** Decided: the separator belongs to the part BEFORE it and is dropped at a break (not two fixed lines). Why: two fixed lines add a meta line to every row at every width, in the pane and the one column alike, where every row sits on one line from 360 px up (P10 round 6's map held every row on one line); the ruling keeps the default 320 and the one column unchanged. How: each part is followed by a 2ch slot (`inline-block w-[2ch] whitespace-pre`) holding ` ·` (the last part's slot is empty); the meta sits in a right-aligned box with `overflow-x: clip` whose lines inside are 2ch wider (`-mr-[2ch]`). A right-aligned line ends exactly at the box's clip, so the slot that would end a line falls in the clipped room and a separator between two parts of one line shows. The meta is monospace (`annotation`), so ` ·` is exactly 2ch. The row breaks in one place only, between two whitespace-nowrap runs, "role · page" and "words · minutes", so a narrow pane gets two even lines and not "6 min" alone. A reader or a copy gets the same words, separators and single spaces as before (`setup · p.1 · 1,234 words · 6 min`); each part's own span, text and `whitespace-nowrap` are unchanged (the slot is a sibling). The one column draws the same markup.

**Rule 3: the reading controls never claim a mode the reader does not see (§1h.121).** The sun means "system" (S16) and was named "Day reading mode" and drawn pressed on a dark page. Now its accessible name and tooltip (`title`) are "Follow the system (now dark)" / "(now light)", read live from `(prefers-color-scheme: dark)` with a `change` subscription (`useSyncExternalStore`; "Follow the system" alone where the query cannot be asked: a server render, the first hydrating render). Its icon is the owner's sun only while the system is light; while the system is dark it is a half-lit disc (the "system" mark, drawn in `reading-controls.tsx` beside the strip's other glyphs, 24 x 24, the same stroke). Why this and not the label alone: a label leaves a pressed sun on a dark page, which is the defect; why not a system mark always: the sun is the owner's (S15/S16, "A, A, sun, moon, fit") and is true whenever the page is day-lit, so it stays then. With the mode "dark" the moon stays pressed as it was; the sun still sets "system" and the moon "dark" (the accent is kept); with the mode "light" (only the Profile page sets it) neither is pressed.
