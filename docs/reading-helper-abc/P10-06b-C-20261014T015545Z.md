## The rules as built

1. **Terms meet in a paragraph** (`relevanceScores`, still exported, pure). A section that gives its `paragraphs` (counts and words of each, silent ones included) scores `0.5 x (best paragraph score) + 0.5 x (the section score as built)`. A paragraph scores the same formula over its own counts and words, with the paper's idf (df over the sections, as before), times `1 + 0.5 x (k - 1)` for `k >= 2` distinct terms it holds. A section that gives no paragraphs is scored exactly as before (the 23 P10-06 score tests are unchanged). The group row rule, the evidence sentence and the cache key are untouched. `topParagraphs` (the paragraph the map points at) now uses the same bonus, so the paragraph that carries the section's score is the one that gets the section's tier; its df stays over the section's own paragraphs. The mentions counts/hits are the section-level counts (see rule 2).
2. **A term's forms** (`termFormsMatch`, exported; the route's own matcher, `lib/scoring/term-expand.ts` is shared with the feed and untouched). A word has one suffix stripped (`tion`, `ing`, `es`, `ed`, `th`, `s`; never down to fewer than four letters); two words are forms of one when one stem is a prefix of the other and the longer is at most one letter longer: growing / growth / grown / grow, deposition / deposited / deposits, parameters / parameter, electrodes / electrode, phases / phase, annealing / annealed. The brief's wording ("a common prefix of >= 4 letters") would also join current / curriculum and grow / grower, so the rule is the tighter one above. Never for a term under four letters or one with a non-letter in it (`LCO`, `110`, `LiCoO2` match as they did: exactly and through the abbreviation table, so `LCO` still counts "lithium cobalt oxide" and `LiCoO2`; `LCO` does not match `LCOs`, `110` does not match `1100`). **The mentions line counts the forms under the reader's own term**: `growing x30` in 2.1 counts growth, grown and grow together; a word `termOccurrences` already counts (the term's plural, the abbreviation's long form) is never counted twice. The facts line's words are unchanged, and it stays a count a reader can check by the forms (the paper's text has them; the evidence sentence is one that holds one of them).
3. **The owner's question measured**: see the tables.
4. **The Plain control** (`plainOffered`): offered under a paragraph whose own tier is strong or medium, and under every paragraph of a strong or medium section with no paragraph marks (a Tier 2 answer's section). Faint, background, unmarked: not.
5. **The term scope** (`routeTermScope`): strong, medium or background (it compared `read`, which no route gives since P10-06). A paper-defined term in a medium section stays in scope. The scope was not removed (see "For the manager").
6. **The overlay cap**: the merged route (`mergeQuestionRoute`) and the overlay alone (`questionRouteOverlay`) wear the strongest tier on three rows at most per question (`MAX_STRONG_ROWS`, now exported from `reading-map.ts`): the model's verified answers first (in the order the model gives them), then Tier 0's strong rows in document order; beyond three a row is medium and keeps its hits, paragraphs and evidence. A section strong in both counts once; a `background` mark is not counted and not demoted; a merge within the cap is unchanged (no model data still returns Tier 0's own object).
7. **The one constant that moved**: `MEDIUM_SHARE` 0.3 -> 0.4 (the line between medium and faint). With rules 1 and 2 alone, Q1 had 3 strong, 6 medium, 2 faint, 1 none: on a paper about growth almost every row names growing, and the brief's own criterion (a third of the rows faint or none) failed (3 of 12). At 0.4 it is 5 of 12. This is the one place the paper decided a number; the alternative is a higher strong share or the rows' own rule, see below. Nothing else changed: the three tints and tokens, the facts line's words, the counts, the evidence sentence, the cache, the group rule, the gist route.
