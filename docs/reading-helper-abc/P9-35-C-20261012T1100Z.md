## 2. The rules, as built

1. **A ledger entry recorded from a job or an event is deleted when the ledger is cleaned — never kept with its origin dropped** (`cleanPreferenceLedger`, `ledger.ts`). It is deleted when its `origin` is `"event"` / `"job"`, when its key is in the `event|…` / `job|…` namespace, or when its record key is. The last two are for an entry that reached the ledger without the field: such an entry is a paper entry, and measured it lifts a paper by **0.10488** of a 0.18 maximum (B's 0.1049; reproduced by `ledger.opportunity.test.ts` on a hand-built prepared ledger). Every reader and writer cleans first (`prepareLedger`, every `apply*` / `remove*` / `setTermLean`, `summarizePreferenceLedger`, the server's `GET`/`PUT /api/profile`, the feed route, `mergePreferenceLedger`), so a device or a server row that still holds the entries is read, summarised, merged and written without them. The Supabase rows and columns are left (R3).
