## What I found first (on c0f97114)

- **Where "Open your notes" goes depends on the width.** `NotesButton` (`components/notes/notes-button.tsx`) is mounted twice by `page.tsx`. The **edge tab** (`variant="edge"`, `hidden md:flex`, from 768 px) opens the floating notes window (`NotesWindow`) and **navigates nowhere** (P10-09b). The **row** in the command stack (`variant="row"`, `md:hidden`, below 768 px) and the `PaperNotes` band on the upload-fallback page ("Open your notes →") **navigate** to `/notes/<id>`. All three go through `usePaperNotes().open` → `openPaperNotes` (`paper-notes.tsx`), which takes `openWindow` (the edge tab) or `push` (the others). So at 1440 and 1280 the real press does not reach the notes page; see "For the manager" 1.
- **The notes page's top-left button** is not in `app/notes/[id]/`. `page.tsx` there only renders `NoteEditor`; the button is `<Link href="/saved">← Saved</Link>` in `NoteEditor`'s page bar (`components/notes/note-editor.tsx`, line 1744 on the base). The page's own "← Saved" is the not-found state (a note not in this browser) and stays.
- **The paper's tab is React state**, `useReaderTab`'s `chosen` (default "digestion"); not in the URL, not stored. The strip's buttons are `role="tab"` with `aria-selected`, ids `reader-tab-summary` / `reader-tab-digestion`; a paper with no body has no strip and is the Summary.
- **The page scrolls in the window** (`document.scrollingElement`); measured: no scrollable container inside it. Body paragraphs have the ids `paper-section-<k>-p<n>`. At 1440 the invented paper's Digestion tab is 12,536 px tall; its Summary tab is 1,140 px (so "scroll about 3,000 px" is only possible on Digestion; on the Summary I scrolled to the end, 240 px).
- Next's `Link` navigation scrolls to the top and focuses the page's article first; the paper's `Reader` effects run about 400 ms later, so the restore has to be its own step after the reading has rendered.

## The rules

1. **"Back to paper" when the notes page was opened from a paper.** The navigating press records, in this tab's `sessionStorage` (one key, `peer-return-to-paper`; never a server, never a store; the module's own test refuses `localStorage`, `fetch`, a beacon or a zustand import): the paper's id, the note it opens, the tab on show, the scroll, the page's height, and the paragraph at the top of the view with its top. The notes page's button (`components/notes/back-link.tsx`, in place of the editor's fixed Saved link; first made in `app/notes/[id]/`, moved in `bd121949`) reads "Back to paper" and links to the paper when the record names **this** note; otherwise "Saved", as before (from Saved, a deep link, a reload with no record, a record for another note, storage blocked). Leaving the notes page by anything but that button (the header's Saved, another note, the browser's Back) drops the record (`leaseReturn`), so Saved's own entry to the same note, later, still says "Saved"; a reload keeps it. The edge tab's window press records nothing (it leaves the paper nowhere).
2. **Back on the paper, the place is restored once.** `useRestoreReaderPlace` (the page's one call): once the reading is on the page (`fromServer`), switch to the recorded tab (the page's own `selectTab`), then after a frame scroll to the recorded scroll, or, if the page's height changed by more than a line (16 px), onto the recorded paragraph at the height it stood; waiting up to 3 s for either to be possible. The record is cleared as it lands. If the reader scrolls or presses a key first, the page is theirs (and the record is cleared). The browser's own Back is untouched (it lands as it did: at the top).
   *Found by measuring, not asked:* after landing, the place drifted: 47 px 200 ms later at 1440 (the browser's scroll anchoring following the left pane as it re-lays itself) and 56 px in one column (content above the paragraph growing). So for 1.5 s after landing, scroll anchoring is off and a drift is put right at the next frame (`restorePlace`, `HOLD_MS`).
3. **Nothing else changes.** The notes page's content, Saved's own entry (measured: "Saved"), the not-found state, the window press, and Tier 0 are as they were; no network, no key, no new dependency.
