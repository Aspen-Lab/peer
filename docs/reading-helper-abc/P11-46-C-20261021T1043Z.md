## The rule as built (ruling §1h.148) and the means chosen

**While a pointer is held anywhere and the tab is not what it went down on, the tab takes no pointer.** In `notes-button.tsx`: `EDGE_TAB_PASSES_DRAG = "[:root:active_&:not(:active)]:pointer-events-none"`, in the edge tab's classes. The emitted rule (read off the dev server's stylesheet): `:root:active .\[\:root\:active_\&\:not\(\:active\)\]\:pointer-events-none:not(:active) { pointer-events: none; }`.

- `:active` matches the pressed element and every ancestor, so `:root:active` is true for any held pointer. A press on the tab's face, its label or its strip (a `::before`, whose state is its element's) makes the **tab** `:active`, so `&:not(:active)` is false and the rule does not apply: the tab is the pressed element and keeps the pointer. A press on the text, a card's control or anywhere else leaves the tab not `:active`: it ignores the pointer, as does its strip (`pointer-events` is inherited by the pseudo-element). The release lands on what lies beside the tab, and the browser's selection ends at the line's end as it would with no tab there.
- It applies to the **whole tab, face included**, not only the strip: a drag released 9 to 24 px past the end is on the face (the n = 9 cells above), and the rule is "a pointer that went down outside the tab passes through it".
- It applies to the edge tab in every state (with a card, with none, with no margin registered), one rule; the phone's `row` variant is untouched.
- The 24 px hit area **is kept**: the face (15 px) and the 9 px strip are exactly as P11-34 left them; the strip is simply not in the way of a drag that began elsewhere. Geometry, the 8 px clearance, the label, `aria-expanded`, `title`, the press and the keyboard focus are not touched.

**Why this means (of the three the brief names, and one more):**
- *A document listener with a flag* (set on `pointerdown` outside the tab, cleared on `pointerup`, cancel and blur): the same effect, but state of ours that can stay set (a lost `pointerup`, a context menu) and leave a tab that cannot be pressed; an effect to mount, a re-render or a DOM write at every press, a `document` guard for the server render. The browser clears `:active` itself on release, on cancel, on a context menu and when the window loses the pointer, so the CSS cannot stick. Not chosen.
- *`pointer-events: none` on the strip alone* (the brief's "catch only a pointer that goes down on it"): leaves the face, 9 to 24 px from the text, catching the drag (n = 9 above). Not chosen.
- *Dropping the strip* (the face's 15 px): under the 24 px floor of P11-34 rule 1; not needed, since the pass-through keeps the 24.
- *An inset of the cards' left edge* (`margin-card.tsx`): not this file's.
- A browser that does not give the root `:active` simply keeps the tab as it was before this change (the strip catches the drag): never worse.
