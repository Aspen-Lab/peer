### M1. Figure text and number-led body lines become headings

**What the 31 extras (P3 3, P4 13, P5 8, P6 7) are.** By the line that opens the row:

| shape | P3 | P4 | P5 | P6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a numbered line in figure text, set at 0.5–0.84 of the body (axis, legend, bar label) | 2 | 5 | 4 | 1 |
| a line of panel labels in another face at 0.96–1.6 of the body (a run of "letter + few words", or letter-ended) | 0 | 6 | 0 | 2 |
| a body-face, body-size line that goes on from the sentence above and opens with a number and a unit | 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| a caption's middle line in the body face at 0.84 of the body that opens with a number (the caption is cut there) | 0 | 0 | 3 | 0 |
| a reference-list entry set at 0.72 of the body, number first | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| a source-code line in a monospace face (`'key': value`) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

**The cause, in `candidateOf` (`pdf-outline.ts:1678–1712`).** A line that opens with a number and a capital word is a heading **whatever its size or face** (`:1695–1700`: no size test, no face test; only a street address or a reference entry is refused). A line that is not numbered is a heading when it is larger than the body or in another face at ≥ 0.95 of it (`:1702–1703`), and the only test on its words is "four letters in a row" (`:1708`). So an axis tick in a 5 pt face, a panel label row at 9.8 pt in the figure's face, and a prose line that happens to open with a number all reach `headingOf`.

**Why C P12-51's O8 (figure text is never a heading) misses them.** `figureTextOf` (`:2700–2750`) takes a run of **three or more consecutive "panel lines"** that stands **within 8 body sizes (about 66 pt) of a caption** (`PANEL_REACH` `:2684`, the test at `:2727`), and a panel line is at most **25 characters** (`PANEL_LINE_MAX` `:2680`, tested first at `:2690`) with no four-letter word unless it is smaller than the body. On the 31 lines: **13 are longer than 25 characters** (a 43-character axis line in 4 pt, a 61-character row of three panel labels, a reference entry, a code line); **5 carry a four-letter word at 0.96–1.6 of the body** (a panel title in the figure's face is a heading by size); **4 are panel-like but their run is shorter than three lines or lies far from the caption**; **9 are not figure text at all**: 6 body-face prose lines and 3 a caption's own middle lines (O8's reach cannot be the answer for them). Of the 26 that have a caption on their page, the nearest caption is **42–554 pt** away; only 2 are inside 66 pt, and neither is a panel line (45 and 49 characters). A Nature figure is a block several hundred points tall with its caption under it; the 66 pt reach is the caption's neighbourhood.

**Why C P12-49's O3 (a numbered heading continues the paper's numbering) misses them.** `numberedOut` (`:1761–1808`, called by `bodyStyle` at `:1182`) judges a number against the last number it accepted, and the numbering **starts empty**: `let last = []` (`:1773`), `numbersAfter(parts, [])` is true for any number (`:1721–1725`, the length test), and a number up to `NUMBER_REACH` = 3 ahead moves it (`:1728`, `:1800`). I traced the numbering on all six papers. P1, P2 and P7 walk 1, 2, 2.1, 2.2 … exactly. P3–P6 have **no numbered headings at all**, so every number that appears is figure text or a quantity, and the trace is a random walk: P4 `[]`→`[2]` (an axis title) →`[4]` (a legend) →`[1]` (a panel label set apart, which resets the numbering because "a set-apart heading that goes back is taken while the numbering is still the body-face lines' own", `:1795–1800`, and sets `set`, after which only a set-apart line moves it again); from then on a tick "1.0", a bar label "80", and the body lines "4 + unit" (p. 8) and "10.1 + unit" (p. 9) all "continue" `[1]` (each is after it and, being body-face or ≥ 3 ahead, leaves it where it is). A body-face line is only judged against the numbering, never against the sentence it stands in. Net: O3 refuses a number that goes **back**; it accepted **all 23 numbered extras**: in 8 the numbering was still empty (any number continues an empty numbering), in 15 it had been moved by earlier noise numbers (P4 [2], [4], [1]; P5 [2,8], [3,0], [4]; P6 [3], [6]).

**Three candidate rules.** Package A is four local shape rules; each is a few lines.

### M2. No Methods part

**Where the heading is lost.** Not in detection (`headingOf` reads it: 10.7 pt on P4, 12.0 pt on P5 and P6, another face), not by a wrap (the sub-heading is 2–3.5 pt smaller; `wrapsHeading` `:1347–1353` needs ≤ 0.6), not in the column or by furniture, **not by the end-matter cut** (`endsTheArgument` is false for Methods, and "Online content" is no trigger: `tail` stays false). It is lost in **`flush()` (`pdf-outline.ts:2804–2820`)**: a heading with no text under it is written only when it is **numbered** (`:2813`, `headingNumber(heading) !== null`); "Methods" is not, and its first content is another heading. Even if it were written, `bucketInheritor` (`html-text.ts:319–336`) gives a sub-heading its parent's bucket **only by number**, `dropStrayHeadings` (`:260`) and `readableSections` (`reading-map.ts:96–107`) drop an empty row unless the next one is numbered by it.

**Why the roles fall as they do** (`reading-roles.ts`). Each sub-heading names no role, so each is `body` and the role rules place it:
- P4: the sub-rows lie between the introduction and the first *named* method row (the last sub-heading, whose words name methods; that row is the `boundary`, `:158`), so A2 (`:195`) calls them **evidence**.
- P5 and P6: the sub-rows come after "Online content", a named apparatus row (END_MATTER_NOTE, `html-text.ts:177`), which is the `boundary`, so A2 does not cover them. A3 (`:188–193`) gives the first row after an apparatus row its role, but it reads `before = top[t-1]` **from the unpatched rows**, so the second sub-row sees a `body` neighbour and keeps `body`. Hence P5: first sub-row **apparatus** (wrong), four rows **body**; P6: the false reference-entry row takes apparatus and the Methods rows stay **body** (one of them results by M5).

**Three candidate rules.** All three are in `buildOutline`'s `flush()` and add a `group` state: set when an unnumbered heading with no text is a **whole-heading part name** (`/^(?:(?:online|extended)\s+)?(?:(?:materials?|experimental)\s+(?:and|&)\s+)?(?:methods?|methodology|experimental(?:\s+(?:section|procedures?|details?|methods?|part))?)$|^results(?:\s+(?:and|&)\s+discussions?)?$|^discussions?$|^conclusions?$|^introduction$/i`) and remembering its size; a later unnumbered heading **set smaller than it by more than 0.3 pt** takes the group's bucket (as numbered children do, unless its own bucket is strong: limitations, references, acknowledgments, supplementary, abstract); a heading as large, or a strong one, ends the group.

| | rule | result on P4 / P5 / P6 | tests moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** (recommended) | the group heading **is kept as a row** with no words (`level` 1) and its sub-headings are level 2 (`level` is a new optional field on `OutlineSection` and `ExtractedSection`, carried by `normalize` `pdf-text.ts:218`, kept by `dropStrayHeadings` and `readableSections`, read by `readingMapOf` `:285`) | a Methods group row (role method, 0 words) with 7 / 5 / 6 children at level 2, all method; P6's sample-preparation row goes results→method (M5) | **0** beyond M1's |
| **B** | as A, **no row**: the sub-headings inherit the bucket and stay level-1 siblings | same roles; "Methods" is not visible in the map; the first sub-row starts the part | **0** |
| **C** loose trigger | the group is any empty unnumbered heading whose `canonicalizeHeading` is an argument part (what `tailPartOf` uses for the end matter) | **breaks P6**: a descriptive heading with the word "model" is bucketed methods (`html-text.ts:214`), is empty, and its four sub-sections are relabelled method; rejected | 0 |

Extensions I measured and do **not** recommend now: (a) a group for *any* empty heading followed by smaller ones (gives P6's empty descriptive heading a row with its four children, canonical forced `body`): **+2 tests** (`pdf-outline.sections.test.ts:545`, `:554`); (b) a group also for a part heading **with** text of its own: **+4 tests** (`pdf-outline.sections.test.ts:227`, `:282`, `:529`, `:545`).

Dependency: **M2 needs M1 on P4**. With M2 alone, the false numbered row inside the Methods text (a body line "10.1 + unit") resets the group and three sub-rows lose the bucket.

**Recommendation: A.** B is the fallback if the C wants the smaller diff (one file); the scorer reads both alike (§1h.436 (d): a row with no paragraphs starts where its first sub-row does).
