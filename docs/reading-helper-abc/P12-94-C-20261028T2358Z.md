## 2. The rules as built

**Rule 1 — `confident-draft` (`judgeCorrections`, `sanitizeReview`, `REFUSAL_REASONS`).**
- A correction whose part has confidence `high` or `medium` is refused as `confident-draft`: a `move_start` of the part, a `set_role` of it, a `split` in it. It is the **last gate**: judged against the draft as given, after every other check, so a correction another check refuses keeps that reason (an out-of-range move of a sure part is `out-of-range`, a `set_role` of a sure part to its own role `same-role`, to `setup` after the opening `setup-after-opening`), and one that passes them all is refused here in place of being kept. The reason is the ninth in `REFUSAL_REASONS`, before `over-cap`.
- A refused-for-confidence correction takes its place in the judging as a kept one did: it is one of the forty, it uses the part's repeat memory and splits, and for the pieces check its move or split still counts (it stays in the judging list flagged `sure` and is dropped from the answer at the end). This is what makes the lower bound hold (rule 3); the cost is that "40 corrections against sure parts, then one against a low part" refuses the 41st as `over-cap`, as before.
- A reply of nothing else is `reviewStructure`'s `all-refused` (fallback `unusable`, the draft standing, `read` and `rejected` equal, `refusedByReason: { "confident-draft": n }`), as §1h.481 rules.
- An `unsure` listing of a sure part draws no look and a look changes no such part (`secondLookTargets`, `resolveLooks`).
- The answer names the refused corrections in an optional `Answer.contradicted` (set only when there are any); nothing is laid over the map for them and the stored entry (`corrections`, `unsure`, `secondLooks`) is unchanged.

**Rule 2 — `contradicts-low` (`LookWhy`, `secondLookTargets`, `resolveLooks`, `restoreReview`).**
- A part the answer corrects that the rules were `low` about draws a look of the new kind `contradicts-low`, ranked after `contradicts-high`, `contradicts-medium` and `unsure`. One look per part, whatever the number of its corrections. The cap is three, unchanged.
- Its corrections stand only on a `contradicts-low` look that says `review`. On `draft`, on a failed or unreadable look, or when no look happened (cap reached) they go (`resolveLooks` ends with a filter on the corrections against `low` parts). The `set_role` a look adds for an `unsure` part is the look's own and stands.
- An `unsure` listing of a `low` part that the answer does not correct keeps its behaviour. A `low` part both corrected and listed `unsure` draws the one look its correction needs (`contradicts-low`); it adds no role (see "For the manager" 5).
- `restoreReview` reads a stored entry with `why: "contradicts-low"` (and still the three older kinds).

**Rule 3 — the lower bound (the differential test), and two closures the test forced.** See 3 and "For the manager" 4. In short: the corrections a reply ends with are a subset of those it ended with before, for every draft, reply and look verdict. The sanitizer's corrections are exactly the old ones without those against sure parts; the looks of the unsure parts are a subset of the old ones; a role added by a look needs room among the forty. `Answer.contradicted` carries the places the refused corrections took.

With the sure corrections out of the list and the unconfirmed low corrections dropped, there was room where the old pipeline had none. A look now adds a role only if the answer's corrections, the refused ones and the three looks come to forty at most. Cost: no role is added by a look for a reply of 38 or more corrections, which cannot happen on the answer budget of 600 tokens in practice.
