## Mechanisms (file:line at the head)

**M1 — label-only caption line outside the picture's width (P1, all 6; the whole P1 failure; a regression from `19dd2cab`).** In P1 the caption's label ("Fig. N", x 42.5 to 62) is its own MuPDF text line, apart from the first body line of the caption (x 68 to 553) in the same MuPDF block, and sorts after it (line tops 181.4 against 180.3). `readPageStructure` makes one `TextBlock` per line (`pdf-engine.ts:216-226`, sort at `:245`). `captionDistance` returns null when the line has no horizontal overlap with the region (`:253-254` via `horizontalPenalty`, `:266-270`); the label line sits left of the raster (x 127 to 468), so it is not a typed candidate. `bestCaptionForRegion` falls to the generic nearest line (`:301-324`, return at `:322`): the label-less 137-char first line. The raster is kept anyway (`candidateRegions:544-550` needs no explicit caption), but the route filter then compares `captionKey(candidate.caption)` with the wanted number (`readPage:808`, `captionKey:674-677`), finds none and answers 404 (`route.ts:118-121`). Before `19dd2cab` a `TextBlock` was the whole MuPDF block (rect and joined lines), so the label shared the block's extent and the caption began "Fig. N". P12-30's report records why `19dd2cab` went to lines (two column captions share one MuPDF block): the fix must not undo that.

**M2 — stacked rasters, each judged alone against the size floors (P3 Fig. 2).** `candidateRegions` takes every image block separately (`:544-550`) and applies `MIN_WIDTH/HEIGHT/AREA` = 120/120/20,000 (`:112-114`). The a-c strip is 76.8 pt tall: dropped. Nothing joins it to the d-o raster 3.9 pt below. Text (the a-c headers and legend) is never part of any region.

**M3 — a drawing's region is the union of path boxes only (P3 Figs. 3, 4).** `pathBox` (`:373-414`), `collectDrawingBoxes` (`:418-482`) and `clusterDrawings` (`:488-527`, tolerance 3 pt at `:125`) see paths. Axis titles, tick labels past the frame, panel letters and sub-floor rasters (icon strips) are text and small images, outside the cluster; `renderRegion` adds 10 pt (`:615-620`), which cannot reach 15 to 70 pt of missing figure.

**M4 — side-by-side panel groups split into clusters; the narrow one fails the width floor (P3 Fig. 5).** Panels a-d and e-f are two clusters 36 pt apart (`clusterDrawings`); e-f is 103 pt wide, below `MIN_WIDTH` (`:555`), so it is never a candidate, and nothing re-attaches it to the captioned a-d cluster although one full-width caption serves both.

**M5 — fixed 10 pt padding crosses into the caption (P1 and P2, 12 images).** `renderRegion:615-620` pads every side by 10 pt; the caption's first line sits 3.3 to 9.4 pt below the picture (gap measured per figure), so the top of that line prints at the picture's lower edge. Cosmetic; P3's captions are 10 pt or farther away.

**M6 — a body line that begins with a figure reference is read as a caption (P3 "Fig 8"; EXTRA).** Outside the engine: `CAPTION` in `web/src/lib/papers/pdf-outline.ts:178` takes any line that starts "Fig. 8)"; `captionOf` (`:1035-1046`) and the loop at `:1805-1823` then collect the caption lines. `placeFigures` (`reading.ts:716-718`) offers it a route URL; the route 404s; the pool's word-overlap fallback (`extract.ts:1454-1467`) returns Fig. 4's picture.

**M7 — caption truncated at a face change (P3 Figs. 1 and 6, the margin caption).** Outside the engine: `continuesCaption` stops when the next line's font differs from the previous line's (`pdf-outline.ts:255`). A bold title line followed by regular lines is cut to its first line (137 and 65 characters). The engine's own captions for these two are fine (500-cap).

**M8 — engine caption length (pool path only).** `mergeCaptionLines` (`pdf-engine.ts:274-292`, cap at `:118`/`:291`) joins following lines until 500 characters: it cuts five P2 captions and every P3 caption, and over-joins P2 Figure 3 by 74 characters (the known close-body over-join; the test at `pdf-engine.caption-pairing.test.ts:138`). The margin does not show it.

## Candidate rules, with cost, and the choice

"Measured" below means a scratch prototype, never in the repository: a copy of the engine with the rule behind a flag, run on the three papers (route calls, per-figure boxes, images read) and against copies of the three existing engine test files (63 tests: `pdf-engine.test.ts`, `pdf-engine.caption-pairing.test.ts`, `pdf-engine.work-budget.test.ts`; the parity test is skipped here). The combined prototype (R1a + R2a + R3a + R5a) returned P1 6 of 6 and fixed P3 Figs. 2 to 5 with P2 unchanged, and all 63 tests stayed green. These are findings for deciding, not the fix.

### M1 (P1: all six margin images)
- **R1a (chosen): narrow row-merge in `readPageStructure`.** A line that is only a label ("Fig. N", "Figure N", optional `.`/`:`/`|`) is merged with the same-block line on the same row that starts within 12 pt to its right (union rect, "label body" text). Cost, measured: P1 6 of 6 found, boxes identical to the whole-document call; P2/P3 identical; 63 of 63 tests green. The un-narrowed form (merge every same-row pair in a MuPDF block) failed 2 existing tests ("preserves two columns with distinct captions, including a raster" and "keeps column labels and continuation when another column interrupts the sorted lines") because the invented two-column fixtures put both column captions on one baseline in one block: the label-only restriction is required. Also fixes the pool caption (starts "Fig. N", full first line). Needs `FIGURE_EXTRACTION_REVISION` (`figure-revision.ts:4`) bumped, since pictures and sizes derive from it.
- R1b: let a typed line borrow its block's horizontal extent in `captionDistance`. Cost: the label line still sorts after the first body line (180.3 against 181.4), so `mergeCaptionLines` (`:274-292`, reads only later lines) would drop the caption's first line; the same-block two-column fixtures would borrow each other's extent. Not measured; needs R1a's merge anyway.
- R1c: accept an unlabelled candidate in the route when it is the page's only eligible region and no typed caption on the page names another figure (`readPage:808`). Cost: smallest code; fixes the P1 margin, not the pool's label-less captions; risks a wrong picture when the reading's page is off and contradicts the "honestly absent" stance of the P12-28 tests (`pdf-engine.caption-pairing.test.ts:53-71`). Not measured.

### M2 and M4 (P3 Figs. 2 and 5: missing panels)
- **R2a (chosen): "satellites".** After the floors and the caption pairing, grow each accepted, captioned region by sub-floor items near it: sub-floor rasters adjacent (gap at most 8 pt, at least half overlap on the other axis), and sub-floor vector clusters that are at least half the floors (60 x 60 pt, 10,000 pt^2), have the same typed caption line, and share half the region's vertical extent. The floors stay 120/120/20,000, and satellites never create a candidate, so "cross-column Table 3 cannot acquire Figure 5 when the actual diagram is below the unchanged floors" stays true. Cost, measured: P3 Fig. 2 gains the a-c strip (top y 153 to 72); Fig. 5 gains panels e, f (right edge 333 to 482); Figs. 3 and 4 gain their icon strips (top y 100 to 56 and 103 to 52); P1 and P2 unchanged; 63 of 63 tests green. The un-guarded form (any sub-floor item, adjacent or same caption) failed the same 2 tests as R1a's naive form, because the invented panel fixture draws 44 x 44 pt marks beside the raster, which were absorbed; the half-floor guard removes that. Open risk, unmeasured: a real page where a sub-floor decoration or a neighbour's small panel sits within 8 pt.
- R2b: relax the floors for a region in the same band as a captioned region. Cost: contradicts the pinned floors (`pdf-engine.ts` header "size floors stay") and the Table 3/Figure 5 test; creates stand-alone candidates (EXTRA risk). Not measured.
- R2c: region from layout: grow from the caption up and out to the white space bounded by long body-text lines. Most general (would also fix M3), but it replaces the "region is the painted extent" contract that the box assertions pin (`pdf-engine.test.ts:37-54`, "a box on the drawn extent") and the work budget; needs its own fixtures. Not measured.

### M3 (P3 Figs. 3 and 4: clipped text and icons)
- **R3a (chosen): text growth.** Iteratively absorb short figure-text lines (at most 60 characters, not typed captions, not the caption's block, above the caption) within 6 pt of the region. Cost, measured: P3 Fig. 3 becomes `[113, 70, 482, 348]` (axis titles in, "x-axis title" now visible), Fig. 4 bottom 609 to 625 (both axis titles in), Fig. 1 grows to `[99, 56, 497, 442]` (its left label and bottom label, correct); P1 and P2 unchanged; 63 of 63 tests green. Limits: 6 pt did not reach P3 Fig. 2's rotated side labels (6.6 pt gap: 8 to 10 pt would); the 60-character test only separates figure text from body text in this paper (its body lines are 63 to 72 characters), so a font-size comparison (figure text 6.5 pt, body about 9 to 10 pt) is safer: a design choice for the manager. Risk: body text within the tolerance of a figure.
- R3b: larger padding for drawing regions (for example 25 pt), clamped at the caption. No new logic; crops body text and page furniture (P3's journal header bar ends at y 50, 2 to 5 pt above the icon strips), and the icons of Fig. 4 are 50 pt away. Not measured.
- R3c: feed text-line and sub-floor-image rectangles into `boxes` before `clusterDrawings`, tolerance 3 to 6 pt. Same result as R3a but changes what the 12,000-rectangle ceiling (`:107`) and the work-budget tests count, and can chain body text into a cluster through 6 pt gaps. Not measured.

### M5 (caption sliver, P1 and P2)
- **R5a (chosen): clamp the padded clip at the caption's near edge** (minus 0.5 pt) when the caption's first line is within the padding. Cost, measured: 63 of 63 tests green (the "box plus 10 pt" case at `pdf-engine.test.ts:56-63` has no caption within 10 pt); P1 and P2 pictures lose the sliver at the cost of at most 6.7 pt (about 19 px) of height; boxes unchanged. Same cache bump as R1a.
- R5b: padding 10 to 4 pt everywhere. Changes every crop, breaks the 812 x 504 px assertion (`pdf-engine.test.ts:56-63`) and the PyMuPDF parity. Not measured.
- R5c: leave it (cosmetic). Cost: 12 of 12 P1/P2 pictures keep a cut line at their lower edge.

### M6 (ghost "Fig 8" caption, outside the engine; EXTRA)
- **R6a (chosen): a separator after the label.** In `CAPTION` (`pdf-outline.ts:178`), require `.` `:` `|` a dash, or whitespace then a capital, after the number (and optional panel letter); refuse `)` `,` `;`. Cost: the other papers' real captions all pass (their labels are followed by `.`, `|` or a space); "Figure 1)" style captions would be lost; the tests of `pdf-outline` (not run here) must be checked.
- R6b: refuse a "caption" whose previous line in the same column ends mid-sentence in the body face. Cost: captions that follow axis text with no stop; needs the face information already used at `:255`.
- R6c: at reading-build time drop figure entries whose engine measure fails and whose text is body-like (long, no label separator): `withFigureSizes` already asks the engine per caption (`pdf-text.ts:243-277`). Cost: engine failure then hides real figures (P1 would vanish before R1a).

### M7 (margin caption truncated to a first line, outside the engine)
- **R7a (chosen): allow one face change after the caption's first line** in `continuesCaption` (`pdf-outline.ts:244-266`, test at `:255`): the line after a title-face line may be in a regular face if the size and pitch match. Cost: the prose under a figure in a mixed-face paper (the reason for the face test, `:239-243`) needs a pitch/size guard; `pdf-outline` tests not run here.
- R7b: drop the face test and rely on size and pitch. Cost: reintroduces the case the face test was added for.
- R7c: for a PDF, take the caption's lines from the engine's typed caption (it has the block). Cost: couples reading to the engine and to the 500 cap.

### M8 (engine caption cap and over-join, pool path)
- **R8a (chosen): stop at a pitch jump.** End a caption at the first line whose gap above exceeds about 1.4 times the caption's own median line pitch (it ends P2 Figure 3 at its true end), and raise the cap from 500 to a longer one only with that stop. Cost: the "close-body over-join" test (`pdf-engine.caption-pairing.test.ts:138-143`) records today's behaviour on purpose and would be re-justified; the cap is shared with the pool's `data:` and report budgets.
- R8b: keep 500, mark a cut caption with an ellipsis in the pool (the margin already does this, `matted-figure.tsx:figureCaption`). Cost: no fix for over-join.
- R8c: ignore the engine caption when the outline has one for the same page and number. Cost: two sources of truth.

## What the manager must decide
