## 2. The rule as built

`withPageCase(pages)` runs on the page text straight after `readPages`, ahead of `withPageLigatures` (`pdf-text.ts`, both `tryExtractPdfText` and `extractPdfTextFromBytes`). Pure, deterministic, no dependency, linear in the number of items (items are compared with their stream neighbours only).

- **Touching.** Two items touch when they are in the same font, on one baseline (within 0.1 of the type size), and the gap from the first's right edge to the second's left edge is at most `max(1 pt, 0.18 x the second's size)` (the outline's own "no word space" measure in `lineOf`) and at least -0.25 x the size (a kerning overlap; B needed -1.5 pt at 8 pt). An item with no width never touches.
- **Evidence (a hit).** A run of one or two ASCII lowercase letters, alone in its item, that either (a) touches a following item that begins with a capital and has no lowercase-ending item touching it on the left (`s`+`E`, `l`+`CO`, `l`+`YC`, `ss`+`Bs`), or (b) touches a preceding item that ends in two capitals and has no lowercase-led item touching it on the right (`DF`+`t`). The font and the letters are noted.
- **A font qualifies** with at least 3 hits over at least 2 different letters, counted after dropping every letter the font also prints as a capital anywhere in the paper (see 8, item 1: this is one condition beyond the brief). A font that does not qualify is not touched.
- **Repair.** In a qualifying font, the hit shapes made only of its noted letters read as capitals (`s`+`E` to `S`+`E`, `DF`+`t` to `DF`+`T`, `ss`+`Bs` to `SS`+`Bs`). One further shape is repaired but never counted as evidence: the first letter of a word that stands alone in its item (one letter, a noted one, nothing touching it on the left that ends in a letter or digit) before a touching item that begins in lowercase: `l`+`i-` to `L`+`i-` (see 8, item 2: the brief lists this shape among the positives).
- Every other item comes back as the same object; a page with no change is the same page; a paper with nothing learned gets the same array. Repaired items are new objects with the geometry they had and upper-case text.

## 8. For the manager

1. **One condition beyond the brief: the capital-printing guard.** A font counts a noted letter only if the font never prints that letter as a capital anywhere in the paper. Reason: the brief's six negatives (`pH`, `eV`, `mM`, `kDa`, `iPhone`, `mAh` as split items) have exactly the shape of a hit, and a body font that sets them split, 3 times over 2 letters, would qualify under the brief's rule alone and be capitalised wholesale (`pH` to `PH`). A font whose capital P reads as `p` cannot also print a capital P, so the guard costs a damaged font nothing (B's Semibold prints no S, L, T, I, U) and protects every font that does. Mutation: without it, 2 tests red. Risk: a damaged font that does print one stray capital of a noted letter loses that letter (never the other way round); if the manager's P3 check shows a letter missing for this reason, the guard is the place (`learnCaseFonts`, the `counted` filter).
2. **One shape beyond the brief's literal wording: the word-start.** The brief lists `l`+`i-` among the positives, but `i-` is not capital-led, so "touches a capital-led run" alone would not repair it. B's stage 2 repaired it (a noted letter standing alone). Built as: one letter, noted, alone in its item, nothing ending in a letter or digit touching it on the left, a lowercase-led item touching it on the right. Not counted as evidence. Mutation: removing it turns 3 tests red. Narrower than B's stage 2 (which took any short run of noted letters, touching or not).
3. **The after-capitals shape needs two capitals before it** (`DF`+`t`, as B's prototype), not one: with one (`T`+`o`, `W`+`e`) a drop-cap-like split in an ordinary font would be read as a hit. The brief says "touches a run beginning with a capital"; this is narrower for that one direction only. Mutation: 1 red.
4. **Letters per run: 2, as the brief says** (`MAX_RUN_LETTERS`). B's prototype took 1 to 3 in stage 1. If the P3 check shows Fig. 4's first letter (`i` + a lowercase continuation) left damaged, the likely cause is that `i` was never a hit letter in that font (word-start only repairs a noted letter); raising the constant to 3 is the one-token knob, and the only way I see to note `i` from the evidence B described.
5. Evidence is keyed by pdf.js's `fontName` (one per font object per document). A producer that embeds the same face again on each page would give each page's copy its own evidence.
6. The test fixture's overlap (1.7 pt at 8 pt) is what makes pdf.js start a new item there (it splits at 0.2 em); the rule's own tolerance is 0.25 em.
