## 2. The defect, and the three places that held it

A selection over 1,200 characters in one block was no target, so the margin offered nothing, not Highlight, not Take notes. At the base, a triple click on each of the twelve body paragraphs of the invented paper (1440 × 900, light, stub key): **4 offered the three buttons (850, 1,166, 1,065 and 946 characters), 8 offered nothing** (1,352 to 3,410) — the owner's 10 of 13 in miniature.

1. `paper-body.tsx` `selectionTarget`: `passage.length > MAX_SELECTION_CHARS` (1,200) → null. The one gate for all three actions.
2. `lib/notes/blocks.ts` `HIGHLIGHT_QUOTE_MAX` = 1,200, read by `store/notes.ts` `addHighlight` (`quote.length > HIGHLIGHT_QUOTE_MAX` → null): behind the first gate, a highlight of more than 1,200 would still have been refused.
3. `lib/papers/explain.ts` `EXPLAIN_CAPS.passageChars` = 1,200: the route's passage cap (`clipPassage`), where the other two came from. **Unchanged.**

What did not hold it: the mark (`anchorRange` has no length limit), the note row (clamps to two lines by CSS only), the three exports (`highlightWords` has no cap), the reload (the stored range is checked, not searched), the sticky's anchor (`locatePassage` finds the whole passage; its `quote` is the start, 300). Measured, not assumed (§8.2).

## 3. The rules as built

**Rule 1 — Highlight and Take notes take any length inside one block.**
- `selectionTarget` has no length limit. `MAX_SELECTION_CHARS` stays, **1,200**, and is now Explain's cap (the request clips at it). It is also still the limit of one thing: a Summary or answer selection across **two blocks** of the one container (`oneBlock: false`). The Summary and the answers are each *one* `data-explain-source` container, so a selection from one Summary block into the next is "one place" to `samePlace` and has always been a target up to the cap (a P10-08 pin: "across two blocks of the Summary is one container's worth"). With the cap gone it would have become a target at any length, so a selection over 1,200 across two blocks of the container is none (D5), and one up to 1,200 across two is as it was (rule 3). A selection across two paragraphs is none by `samePlace`, as before.
- A triple click on a Summary block is ended by the browser at the start of the next block; `readSelection` already moved the end back for the body, but not for a container (one place), so a long triple click on a Summary block would have read as "across two blocks". `endsAtNextStart` (the existing test: end offset 0, no break inside the words) is now also what says the two ends are in one block. Found by a red test of my own.
- `HIGHLIGHT_QUOTE_MAX` is deleted; `addHighlight` keeps the whole passage, whitespace collapsed, found whole in its block. The block is the bound.
- A sticky is anchored over all the words (as it always was) with its `quote` the passage's start, at most 300 (`STICKY_QUOTE_MAX`, unchanged — see §9.1).
- `selection-toolbar.tsx`: a comment. What the group offers never read the length; with option (a) nothing in it changes.

**Rule 2 — Explain this on a long selection: option (a), offered, clipped.**
- `explain-box.tsx`: `EXPLAIN_PASSAGE_CHARS` = 1,200 and `explainPassage` (collapse, trim, cut at a word, an unbroken run at the cap) — the browser's copy of the route's `clipPassage`, pinned equal for eleven kinds of input. `requestExplanation` posts a passage over the cap clipped; a passage within it goes **exactly as given** (rule 3; a raw "  a   b\n" is not collapsed).
- The card's quote is `previewOf(passage)`, the first 80 characters and an ellipsis, as for any passage over 80 characters; for the clipped passage it is the same words. So "the card's quote showing the clipped words with …" is what it already did; a test pins it. The kept answer, the thread and the window's key stay the whole passage's (`explanationFor`, `passageHash` in `page.tsx`, which is not mine), so only the first request is clipped; a reply's request carries the whole passage and the route clips it to the same words (§9.4).
- **Why (a).** The route clips first (`clipPassage`), then locates the clipped passage in its paragraph, then prompts with the paragraph as context. Measured from `buildExplainPrompt` on invented paragraphs (lengths only; `explain-box.long.test.tsx` pins the shape):

| 1,187 (within the cap) | 1,187 | 1,533 (whole) | yes | 5,596 | 6 |

  The parts, schema, rules and system prompt are those of a short passage's, byte for byte (pinned). So the first 1,200 characters are explained with the paragraph around them, and the answer is of the same kind. In the browser the stub answered all of them (status 200, the card's quote ends with an ellipsis, an answer shown). **(b) would have taken a feature from the reader for a limit the route already handles.**

**Rule 3 — short selections are unchanged.** A selection up to 1,200 characters reads (`selectionTarget`, `readSelection`), highlights, anchors, posts and explains as it did: pinned in the tests, and identical before and after in the browser (§8.1).

## 9. Unsettled

1. **"A sticky's quote keeps them too."** A sticky's `quote` has always been the passage's *start*, at most 300 characters (`STICKY_QUOTE_MAX`; the header shows 80), and its `start`/`end` cover all the words — that is what "keeps them" is here: the mark covers 2,974 characters, a reload keeps it. I did not make the quote hold all the words: that would change every sticky over 300 characters (rule 3). Say if you meant the field. Two consequences of the field being the start: a sticky is *repaired* (the paper re-read, a paragraph's id moved) by its quote alone, so its mark would shrink to those 300 characters — true already for any sticky over 300, more visible now; a highlight's quote is whole, so its repair keeps all its words (pinned). And a sticky on a Summary block or an answer has **no range at all** at any length (`useTakeNote` knows only the body's paragraphs), so it has no mark, with a 300-character quote: unchanged.
2. **A short selection across two Summary blocks** (579 characters, within the cap) is offered all three buttons, as before (a P10-08 pin), and **Highlight makes nothing and leaves the selection** (checked in the browser: stored 0): the passage is not found whole in the first block. D5 says "across two blocks offers nothing"; I kept rule 3 and only stopped a *long* one from being offered. Whether the short one should offer nothing is the manager's call.
3. **No Summary block or answer is over 1,200 characters with the stub**: the model's text is capped at 600 (a Summary block) and 360 (an answer) by `REPORT_CAPS`; the abstract (1,193, then 1,238 with a second paragraph) is the longest block, and the longest answers' block I could draw was 837. The long Summary and answer paths are unit-tested (`paper-body.selection.long.test.tsx`, `highlight.long.test.tsx`) and the abstract block was measured in the browser; a real run with five answered questions may pass 1,200.
4. **A reply's request** (`requestReply`, in `explain-thread.ts`, not named in the brief) carries the whole passage and the route clips it to the same words (`clipPassage`, before it locates and prompts, so the prompt and the memory key are those of the first request). One line there would clip it in the browser too; I did not touch the file.
5. **The Explain memory keeps the whole passage**, up to a block's length (the store's comment says "clipped"): ≤ 32 passages a paper, 3,000 characters or so each, in the reader's browser. Clipping it would need `page.tsx`'s lookup (`explanationFor(…, explainTarget.passage)`) to clip too, or a kept answer would not be found.
6. **The card does not say it explained the first 1,200 characters.** The quote is the opening words with an ellipsis, as for any passage; a line such as "the first 1,200 characters" would be new copy (`copy.ts` is not mine).
7. **A late selection in a long paragraph** (starting more than about 800 characters into a paragraph over 2,000) can reach past the route's 2,000-character context cut: the passage is in the prompt whole, its tail is not in the context. The route's existing behaviour for any passage; its cap is unchanged.
8. **The base probe needed a retry and the after probe a click spot clear of the sticky masthead** (a harness artefact, not the product): my first triple click on the 3,410-character paragraph landed under the sticky header after Playwright scrolled the paragraph's top to the window's top.
