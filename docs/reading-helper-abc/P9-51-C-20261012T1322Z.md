## 2. The rules, as built

**Rule 1 (F08): each row says its words.** A row's meta is one list of parts — role (when Peer placed the heading), page (a PDF's), **words**, minutes (none when the section has no words, as before) — from one function, `metaParts(row, role)`. A row reads `setup · p.1 · 569 words · 4 min`, `p.2 · 158 words · 1 min` (no role), `90 words · 1 min` (no role, no page). The words are `row.words`, the count `row.minutes` is computed from (`lib/papers/reading-map.ts`: `minutes = ceil(words / WORDS_PER_MINUTE)`), written by the new `MAP.words(n)`: the summary line's number format (`toLocaleString("en-US")`, "1,234 words"), "1 word" singular, "0 words". Each part is `<span data-meta-part="role|page|words|minutes" class="whitespace-nowrap">`; every part but the first starts with its own `"· "`, so no part, and so no line, can end on "·" (P9-22's rule); one plain space between two spans, outside both, is the only break the meta has. Text content is the string a reader or a copy gets: `setup · p.1 · 569 words · 4 min` (before it was `setupp.14 min`: the spans were spaced by the flex gap, not by a space).

The rows' words are the ones the summary line adds up: `totalWords` in the view is the sum of `row.words` (it already was), and the rendered rows sum to it (§6: 11 rows, 5,478 = 5,478).

**Layout the words need.** `role · p.N · W words · N min` is up to ~290 px of monospace; beside a heading at 390 px it left the heading 50–160 px. So the heading and its meta are one group beside the fold toggle (`flex min-w-0 flex-1 flex-wrap items-baseline gap-x-2`); the heading link is `flex-[1_1_14rem]` (a floor of 224 px before the meta wraps) and the meta is `ml-auto min-w-0 max-w-full text-right` (right-aligned as it was, whether beside the heading or alone under it; it may itself wrap between two parts in a very narrow column). At 1440 the meta stays beside the heading on one line, as before; at 390 it sits under the heading on every row. Commit 2 first built this as a `flex-wrap` on the row with an 8rem floor; commit 4 moved the wrap to the group (§ rule 2) and commit 5 raised the floor to 14rem after measuring a 54-character heading at 390 px: 8rem left it 163 px, in 3 lines, beside the meta (row 58.7 px); 14rem gives it its whole 330-px line (row 44 px).

**Rule 2 (N5): the map's toggles are 44 px on touch.** `[@media(hover:none)]:min-h-11` (P9-23's rule, one constant `TOUCH_TALL`) on "show map" / "hide map" (both states) **and on each row's fold toggle ("+" / "−")** — the map's only other text toggles; the brief says "if any", there is one per foldable row. The visible text and the names are unchanged; with a mouse nothing moves (the floor is behind the media query; no bare `min-h-11`). With a 44-px toggle at the head of each row and the meta dropped under the heading, a row would have been 44 + the meta line (60 px; the map 613 px at 390 touch against 216 before), so the row (`items-baseline`) is `[@media(hover:none)]:items-center` on touch: the toggle is centred against the two lines and every row is 44 px (the map 484 px). With a mouse the row is baseline-aligned as it was.

**Rule 3: no other change to the map** — rows, order, tints (the `markedClass` on the same row `<div>`, same attributes), the summary line (`MAP.summary` and `BODY.provenance` untouched), the folds, the fact line and the paragraph lines are as they were. The heading link is still a ~20-px-tall link (it is not a toggle; not changed).
