## The rules

1. **Nested marks.** `passageSegments` (note-tags.tsx) returns a tree: a `mark` segment now holds `inner` runs (plain text and the marks of notes taken inside it) and a `depth`. Ranges sort by start, the longer first, the earlier note first; a range whose end lies inside a container is cut inside that container (clipped to where the earlier mark ended when the container is itself the later half of an overlapping pair). Equal ranges: the earlier note is the outer. Overlapping, not nested: unchanged (the later mark starts where the earlier ends). `NotedText` (paper-body.tsx, the paragraph render only) draws `<mark data-note="N">` inside `<mark data-note="M">`, each tag right after its own `</mark>`; the inner tag therefore stands inside the outer mark, then the outer tag after it. **A third inside the second:** the DOM nests as deep as the ranges do (the flash's `mark[data-note=N]` still finds each), and every mark at depth 1 or more shares the second shade — "a third nests as a second" read as a shade, not a split of the second mark (a split would give two `<mark data-note="2">`). **Shade:** new token `--color-note-nested` in the three palette blocks of globals.css (light `#fbe48d`, muted ink 4.53:1, heading 13.3:1; dark `#3a2f10`, muted 5.61:1, heading 11.9:1; mark against the highlighter 1.08 / 1.15). It is named so that `globals.css.test.ts`'s "read only by the body's note mark" list (a regexp for `--color-note-mark`) does not match it, so that test is untouched. A new test in `paper-body.mark.test.tsx` pins the token in each block, its difference from the highlighter, and the two contrasts.
2. **A tag stays with its words.** The tag's `<sup>` is `whitespace-nowrap` and opens with a word joiner (U+2060) before its button (the joiner, not an inline-block). **What the browser measurement found:** in Chromium the joiner alone did not hold a tag to a mark that ends in a space — the line broke after the space even before the joiner (a mark ending in `", "`: the tag stood alone at x 22 at widths 429–438 and 615–621, joiner present). A selection that runs on over a space (a double-click, a drag) stores the space in the range. So `rangeOf` now ends a mark at its last non-space character (`trimEnd`); the space stays a plain run after the tag. With that, the sweep below finds 0.
3. **Nothing else changes:** anchors (`start` / `end` / `quote` as stored; a range that is only white space draws no mark, its tag at the paragraph's end), the flash (`goToPassage` untouched), the sticky's numbering — the touched files are `note-tags.tsx`, `paper-body.tsx` (the `NotedText` block and its import), `globals.css` (the token), and their tests.
