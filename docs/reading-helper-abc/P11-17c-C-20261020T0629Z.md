## 2. The rules, as built

1. **One Escape, one card — the front one.** New `components/reader/front-escape.ts` (and its test): `frontWindowId` and `isFrontCard` (the window raised last; a lone card is the front), `takeFrontEscape` (the key is a card's when it is Escape, not `defaultPrevented`, the card is the front of `useWindows`' stack and no `[aria-modal="true"]` is over the page; it then prevents and stops it, so the keyboard layer's "back" on `window` does not run), and `useFrontEscape(card, enabled, take)` (one `document` listener in the bubble phase; the front is asked when the key comes, so a card that becomes the front because the one over it just closed answers the very next key). `card` is an id or `{ kind }` (the stickies are many).
   - `explain-box.tsx`: its own listener (its source pins are untouched) asks `isFrontCard` only while its window is in the stack; below 1024 it is outside the stack and answers as it always did.
   - `sticky-note.tsx`: `StickyNotes` registers `useFrontEscape({ kind: "sticky" }, …)` while any sticky stands and closes the front one (its writer flushed first); `StickyWindowView`'s own `onKeyDown` (the focus inside the card) answers only in front.
   - `notes-window.tsx`: `NotesWindow` registers `useFrontEscape(NOTES_WINDOW_ID, !!note, …)` (an open export menu first, as in the card); `NotesWindowView`'s `onKeyDown` answers only in front.
   - `figure-host.tsx`: `figureIsFront` is `isFrontCard(…, FIGURE_WINDOW_ID)`; its listener (already gated) is unchanged, and its header comment says the listener order no longer carries the rule.
2. **The left pane's top never scrolls inside itself.** On a window under 700 css px high (`@media (max-height: 699px)` in `globals.css`): the pane's title is clamped to **three** lines, not four (`[data-pane-title]`, set by `title-block.tsx` in the pane; the utility's `line-clamp-4` stays and the unlayered rule overrides it), and the gaps between the zone's blocks are **8 px, not 12** (`--pane-gap`; `reader-layout.tsx`'s two wrappers — the commands' top gap and the strip's, 4 px more — and the progress part's bar and status line read it with the 12 / 16 / 6 px they had as the fallback, so the one column, which has no pane to set it, is unchanged). The progress part is never hidden, the map never pushed off, and the cap (62 % of the pane's body) is **not** raised. From 700 px high the zone keeps four lines and the 12 px gaps.
   - Which means, from what height: the title's clamp first (4 → 3 lines, −31 px) and then the gaps (−14 px with a bar), from **699 px** down. The brief's "then the commands' row tighter" is the gap above it (12 → 8); the commands' own buttons (`reader-commands.tsx`, not mine) are untouched.
   - Why 700: the full zone is 337 px with a bar; the cap is 367 at 700, 30 px of room, and 329.8 at 640, where it would not fit.
3. **The figure fills the margin at Fit.** `figure-card.tsx`: the box's 8-px padding and `FIGURE_MAT_PAD_PX` are gone; `figureViewport` is the measured box whole. The card's border, header, zoom row and caption stay.
4. **The toolbar reads one marker.** `OPEN_CARD_SELECTOR = MARGIN_CARD_SELECTOR`. Checked in the DOM (§3.4): every element the legacy list matched also carries `data-margin-card`, so no card needed an attribute added (`margin-card.tsx` untouched).
5. **`defaultsFit` retired** from `pane-layout.ts` with its one test; `pane-layout.retired.test.ts` pins its absence.

## 7. For the manager

1. **A file outside the brief's list: `front-escape.ts` (+ its test).** `windows.ts` is read-only and four files share one rule, so the front-card logic is one small module, new, touching no one else's file. `figure-host.tsx` imports it for `isFrontCard`; nothing of C P11-17a's or P11-17b's is changed.
2. **A paper from the briefing is taller than the papers I could measure.** Both measured papers are uploads: no venue, no date, no authors. A feed paper's title block also has a meta line (22 px), a byline of three names and "+N more authors" (about 50) and the first author's affiliation (about 24). Injected with the block's own classes into the invented paper (`h/m-authors.mjs`, a stress and not a product state) the zone grows by about 117 px: **it scrolls inside itself again** at 1024 x 600 (settled: 342 in 305; running: 368 in 305) and at 1093 x 614 with a report running (337 in 314), though not settled there (310 in 310) nor at 1280 x 720 and up. The brief's means (the title's clamp, the gaps) do not reach it. A one-line byline in the pane (the full list in the scroll zone or behind the control), or the affiliation out of the fixed zone, would; it needs a ruling and is the next step if feed papers are to meet rule 2.
3. **The threshold is in real css px, and Fit scales the page.** Fit's zoom is 1 until the window is about 1,581 px wide (`fitZoom`); the cap is divided by the zoom, the media query is not. A window of about 1,920 px that is less than about 770 px high with Fit on (zoom 1.21) is a case rule 2 does not cover. Below 600 px high it does not hold either (1024 x 560, running: 12 px). Neither is in the brief's floor.
4. **A dev-server artefact, not the product's.** After the stylesheet and the TSX were edited in the same second, Next's dev server (Turbopack) served `globals.css` without the new `@media` block, even after a restart; clearing `.next/dev/cache` of my worktree fixed it. Compiling the file with the PostCSS plugin directly, and the production build (`gate-r2`, checked in the emitted CSS: `@media (max-height:699px){.reader-left{--pane-gap:.5rem}.reader-left [data-pane-title]{-webkit-line-clamp:3}}`), both carry it. Anyone measuring rule 2 on a long-lived dev server should expect that.
5. **The settle in `627f2cd4`.** The red commit's four-card test passes at `5d4ed0bb` without it; the commit only makes the harness settle between events as React does (the staged runtime renders once per event). The first draft of the hook asked "am I in front" at render, which needed it; the final hook asks when the key comes, which does not.
6. **Rule 4 needed no attribute.** `margin-card.tsx` is untouched. The explain card carries `data-margin-card` only as the dock (front and behind); the card under the selection and the phone's sheet do not, and are not cards of the margin (a test pins both).
