## 3. Where I departed from the brief, and why

a. **The panel is a window-high flex column; "Terms to know" shrinks and scrolls inside itself (`reader-panel`, `globals.css`).** The brief says pin by the top, "it is short now", the Terms strip may scroll with `max-h-[min(26rem,…)]`. Measured, it is not short: 757 px at 1440×1000 with no plate, three terms and a one-line title; a real paper adds a ~230-px plate and up to eight terms. Pinned by its top and cut by the window, the lower part is unreachable, and the brief's cap (min(26rem, 100vh − 24rem)) leaves it so with a plate. So: `display: flex; flex-direction: column; max-height: calc((100vh − 4rem − legend − 1rem) / zoom); overflow-y: auto; margin-inline: −6px; padding-inline: 6px` (the 6 px keep the 2px+3px focus ring inside the scroll port), and the strip's wrapper in `ReaderLayout` is `xl:min-h-[7rem] xl:shrink xl:overflow-y-auto`: the one block that gives. Only a window shorter than the plate, title, commands and strip makes the panel itself scroll (1280×560 here). No `--panel-h`, no `min()` in `top`, no JS. Pinned in `globals.css.test.ts`. If the manager wants the brief's literal cap instead, it is one class.
b. **The Summary blocks keep today's order** (proposal, how it was done, results, for your project, glance, related, thin, next step, record, library). The brief's list names "Where it is thin, For your project, Next step, At a glance, Related…", which differs; I read it as membership, not a reorder (the page for a paper with no body must stay as it was).
c. **The decision moves above the abstract for a paper with no body too** (Q1 says "the narrow layout too"; the brief's "a paper without a body is unchanged" is about tabs, the common blocks and the notes). The commands-first order inside the block is for every paper.
d. **"Terms to know" in one column** stands after the common blocks and before the tab's blocks (the brief names the common blocks, then the active tab's blocks; the strip had to go somewhere). On the spread it is in the panel under both tabs as the brief says.
e. **A tab the reader picks scrolls to its start when that has scrolled off the top** (`atStart`). Not in the brief; without it a pick from deep in the body lands wherever the other tab's scroll position clamps to (the bottom of Summary).
f. **The notes' "cited in" backlinks are gone from the reading page** (they were part of the band). The band still has them on the fallback page. Putting them somewhere (the record, "In your library") is a small item if the owner wants it.
g. **A sixth code commit (4b)** beside the four.
h. **`ExplainBox`'s `SectionLinks` is not given `reveal`**: its card is closed by every tab change and opens only from a selection in the body, so its quotes never meet a missing target. (The pin in `page.explain.test.ts` on that element is unchanged.)
