## The rules as built

**M2 (A), the group row.** `buildOutline` keeps `group` (the bucket and the heading size of the open part) and `headingSize` (the first line's size of the heading being read; a wrapped heading keeps it). In `flush`, for a heading that is **unnumbered**:
- if a group is open, the heading's own bucket is not structural (`STRONG_BUCKETS`: limitations, references, acknowledgments, supplementary, abstract) and it is set **smaller than the group's heading by more than 0.3 pt** (`GROUP_STEP`, strict), it takes the group's bucket and `level` 2;
- otherwise the group ends; and if the heading has **no text**, is being **read**, and its **whole** heading (after the glyph lead) is a part name (`PART_NAME`, B's regex: methods / methodology / experimental + section|procedures|details|methods|part with the optional online|extended and materials|experimental-and prefixes; results (and discussion); discussion; conclusion(s); introduction), a new group opens and the heading is written as a row with `text: ""` at `level` 1.

A **numbered** heading, and the unheaded opening, end the group and take no level. `level?: 1 | 2` is on `OutlineSection` and `ExtractedSection`; `normalize` carries it; `dropStrayHeadings` and `readableSections` keep an empty row with `level` 1 only when the next section has `level` 2 (an empty `level` 2 row, a `level` 1 row with no child, an empty row with no level: dropped as before); `readingMapOf` uses `section.level ?? levelOf(heading)`, so the group row is level 1 and its children level 2 with `parentId` the group row, and `groupTotals` and `roleOf` need nothing. `canonicalizeHeading` is not loosened (B M2-C). `STRONG_BUCKETS` in `html-text.ts` is now `export const` (one word) and `pdf-outline.ts` imports it, so the two files do not keep two lists.

**M5 lexical.** In `canonicalizeHeading`, after the `discussion` line and before the unanchored `evaluation|experiment|benchmark` line: `\bexperiments?\b` **and** one of `sample|specimen|preparation|synthesis|fabrication|assembly|measurements?|setup|details` (word-bounded, singular `sample`/`specimen` as the brief lists them) is `methods`. The anchored lines above it still place "Experiments" (results), "Experimental details" (methods) as before. The structural half of M5 (a sub-heading under a Methods part takes the part's bucket) is part of M2.

**M7 R7a.** The `discussion` line of `canonicalizeHeading` is now `discussion` **or** (`\bimplications?\b` or `\boutlook\b`) → discussion, so it stands before the methods words. One addition to the brief: the two new words do not apply when the heading also holds `conclusion` or `summary` ("Conclusions and outlook", "Summary and outlook", "Conclusion and implications" stay `conclusion`, as they were; the brief's "nothing else in `canonicalizeHeading` changes"). Lines before it still win ("Results and implications" → results, "Limitations and implications" → limitations).
