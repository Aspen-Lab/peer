# P10 — the owner's review of 2026-10-13: the rubric

The owner's review (`peer_branch_review-10-13-2026.pdf`, three pages, received 00:1xZ 2026-10-14; ruling §1h.78) is the spec of round P10. The owner's order: the `reviewing-cycle` skill — Fable the manager, Sonnet the A/B/C workers, **a PhD-level reviewer**, every theme **≥ 95 in two consecutive rounds**, the hourly Sonnet clock on, every item started at once. Measured the same way every round: a fresh stubbed dev server at the branch head, the owner's 19-page PDF (private, never committed) through the real upload, the walk at 1440 × 1000 (light and dark) with no key and with the stub key; 390 is measured and recorded but not scored (phone work deferred, §1h.77 (e)).

Two themes, each a list of **units** taken one for one from the review's numbered points. A unit is PASS (1) or FAIL (0) as its line says; a PASS needs the thing to work on the walk, not to exist in the code. **Extras** the walk finds are deductions by kind: a control over the reader's words, a window the reader cannot use or that covers the paper's words, a crash or an error view, an order that hides the content — 1.0 each; a wrap, a separator, a label, a count that misleads — 0.5; a cosmetic — 0.25.

Score = `100 × (Σ pass − Σ deductions) / N units`, floored at 0.

**Pass requirements beside the score** (a failure here is the theme at 0 whatever the arithmetic): no React error overlay and no "This view hit a snag" view anywhere in the walk; Tier 0 complete without a key (a locked block is hidden, never shown broken); nothing per-user (a question, a note, a passage, a key) in the shared reading cache, a server log line, a fixture or a checkpoint; the paper's text never hidden, collapsed, greyed or reordered by a route (§1a.4); the P9 feature list `scratchpad/p9/r1/features.json` F01–F24 still PASS except where this round retires or amends a line (F12 retired with "Terms to know"; F10 amended to one question; F11 amended to graded tints; F14 amended to the answers under the question; F15 amended to no window on entry; F16 amended to the floating notes window).

## Each round

1. The manager lands the round's items; the branch head is the round's code.
2. The PhD reviewer (A, on Sonnet, reading as a PhD student in electrochemistry who wrote this paper and now reads it in Peer) walks the surface at the head: a fresh stubbed server, the owner's PDF through the real upload, 1440 × 1000 light and dark, no key then the stub key, 390 recorded; every unit PASS / FAIL with one line of evidence and a screenshot; every extra costed; every earlier extra GONE / STILL / CHANGED; the score with its arithmetic and a sensitivity line.
3. The manager reads the whole walk (the screenshots, the DOM dumps) as the director; three blind readers score the same evidence pack against this rubric independently; the score of record is the median of A and the readers, the director's reading beside it; the director checks every disagreement against the evidence and voids a mark the evidence contradicts.
4. Defects clustered by mechanism → B where the cause is unknown → C items in parallel in detached worktrees → the manager verifies (gates, the touched tests ×3, one own mutation restored byte-identical) and lands.
5. Both scores recorded here and in the state file with the head; the owner gets the numbers, what changed, what is left, and the decisions only they can make.

Stop: every theme ≥ **99** in two consecutive rounds (the owner, 05:0xZ 2026-10-14, ruling §1h.89 (a); the original line: ≥ 95 — both recorded under each round from round 2 on). The loop's end is the manager's measure; the owner's own look at the surface is the acceptance above it.
