## 3. The pressed-toggle button

Confirmed as C says, in all five runs. With a rewrite showing, pressing "Say it plainly" hid it with 0 requests; pressing it again showed it again with 0 requests (from `peer-plain-v1`). `aria-pressed` is "true" while it shows. At 390 with real taps (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`) the same sequence worked, so a touch reader can undo without a keyboard: the toggle is the only touch undo, since there is no `u` on a phone. **My view: keep it.** It adds no copy, matches the three-level group's own pressed idiom, and without it a phone cannot take a rewrite back. The one cost is that the pressed state differs from the unpressed only by ink (`aria-pressed:text-heading`), so a sighted reader may not guess that a second press hides; the screen-reader state is correct. If the manager wants a cue it is one style line, not a reason to drop it. The manager rules.

## 5. §1h.12 (b): does a standalone upload's record reach `/api/saved`?

**Yes, when the signed-in reader saves it.** An upload's page offers Save like any paper (`page.tsx:1082-1086`, `save()` -> `savePaper(paper)`); `savePaper` (`store/feed.ts:2272-2299`) calls `cloudSave(paper.id, "paper", saved)` (`:2292`, `:325-337`), which POSTs `{ itemId, itemKind, payload }` to `/api/saved`, and the route upserts `payload` as-is into `saved_items` under the session's `user_id` (`app/api/saved/route.ts`). The same record is pushed again on sign-in for every saved paper (`components/feed-sync.tsx:215`, `pushSaved`). `payload` is the whole `Paper` record the upload produced (`uploadMetaToPaper`, `lib/papers/upload-store.ts:289-311`): `id` (`upload:<hash16>`), `title`, `summaryIntro` (the PDF's own abstract text, up to 400 characters, `upload/route.ts:296`), `summaryExperimentKeywords` and `preferenceSignals` (concept labels extracted from the PDF text), `uploadDocumentKey`, `revision`, `extractionVersion`, `doi`, `pageCount`, `textStatus`, `linkPaper` (`/api/papers/upload/<hash16>/file`), `isSaved` and `feedback`. Not sent: the body text or any paragraph, and nothing from Say it plainly (rewrites stay in the browser store). Saving also sends a feedback snapshot (title and concepts) to `/api/feedback` (`feedbackSnapshotForPaper`, `lib/preferences/ledger.ts:761`). The /privacy upload sentence ("a record of it (its file name, title and abstract, …) … in its own file storage against your account") describes the upload store only; it does not say that saving the upload copies that record, abstract included, into the account's saved papers. For the manager to correct: one clause saying that a saved upload's record (title, abstract excerpt, keywords) is stored with your saved papers. I did not read the profile sync (`recordUploadPreference`) beyond noting that it exists; the preference signals are the same labels.

## 6. §1h.12 (g): `displayQuote`'s aligned span

**A quote over 400 characters reaches the reader, in the explain first answer and in a reply** (both go through `placeQuote` -> `displayQuote`: `explain.ts:788` and `:936`). The deep report's evidence does not (it goes through `report.ts`'s own `text(…, REPORT_CAPS.evidenceChars)`). Reproduction through `verifyExplainAnswer`, invented text: a paper section holding 58 five-letter words each followed by a citation bracket `[n]`; the model's quote is the same 58 words without brackets (347 characters, under the 400 clip of `quoteAsSent`). The model's copy is not verbatim in the section (the brackets interrupt it), so `alignedOriginal` returns the paper's span: **575 characters**, `peer` not set, shown as the paper's quote beginning `w000x [1] w001x [2] …` and ending `… [1] w055x [2] w056x [3] w057x`. The code allows up to `2 * evidenceChars` = 800 (`explain.ts:734`); with denser brackets the reader would see up to 800. What the reader sees is the whole paper span, with its brackets, unmarked, in a quote card sized for 400; it is faithful to the paper, only longer than the cap. (A second fixture with a multi-number bracket `[10–25, 31]` fell back to the model's own 319 characters, since that bracket does not clean to nothing.) Manager rules: leave at 800 and say so, or return `null` above 400 so the fallback (cut at 400) applies.
