## The rules as built

**Rule 1 — a Shift+click outside the column, with a selection that began in it standing, ends at the column's nearest visible point to the pointer.**
- *Trigger.* A primary mouse `pointerdown` with `shiftKey`, whose target is **outside** the column and **on no control of its own** (`CONTROL_SELECTOR`; a control that wraps the column is not one), while `window.getSelection()` has an **anchor in the column**. The anchor (node, offset) is read at the press, in the capture phase, before the browser's own move.
- *A caret counts.* The brief's trigger says "not collapsed". In the browser a click on a word leaves a **collapsed** selection (`Selection.type` "Caret", `anchorNode` in the paper's text), and that is exactly what the walk's `y15` Shift+clicks from; a trigger that excluded it would never fire in the walk's own cells. So a caret in the column is "a selection that began in the column". A text field's own caret (the question field, any `input` / `textarea` / `[contenteditable]` with the focus) is not: it is the field's.
- *The correction.* One at the next animation frame (the browser has extended by then, and nothing paints in between), then on each move while the button is held (a click that is not quite still is a drag-extension to the browser: measured, a 1-3 px wobble re-extended it to the margin, 39,208 characters) and at the release — the press is followed as a press in the text is, each correction carrying the anchor read at the press. The end goes where a drag's release goes: `endSelectionInColumn` (reused as it was; its 7th argument `press` is the anchor).
- *What `press` changes in `endSelectionInColumn`.* (1) A caret is not "collapsed, leave it": the press's selection may be a caret. (2) A pointer over the column's box is still corrected: the press's target was outside the column, so the pointer is over an overlay (the legend at the window's foot, the masthead), and the caret is found further in, as for a drag's release there. (3) The anchor: where the selection no longer has an anchor in the column, it is put back from the anchor read at the press (`collapse(anchor)`), then ended at the edge, in that order. (4) False, nothing touched, where the press's anchor is no longer in the column.
- ***The 12-23 px band over the separator.** The brief says the browser collapses the selection on the press there. **Measured, it does not: it changes nothing.** The separator's own `pointerdown` calls `preventDefault()` (pane-divider.tsx, "the press is ours"), so the browser's selection handling does not run: no `mousedown` is dispatched, a caret stays the caret (the walk's "0 characters" is a caret that was not moved), a range stays the range. The correction therefore extends the standing selection from its own anchor to the line's end at the pointer's height (the same place as 40 px past). The restore-from-the-press branch is built as asked and unit-tested, but **I could not make the real browser take it**: even with the margin made `user-select: none` the browser left the selection alone (the calls made on it were `extend` only). It stays as a defence.*
- *Wrapping.* `keep-selection.ts` only; the hook's one call in the shell is unchanged (`useKeepSelection(grid)`, still one call).

**Rule 2 — nothing else changes.**
- A **drag** (a press that began in the text) is byte for byte as before: the controller's drag path calls `env.endAt(x, y)` with two arguments exactly as before (`release` is the old `pressed = null; ask(...)`), `endSelectionInColumn` with no `press` runs the old two early returns unchanged. Measured: 576 drags before and after, **576 cells identical** (length, where it ends, end position, buttons).
- A Shift+click **inside** the column is the browser's (its press is in the text: followed as any press in the text, as today). A Shift+click **on a control** (the notes tab, a card's button, a map link, a masthead link, the notes editor, a sticky's editor) is the control's. A Shift+click outside the column with **no column selection standing** (a card's text, the notes editor's, the left pane's title selection) is left alone. A press **without Shift**, **touch and pen**, a secondary button: as before. The separators' own drag and keys, the notes tab's pass-through, the one-column page: untouched (measured below).
- **A press that puts a text field in focus** is the field's (found in the frame after, then skipped): the notes window's static text turns into its editor on a click, and the click puts its caret in the editor; correcting the paper's selection over that left the editor focused with the document's selection elsewhere. Measured on the first build (before this guard): a Shift+click on the notes window's text with a column caret standing ended with 65 characters in the paper and the editor focused; with the guard it is what it was before (a caret in the notes editor, 0 characters, editor 67 characters, identical). This guard is the card-editor answer to the brief's "confirm the card's own editor is not disturbed".
- **A Shift+click on a card's own text** (the brief's question): the card's text is outside the column, so rule 1 applies — I kept that. Explain card open, a column caret standing, Shift+click on the card's text: 39,601 characters ending in the card before; **65 characters ending at the line's end, the three buttons offered** after. The card's own *editors* (a sticky's, the notes window's) are controls / become fields: the sticky's `[contenteditable]` is a control (identical before and after: 39,347 characters ending in the sticky, 27 characters in the editor, as today); the notes window's editor is identical before and after (above). A selection that began *in the card* (a double click on a word of the card's text) is not a column selection: a Shift+click on the card's text is the browser's, identical before and after (paired rerun with the base file swapped in and restored: 2 -> 31 characters both).

## Unsettled / for the manager

1. **The brief's "the browser collapses the selection" over the separator is not what the browser does** (it changes nothing: the separator's `pointerdown` is prevented); the restore-from-the-press branch is built, tested in Node, and not reachable in any real case I could make. If you would rather not carry a branch that the browser never takes, it is two lines (`kept` and the `collapse` in `endSelectionInColumn`, and its tests S4 / the restore cases).
2. **A caret counts as a standing selection** (the brief says "not collapsed"). That is what the walk's own cells need (a click, then a Shift+click). A stale caret in the paper therefore makes a Shift+click far away end at the column's edge near the pointer, where the browser ran to the paper's end: I think that is the intent, but it is a reading.
3. **A Shift+press that begins on a separator and moves (a Shift+drag of the separator)** now also ends the selection at the column's edge at the pointer's height while the pane resizes (the pane's own drag is measured identical). Nobody holds Shift to resize, but it is a behaviour the brief did not name.
4. **The masthead case offers no buttons** for a selection whose top edge is under the masthead — the toolbar's placement, the same for a drag; not mine.
5. **A Shift+click inside any other modal or overlay with text** (outside the reader shell) with a paper selection standing would be corrected too (the hook is the document's). None exists on the reader page that I found; a `dialog` would need a target test if one is added.
6. The base's full-suite count (10,471) is derived by subtraction.
