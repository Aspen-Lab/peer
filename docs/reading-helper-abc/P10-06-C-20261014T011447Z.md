## The rules as built

1. **Score** (`relevanceScores`, exported, pure): `idf = ln((N + 1) / (df + 1)) + 0.1` over the sections that have paragraphs of their own; score `Σ idf × ln(1 + count) ÷ ln(2 + words / 200)`. The counts are the existing `hits` counts (same function, same text), so the mentions line is unchanged. A group row (no paragraphs, with rows whose `parentId` is its id) takes its strongest child's score, the union (summed counts) of the children's hits and that child's evidence; it is not in N or df. `parentId` is read structurally (`ReadingMap["sections"][number] & { parentId?: string }`), so it works before and after P10-04 adds the field; `level` is not needed.
2. **Tiers** (`gradeScores`, exported): `strong` = score ≥ 0.6 × max **and** one of the top three rows by score (document order breaks ties); `medium` ≥ 0.3 × max; `faint` any other mention; `none` score 0. One mentioned section: strong. `RouteTier = "strong" | "medium" | "faint" | "none"`; the gist route: `read → strong`, `skim → faint`, `background` still a fourth mark of the display layer. Tokens `--color-route-strong / -medium / -faint` replace `read / skim`; `--color-route-background` stays (a cooler green now, see below). `ROUTE.tiers`: `strongest`, `relevant`, `less relevant`, `background`, `not mentioned`.
3. **Paragraph lines**: `RouteSection.topParagraphs?: number[]` — the paragraphs that say most by the same score over their own words (df over the section's paragraphs; ties all top). `sectionMark` gives a top paragraph the section's tier and every other mentioning paragraph `faint`; a section with no `topParagraphs` (hand-built route, model mark) tints its paragraphs all alike, as before; a paragraph with no hit is not in `paragraphs` and stays untinted.
4. **Facts line**: `strongest · mentions …`, `relevant · mentions …`, `mentions …` (faintest: no tier word), `not mentioned` unchanged; `less relevant` stands alone only for the gist's faint rows (no counts).
5. **Nothing else**: mentions counts, evidence sentence, the cache and its key (`routeByQuestionsCached` untouched; it caches the whole `QuestionRoute`), the `background` mark, order, anchors, export. `PaperReading` is unchanged and has no copy of the route: no version bump.
