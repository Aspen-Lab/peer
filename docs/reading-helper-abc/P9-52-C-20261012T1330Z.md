## 2. The rules, as built

Every addition is behind `[@media(hover:none)]:` (the P9-23 rule, `min-h-11`); with a mouse no class of any control changed, and the measured boxes at 1440 are identical to the pixel (section 6). Where a control grows on touch, the box is the control's own (not a pseudo-element), because A's sweep reads `getBoundingClientRect()`.

**Rule 1 - the reader's** (commit 2).
- `terms-strip.tsx`: the "show all N terms" / "fewer terms" toggle gets `min-h-11` and gives up its `mt-3` on touch (`[@media(hover:none)]:mt-0`: its 16 px words sit 14 px down a 44 px box, so the 12 px of margin would have put them 26 px under the last term; they sit 14 px, 2 px lower than before). Each term button gets `TERM_TOUCH` = `min-h-11 py-[11px] bg-clip-content`. The padding makes the box 44.5; `bg-clip-content` keeps the *marked* term's highlight (`aria-pressed:bg-[color:var(--color-term-mark)]`) to the word's own 22.5 px line, so the 44 px is a hit area and not a taller block of colour (checked: pressed COD, 32.7 x 44.5, `background-clip: content-box`, padding `11px 0`; screenshot `shots/after-reader-termmark.png`). The button's own width stays the word's (30 to 90 px).
- `question-field.tsx`: the × (a lone glyph) gets `min-h-11 min-w-11`, 44 x 44 (the glyph centred; the textarea, `flex-1 min-w-0`, gives it the room). The "Not for recommendations" row: the **label** gets `min-h-11`; it is already `basis-full`, so 350 px wide; a tap anywhere on it ticks the box (verified: a touch tap at the row's empty end flipped `checked` false -> true).
