## How today's map was made

The pipeline the app runs on an upload, with no key (Tier 0): `extractPdfTextFromBytes` (pdf.js text layer → `buildOutline` → `normalize` → figure sizes, the call `app/api/papers/upload/route.ts` and `full-text.ts` make) → `readableSections` → `readingMapOf` (`reading-map.ts`). Driven from a scratch Vitest file and config kept outside the repository (scratchpad `p12/b43/tools/`), run against the worktree's code. Every row's level, page, canonical bucket and role (`roleOf`) were recorded. The rubric's formula is applied to the data of the map; the page's own words for a role were not rendered, so the 0.25 charge ("a role shown in words a reader would misread") is not assessed.

Scoring conventions I had to choose (the manager may rule otherwise; none of them moves a score off 0):

- **PASS strict:** a row whose first paragraph is the part's first paragraph and whose role is the ruled one. **PASS lenient:** the same, but the row's first paragraph may carry one foreign fragment ahead of the part's start (the extractor joins lines of two columns).
- **Extras (lower bound):** 0.5 for each row that is not a part of the paper (title and author block, abstract fragment, figure or chart text) and 0.5 for each sub-row whose role contradicts its part. Rows that hold fragments of the reference list are back-matter sub-rows and are not charged unless their role contradicts.
