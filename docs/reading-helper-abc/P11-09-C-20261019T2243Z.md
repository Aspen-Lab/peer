## 2. What each rule is, in the final tree

**Rule 1 — the mark.**
- `note-tags.tsx`: `AnchorSpot` gains `colour?: HighlightColourId` and `order?: number`; `isHighlightSpot(spot)` (a spot with a colour); `tieOrder(a, b)` = highlight rank (a sticky is the outer of a highlight), then `order ?? n` (two stickies by number exactly as before, two highlights by the earlier `order`), used by `passageSegments`. `NoteTag` takes an optional `ink`.
- `paper-body.tsx`: `coveringSegments`' sort uses the same `tieOrder`. `notedRuns(text, segments, within?)`: a highlight spot is `<mark data-highlight="id" style="background-color; color">` from `highlightPaint(colour, segment.depth)` (depth 0 the fill, deeper the deep shade), no tag; its `part` marks `data-highlight-part`; a highlight's loose tag segment is dropped. `within` = the nearest enclosing highlight's colour and the ink under the words: a sticky's mark / part inside a highlight wears `highlightPaint(that colour, 1)` inline (no token class) and keeps its tag, which gets `ink` (a sticky's `[n]` in muted grey on a bright highlight was 1.35:1 on the dark theme's deep yellow, 3.31:1 on the light theme's: not in B's design, which treated marks only; see §6).
- I4: with no highlight in a paragraph every branch is the old one (`wear` null, `ink` undefined): `paper-body.mark.test.tsx` (all of it) and `globals.css.test.ts` pass unchanged.

**Rule 2 — the click and the flash.**
- `paper-body.tsx`: new optional `PaperBody` prop `onHighlightClick(id, rect)`; one `onClick` on the body's root `<div id="paper-body">` (none when the prop is absent) reads the click with the new exported `highlightClickOf(target, selection)`: the highlight id (of `data-highlight` or `data-highlight-part`; a sticky's mark inside a highlight is the highlight's) and `rect` = the first line box of the highlight's first mark in its paragraph (a nested part comes first in reading order); null for a non-collapsed selection (a drag that ends on a mark, a double click's second click), for a click on a control (a sticky's tag keeps its own click), and for any click on no highlight's mark. One delegated listener, not one per mark, so a click on a nested mark tells once (the innermost highlight).
- `note-tags.tsx`: `goToPassage(anchor | HighlightGoal, env)`; `HighlightGoal = { paperId, paragraphId, highlightId }`; flashes `highlightMarks(id)` = `mark[data-highlight="id"], mark[data-highlight-part="id"]` with the sticky's `MARK_FLASH_CLASSES`; a highlight with no mark drawn flashes the whole paragraph, as a sticky does.

**Rule 3 — the selection's offset (I10).**
- `paper-body.tsx`: `SelectionTarget.offset?: number` (so `ExplainSelection`'s); `readSelection` sets it for the paper's body only: a `Range` cloned from the selection's, `selectNodeContents(<p>)`, `setEnd(startContainer, startOffset)`, `wordsWithoutTags(cloneContents())` else `toString()`, its length. A hint, never required: a range without `selectNodeContents` / `setEnd` / `startOffset`, or one that throws, leaves the selection as it was (no key). `RangeLike` gains those members as optional. `sameSelection` also compares `offset`.

## 6. Beyond the brief, and what is unsettled

- `NoteTag` gained `ink?` and `notedRuns` passes it for a tag inside a highlight (§2): B's design reasoned about marks; the tag's muted ink on a bright highlight is unreadable (1.35:1 dark / 3.31:1 light on the deep yellow). Its hover colour is now the inline ink (the underline on hover stays).
- The click listener sits on the body's root, not on each mark (testable on the element tree, and one event per click); `PaperBody` renders no `onClick` when `onHighlightClick` is absent.
- `highlightClickOf` returns null when the element gives no box rather than inventing a position.
- A double click on a highlight raises one `onHighlightClick` (the first click of the pair has a collapsed selection) and then the word is selected: the strip's owner drops the strip on the next selection (B §5), so nothing more is needed here.
- The mark is not a button for the keyboard (B §3.3: the notes row is that path); no pointer cursor is set on it (a `className` would not be byte-identical for the page that passes no handler, and there is no per-mark knowledge of the handler).
- The merge: this commit's `paper-body.tsx` hunks are the two `import` lines, `SelectionTarget` (+`offset`), `sameSelection` (+1 line), `RangeLike`, `wordsBefore` and `readSelection`'s return, the new `highlightClickOf` block before `useSelectionListener`'s comment, `NotedText`'s comment, `coveringSegments`' sort, the `Within` / `notedRuns` block, and `PaperBody`'s props, closure and root `<div>`.
