# P12-89 C addendum: the gap between the paper's text and the margin divider's strip (ruling §1h.475) - measured, NOT built

Written by C (Claude Sonnet 5.5), 2026-10-28 (UTC 22:10), on top of `P12-89-C-20261028T2142Z.md` (`c4e930c0`). The manager's ruling §1h.475 asked for rule 1 to be revised (the tab to the left of the strip, in the centre column's right gutter, 2 px clear of the strip, off the text and every control; no header dodge, no `z-[69]`), on one condition: build it only if the gap between the paper text's right edge (the widest text line's right) and the strip's left edge is at least 17 px (15 tab + 2 clear) in every cell. ## Method

The same static page as the checkpoint (section 6): the real `.reader-shell` grid with the pane widths `fitPanes` gives at each window and step (`centreMinimum` = `readingCentreMinimum(step)`), the real stylesheet (Tailwind compile of `globals.css`), headless Chromium 1194, no dev server. The centre holds eight invented sections (a heading, a justified paragraph of the paper's text classes `font-reading text-body reading-justify measure-paper` inside a `.reading-scaled` wrapper at the step's `--reading-scale`, an italic caption), the paragraph lines being full width. The text's right edge is the largest `right` over every line box of every text node in `.reader-centre-inner` (`Range.getClientRects()`); the strip is the margin divider's rect. One, two and three sticky cards stand in the margin (their rows from `stackLayout`, with the real header) in each cell; the cards stand at the margin's left edge (the strip's right edge) and do not move the text, so the gap does not depend on their number. Harness and results: `…/scratchpad/p12/c89/measure/` (`gap.mjs`, `gap-input.json`, `gap.json`, `zz-gap-c89.test.tsx.live`).

## What the numbers say

- The gap is the centre column's own 12 px of side padding (`.reader-centre { padding-inline: 12px }`) plus whatever blank the 38em cap leaves. Wherever the column is narrower than the cap (1024 and 853 px, at the default step; 853 px at both steps) the paragraph fills it and the gap is exactly 12 px; at 1024 x 576 at the largest step the cap leaves 1 px each side (13 px). Only the 1440 x 900 cells have room (45 and 70 px).
- A 15 px tab 2 px clear of the strip needs 17 px; it is **5 px short** in the two rail cells that matter (1024 x 576 and 853 x 480 at the default step: the tab would be over the last 5 px of every text line at its height). A tab narrowed to the least that holds its label (about 12.5 px: the 11.5 px type and its border) would still need 14.5 and be 2.5 px over the text.
- At the largest step the page registers the bottom band at 1024 x 576 and 853 x 480, where the tab stays on the window's right edge, 254.9 px from the strip, with no overlap of the strip, the text or a card (checkpoint section 6); the gap above is then not an input to the tab.
- The ruling's condition is therefore not met at 1024 x 576 and 853 x 480 (default step), and I did not build. Rule 1 as committed (`e65cb81f`: the tab inside the margin, with the header dodge and `z-[69]`) is still the tree's rule 1, with the two overlaps the ruling names (the tab over the first ~5 px of a card's text lines at its height; with three cards in a short window, over a header: 49 of 49 and 40 of 40 sweep anchors).
