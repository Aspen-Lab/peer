## The rules as built

1. One question, one bar: `question-field.tsx` holds a single `textarea[data-ask-line]` (rows 1, grows, 500 chars, counter from 400, a pasted newline folded) in a `[data-ask-bar]` flex box, `min-h-12`, full width, with the Submit button inside its right end. No second line, no ×, no "Up to five". Enter submits (Shift+Enter the same; an IME composition's Enter is not a submit). A reader with up to five stored questions sees the first; storage is left alone until the bar is first changed, and Submit makes the stored list the one question (`MAX_QUESTIONS` stays 5 in the store).
2. Label `ASK.heading` = `What you want to learn about this paper:`; the standing questions' hint re-worded ("listed under the question bar while you type"); `/privacy` re-worded. The old label is gone from every file I own.
3. `border-2 border-heading bg-surface`, placeholder `text-text-muted`. Contrast (from `globals.css`, also asserted by a test): light border `#1d1d1d` vs page `#fafafa` 16.15:1, vs the bar `#ffffff` 16.86:1; the bar `#ffffff` differs from the page `#fafafa`. Dark: border `#f3f3f3` vs page `#111111` 17.02:1, vs the bar `#232323` 14.16:1; bar differs from page. Placeholder (muted) on the bar 5.74:1 light, 6.69:1 dark. No new token.
4. The dropdown: `role="listbox"` under the bar (groups with headings; options `role="option"`), the combobox attributes on the textarea; opens with focus on an unsettled question (text differs from the last submitted one, or nothing submitted), closed after Submit, on blur, on Escape (the first Escape is the dropdown's and stops the page's own Escape-blur; Down reopens), Down/Up wrap through the bar, Enter picks a highlighted item (else submits), the pointer picks (mouse-down does not blur). A pick fills the bar, puts the caret at its end, submits nothing. "Just get the gist" is the last item, pinned below the scrolling examples under a rule; picking it is the gist route (no text, gist on, the bar's placeholder reads it), again turns it off. Nothing is listed at rest. Items have the 44-px touch floor.
5. Submit: disabled (grey) until the text changes after a submit; a submit with the same text does nothing (`hasNewQuestions`); settle paths are Submit and Enter only.
6. `profile.questionsTuneRecommendations?: boolean` (absent = on; off stores `false`, on removes the key; also in the backup file, a non-boolean ignored), the switch (`Toggle`, "Use my questions to tune recommendations" and the one line) in `standing-questions-field.tsx` under the standing questions; `syncQuestionTerms` returns early when it is `false` (nothing learned, nothing removed). The per-question `notForRecs` / `settledNotForRecs` keys are ignored, not migrated, and a write drops them. `/privacy`: the tick sentence became "Switch off “Use my questions to tune recommendations” on your Profile and the words of the questions you submit after that are not added; the words already added stay."
7. `ForYourQuestions` is one button `Answers generated by AI based on this paper` (`FOR_YOUR_QUESTIONS.control`), `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `useState(false)` on every visit, directly after the field in the `common` block (page.tsx untouched: it already drew `{answers}` right after `<QuestionField>`); the panel (`hidden` when closed) holds, per question, the question, the verdict line, `ul[data-answers] > li` one per answer (text, then `EvidenceQuote` with section and page), then Read next. No control for no questions. Interpretation: a report whose every verdict is "not addressed" still shows the control (the sentence is the information).
