## 2. The rules, as built

**1. One scroll region on the spread (>= xl)** — `reader-layout.tsx`, `globals.css` (comment only).
`TERMS_PANEL_CLASS` (`xl:min-h-[7rem] xl:shrink xl:overflow-y-auto xl:overflow-x-hidden`) and its wrapper `<div>` are gone: the spread's panel now holds `{p.terms}` directly, an ordinary block. (`overflow-x-hidden` went with the rest on purpose: with `overflow-y` visible it computes `overflow-y: auto`, so it would have kept the strip a scroll container.) A flex item with `overflow: visible` has `min-height: auto`, so inside `reader-panel`'s flex column it never shrinks below its content; the panel (`overflow-y: auto`, bounded by `max-height: calc((100vh - 4rem - var(--legend-h) - 1rem) / var(--page-zoom, 1))`) is the one scroll region. `reader-panel`'s numbers are unchanged (its pinning tests in `globals.css.test.ts` pass untouched); the P7-14 R2 note "the one block that can give" is replaced, in `globals.css` and in the layout's header, by "the panel scrolls as one; nothing inside it scrolls on its own".

**2. The tab strip directly above its panel in one column (< xl)** — `reader-layout.tsx`.
The one-column return is now plate, title, decision, `{common}`, `{p.terms}`, `{p.tabs}`, `{panel}`, `{p.next}`. The spread's order is unchanged (strip in the panel beside the column, a remote control by design, P7-14 R10). One addition the brief did not name, found by measuring (below): the one-column tabpanel carries `flow-root`. Without it the first block's own top gap (the Map's `mt-6`, a band's `mt-12`) collapsed out through the panel, so the strip's bottom and the panel's top were 24 px apart (measured), against the brief's "<= 8 px"; with it the panel's box begins at the strip's foot (0 px). The visual gap is the block's own, as before. The spread's tabpanel is already a flex column and does not take it.

**3. The Terms strip folds on a phone (< sm)** — `terms-strip.tsx`, `copy.ts` (`TERMS` only).
Below `sm` the strip shows its first two terms (`PHONE_TERMS = 2`); terms 3 and on carry `hidden sm:block` while folded (the pure `hiddenOnPhone(index, total, open)`, true only for `total > 2 && !open && index >= 2`); a toggle after the list, `aria-expanded`, `sm:hidden`, text `TERMS.showAll(n)` = `show all ${n} terms` / `TERMS.fewer` = `fewer terms`, exists only when there are more than two terms (`terms.length > PHONE_TERMS`). All terms stay in the DOM (server and client markup agree; the map's pattern, copied not imported). `>= sm`: all terms, no toggle. The prop `phoneOpen` (default false) is the initial state, for tests, as the map's. `useEscapeToClear`, `termButtonState`, `findInBody` and the `Find X in the paper` labels are untouched.
