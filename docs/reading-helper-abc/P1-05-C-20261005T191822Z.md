### `web/src/components/reader/paper-body.tsx`

The shared route marking lives here, beside the anchors, because the rail and the map already import from this file. Putting it in `reading-map.tsx` would have made the two modules import each other at runtime.

New exports:
- `TintTier = RouteTier | "background"`.
- `ROUTE_TINT`, the tier → class table. It maps `read`, `background` and `skim` to `bg-[color:var(--color-route-*)]`, and `none` to null.
- `HEADING_MARK = "w-fit -mx-1 px-1"`: a tinted heading's tint hugs its words, and the text does not move.
- `SectionMark` and `sectionMark(route, sectionId)`. Across the questions, ignoring vague ones and tier `none`, it computes:
  - the highest tier (read > background > skim);
  - `title`, the numbers of the questions that mark the section ("Q1, Q3"), or "Just get the gist" for the gist route;
  - merged `hits`, spelled as the reader typed them and stable-sorted by count;
  - `evidence`, from the highest-tier question that has one;
  - `paragraphs`, mapping each paragraph index to the highest tier of the questions that mention it.
  - It returns null for no route, a vague route, or a section no question marks.
- `routeAsksQuestions(route)`: true when the route is not vague and is not the gist.
- `markedClass`, a small class helper.

`PaperBody` and `Section` take `route?`. The `h3` gets `data-route` and the tint plus `HEADING_MARK`. Nothing else in the body changed.

## Choices beyond the brief, for A and the manager

1. **Tokens in three blocks.** They are in `html[data-mode="dark"]` too, not only `:root` and the system-dark block, per the file's keep-in-sync rule.
2. **Where the route shows.**
   - Tint plus `title` on the rail row (`<a>`) and the map row (header `div`).
   - Tint plus `data-route` only on the body `h3`. The brief allows exactly an attribute and a class there, so there is no `title`.
   - The paragraphs are never tinted.
3. **Fact line placement.** It sits on its own line under the row header, aligned with the heading, rather than inline. It is the tier label plus `mentions …`, capped at 3 terms. The tier label is the only place the reader sees the word "read", "skim" or "not mentioned".
4. **Spelling.** Hits are shown as the reader typed them (the question's token with the same lower-case form), e.g. "LCO". The route still reports lower-case (§1f.6); only the display restores the case.
5. **Gist rows** are titled "Just get the gist" (not "Q1"), show the tier label only, and never show "not mentioned".
6. **"not mentioned"** appears on unmarked map rows only when at least one non-vague typed question exists.
7. **Evidence for several questions.** The evidence comes from the first question at the section's highest tier that has one.
8. **`readingRoute` in `reading-map.tsx`**, so the page and the effects tests share one function.
9. **`evidence-quote.tsx` touched** (an export added, `EvidenceQuote` refactored onto it with identical output) to share the link between the three attributions.
