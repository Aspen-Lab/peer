### 3.4 What using the boxes costs

- **Order.** The outline is built synchronously from pdf.js text, before the engine runs: `extractPdfTextFromBytes` calls `readPages`, then `buildOutline`, then `withFigureSizes`, which asks the engine for sizes only after the sections are made. A box rule needs the engine first, as a new pre-pass, and `buildOutline` a new input (like `prior`); after the sections are joined the geometry is gone.
- **Time and size.** The engine's whole pass takes 0.7 s (P3), 2.0 s (P4), 1.2 s (P6); P5, at 25.6 MB, is refused by the whole-document call (ceiling 18,000,000 bytes; `withFigureSizes` finds nothing for it today) and takes 2.9 s page by page (the one-page ceiling is 27,000,000). The upload ceiling is 26,214,400, so the page-by-page route is the only one that reads every upload.
- **Dependency on captions.** A vector cluster is a figure only with a "Figure N" caption, as the outline's own rule is; the box rule has no reach where the engine finds nothing.
- **Revision.** Text derived from engine boxes belongs under `FIGURE_EXTRACTION_REVISION` (now "11"); the upload document cache key already carries it (`uploadDocKey`), so a bump rebuilds cached text.
- **Degradation.** If the engine fails, is over its 25 s budget, or the file is over the ceiling, the outline rules of §4 still stand alone. That is the order AGENTS.md asks for: the plain rule first, the engine as an enhancement.

## 4. Q3: the smallest rule for each mechanism, and its blast radius

All rules below are variants of `isPanelLine` and `figureTextOf` (and, for the running foot, `furnitureOf`) in `web/src/lib/papers/pdf-outline.ts`, run through the six maps; "tests" counts the red ones among the 683 of `pdf-outline*`, `pdf-text*` and `case-repair` (22 files). P1 and P2 are unchanged (0 and 6 short blocks, no line removed) under every rule except the negative control.

### 4.1 The rules

- **R-B (length), for B and for the D it causes.** A line set smaller than the body (below 0.95) may be up to **60** characters; a line at or above that size stays at 25. One new constant beside `PANEL_LINE_MAX`, one conditional in `isPanelLine`. The "smaller than the body" guard is not optional (§4.3).
- **R-A (reach), for A.** Replace "within 8 body sizes of a caption's line" by "joined to a caption of its page by nothing but lines that are not body lines": walk from the caption's first line up, and from its last line down, over the non-skipped lines of the same page, and stop at the first **body line**, a line set within 5 % of the body size with 40 or more characters (or at another caption, or the page's edge). A run with a member in that stretch is beside the caption. The simpler form, "any caption on the same page", also works (below) but moves one pinned test.
- **R-C (word), for C and BC; optional.** Inside that stretch, drop the four-letter-word test for a panel line. It takes 7 more blocks on P4 and 3 to 4 on P6, and moves one pinned test.
- **R-D (run), for D; not proposed.** `PANEL_RUN_MIN` 3 to 2: two more blocks on a paper at best, three pinned tests.
- **R-F (running foot), for the largest part of F.** In `furnitureOf`: (1) fold the two folio sides into one key (a page number that stands at either end of the line, next to a bar, is not part of the head), (2) take the 40 % of the pages that *carry text* (15 lines or more), not of all pages. Nothing else in `isFurniture` changes.
- **R-Box (box), for A, B, C and D at once.** A held line lying in an engine box on its page is figure text.

## 5. What no figure rule reaches, and what a figure rule hides

- **The F group is not figure text** (25 blocks): feet 12, formula fragments 9, one-line prose 2, reference fragments 2. R-Box and R-A/R-B leave all of them, except that R-F takes the feet.
- **A figure rule hides the feet unless R-F lands with it.** After the figure text goes, a foot at a page's bottom is no longer its own short block: the unfinished paragraph above it takes it in. On P5 the standalone short feet go 6 → 3 (R-A + R-B) → 1 (R-Box) while the foot count in the map stays **8**, 2 → 5 → 7 of them now inside real paragraphs; on P6 7 stays 7, with 3 → 3 → 4 inside paragraphs. "Short, unended blocks" would fall and the prose would get worse. **R-F must land with, or before, R-A and R-B.** (This is also why the box variant's P5 count of 1 is not the whole story.)
- **Page-break fusions:** 14 blocks across P3 to P6 weld an in-box line at the top of a page to the unfinished paragraph from the page before (P3 3, P4 4, P5 4, P6 3). R-A/R-B/R-Box remove the figure line from them; the foot and the sentence stay joined.
- **Formula fragments** (9) are a different problem (unnumbered display equations; §1h.41 (c) chose to keep a lone line of symbols as prose).
