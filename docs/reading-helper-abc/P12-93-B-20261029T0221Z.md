## 2. Q1: what keeps each block

### 2.1 Reach (A) is the main mechanism: 177 blocks, 197 lines

`figureTextOf` takes a run only if some member stands within 8 body sizes (66.4 pt on P5 and P6, 65.6 on P4, 74.4 on P3) of a caption's baseline. The figures here are 25 to 66 body sizes tall (text-bearing boxes: P3 29.9 to 61.6, P4 28.6 to 66.4, P5 38.0 to 56.2, P6 25.2 to 59.6), with the caption under the figure. The rule therefore reaches the lowest eighth of a figure's height, and what is above it stays prose.

| Paper | A-runs (3 or more panel lines, none within 8 sizes of a caption) | run length | nearest member to the caption, body sizes |
|---|---|---|---|
| P3 | 6 | 6 to 20 | 9.8 to 47.7 |
| P4 | 14 | 3 to 14 | 10.1 to 57.4 |
| P5 | 24 | 3 to 16 | 8.5 to 54.2 |
| P6 | 4 | 6 to 9 | 20.0 to 49.0 |

Of the 48 runs, 3 have a member within 10 body sizes of the caption, 6 within 12, 10 within 16 and 25 within 24 (median 23.0); the farthest is 57.4. So the rule is short by a factor of three to seven, not by a hair: a reach of 16 would take 10 of the 48 runs, and only a reach of 58 takes all of them.

### 2.2 Length (B) is second: 89 blocks

The line is 26 to 59 characters (median 31 on P3, 33 on P4, 37 on P5, 40 on P6). It cannot be a panel line (`PANEL_LINE_MAX` = 25). These are axis titles, legend entries and panel titles. They keep themselves, and they cut the runs of short tick lines on either side of them (D).

### 2.3 Run under 3 (D): 31 blocks, a consequence of B

All 32 D lines inside a box have a longer neighbour. Relaxing B gives the runs back; a `PANEL_RUN_MIN` of 2 would remove about two more blocks per paper and costs three existing tests (§3).

### 2.4 Word (C, BC): 11 blocks, none set small

P4 holds 8 blocks (C 4, BC 4) and P6 3 (C): panel titles and letters at 1.16 to 1.23 of the body size on P4, and three legend-like lines at 0.96 on P6. The four-letter test lets a *small* line through (below 0.95 of the body); these lines are not small.

- **Running feet (12: P2 1, P5 6, P6 5, the last counting a copyright line).** The journal prints its name, volume, date and page number at the foot of every main-text page of P5 and P6, with the page number on alternate sides. `furnitureOf` wants a line's normalised words on at least `max(3, 40 % of the pages)`; P5 has 21 pages (the Extended Data pages carry no foot) and needs 9, P6 has 11 and needs 5, and each of the two forms of the foot stands on fewer pages than that. Five of these blocks (P5 3, P6 2) are a foot **fused to a panel letter or panel word at the top of the next page** by the page-break continuation (`continuesAcrossPage`).

None of the F blocks is in a figure box, so no figure-box rule and no figure-text rule touches them.
