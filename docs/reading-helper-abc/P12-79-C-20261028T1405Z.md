## 2. The rule as built

**Mechanism (confirmed in the code, as A named it).** `ResponsiveMargin` registers the bottom band (`presentation: "bottom"`, left 8) whenever `useSpread()` is false, that is whenever the window is narrower than `railMinimumWidth(readingScale)` (852 px at step 1.00, 900 at 1.10, 1,005 at 1.32, 1,138 at 1.60). `NotesButton` stood the tab "beside" a card whenever the window was not under md, so from 768 px up to that width, and at the larger steps beyond it, the band's left (8) became the tab's `left: 8px; transform: translateX(-100%)`, x −7 to 8. C P12-77 had limited the band rule to a phone (`phone && standing && presentation === "bottom"`).

**The rule is the band's, not the width's** (`notes-button.tsx`, in `NotesButton` and `EDGE_TAB_BAND`):
- `active = variant === "edge" && (standing || shown)` (a card standing, or the notes window shown from the tab, which registers in the stack one frame later); `banded = active && rect?.presentation === "bottom"`.
- `beside = active && !phone && !banded`: a card (or the window) in a registered bottom band is "no side margin", at every width. A side margin (the rail, `presentation` unset) places the tab beside the card as it always did.
- `bandTop = banded ? rect?.top : undefined`: written as `--tab-band-top` on the tab, so the tab's `top` clamp of P12-77 (`min(…, --tab-band − 8px − length)`) ends its bottom exactly 8 px above the band's top.
- `EDGE_TAB_BAND` loses `md:[--tab-band:100vh]`, the class that switched the band off from md (it is the second half of the defect: without it the clamp would not have been read between 768 and the rail's minimum). With no band written `--tab-band` is `100vh`, which clamps nothing, so the rail, a window with no card and the phone without a card are placed as before.
- The least `top` is unchanged: the masthead's foot (3rem) from md, 0 under it.
- Comments: a P12-79 paragraph in the header, the `EDGE_TAB_BAND` doc and the `bandTop` prop doc say the rule is the band's.
