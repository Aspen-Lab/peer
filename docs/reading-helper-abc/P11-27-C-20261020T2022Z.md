## The rules as built

**1. The buttons never leave the margin (X-1; amends §1h.113 (b) O-2).** `selection-toolbar.tsx`: `actionsPlace({ bounds, viewport, margin, sizes })` — with a margin registered the group is always the column at `margin.left + 8`, `top = placeInMargin(margin, first line, height)`; no `cards`, no `mode`, no slack, no hysteresis (`CARD_SLACK_PX`, `MODE_HYSTERESIS_PX` are gone). Without a margin (under 1024 px) it is the pill above the selection, unchanged; under 768 px nothing, unchanged. The group is `fixed z-[70]` (over the stack's cards, `STACK_Z_BASE` 60 + their number, under the dialogs' z-[80]), on its own surface (`bg-surface`, `border-border-strong`, `shadow-card-hover`: the theme's tokens, opaque). New: `data-over-card` says when its rect overlaps an open card (it keeps asking for the cards through `OPEN_CARD_SELECTOR`, which `margin-card.markers.test.tsx` pins; the measurement reads it). `highlight-strip-host.tsx`: `stripStanding({ mark, box?, viewport, margin })` is the same rule for the strip (`StripMode` is `"margin"` only; `STRIP_ABOVE_WIDTH_PX` and the stack subscription are gone: a card opening moves nothing).

**2. A selection in a caption or a heading is offered its actions (X-4).** The anchor: a **stable id per heading and per caption in the shape every anchor already has**, `paper-section-<k>-p<n>`, where n counts the section's blocks — its paragraphs (0 .. P − 1), its heading (P), its captions in the order the section holds its figures (P + 1 + k) — `lib/notes/anchor-blocks.ts` (`blockAt`, `anchorBlocks`, `captionText`, `parseAnchorId`). Because the shape is the paragraph's, `selectionTarget`, `stickyAnchorId`, `canHighlight`, `highlightSelection`, `passageFromHash` (the notes page's `#hash`), `goToPassage` (`getElementById`) and the saved reading place take the new ids as they are; **`sticky-note.tsx` is not touched.** Like a paragraph's id it is positional: a re-read that adds a paragraph moves the heading's id on, and `resolveAnchors` (now over a section's blocks, `findQuote` too) finds the note again by its quote. The words of a block are what the page draws: a heading's; a caption's is label · words (· page of the PDF where the picture is not drawn). `paper-body.tsx`: the `h3` carries its id and `data-anchor-block="heading"`, the `figure` its id and `="caption"` (the picture's button is no part of the caption: the words are the `figcaption`'s), both `scroll-mt-20`; `readSelection` finds them (`blockOf`; the target says `block: "heading" | "caption"`, paragraphs say nothing); marks, `[n]` tag and highlight colour are drawn in them through the paragraph's own code (`NotedText`; the figure's label keeps its quiet face inside a mark and takes the highlight's ink inside a highlight); a selection across two blocks, or from the caption into its picture, is still nothing. The notes store reads the words of a block from the body `PaperBody` has on show (`setBodyOnShow`, memory only) when its caller gives no paragraph text — the page's `useTakeNote` and `useHighlight` know only `paragraphs[n]`. `highlight.tsx`: one guard removed (it returned before the store could read the words). `note-tags.tsx`: the lost check's body type holds headings and captions. **Explain this: not offered for them, and it cannot be.** The explain route finds the passage by `locatePassage(doc, passage, hint)` in `readableSections(doc)[k].paragraphs` only (`lib/papers/explain.ts`); a heading or a caption is in no paragraph, so the route answers 422 `not_in_paper` (the card then says "Select text from the paper itself."). The toolbar therefore hides it (`explainable = canExplain && target?.block === undefined`); `e` is not mine (see "For the manager").

**3. The strip leaves when its mark leaves the window (§1h.123).** `stripStanding` returns null as soon as no part of the mark is inside the window: its box ends at or above the masthead's foot (the margin band's top) or starts at or below the window's foot — the bounds the selection's actions are hidden by. The whole mark counts (`markBox`: the first piece's first line to the last line of the highlight's own mark), so a mark whose first line has scrolled out and whose later line has not keeps its strip, held at the band's top. When it is out the host hides it and the store forgets it; a click on the mark brings it back, as before; Escape, a press elsewhere, a new selection and a press on the strip are `highlight-strip.tsx`'s and untouched. `AWAY_SCREENS` is retired.

## For the manager

1. **`e` on a caption or a heading** opens the Explain card with "Select text from the paper itself." (with a key; the route cannot find the passage). The button is hidden by the toolbar; the key and the card are `explain-box.tsx` / `reader-keys.ts` (C P11-26's, not mine). A guard on `selection.block !== undefined` in `ExplainBox`'s `offered`/`start` would make `e` inert there.
2. **Over a card the group covers the card's own controls** while a selection stands: at a first line level with the card's top it covers the Explain card's accent X (top-left), and at 330 on the figure card its zoom bar (the "−", "100 %" buttons; in the all-three shot). The ruling says over the card; it goes with the selection. If it should dodge a card's header, that is a nudge within the ±8 px.
3. **z-order:** the group and the strip are `z-[70]`; the stack's cards are `STACK_Z_BASE` (60) + their number (`windows.ts`, C P11-26's), so the 11th open window is at 70 and a 12th over the group. The strip's own class is in `highlight-strip.tsx` (not listed as mine). Ten stacked windows is far from a reader's day; flagged, not fixed.
4. **The registry (`setBodyOnShow`)** exists only because `useTakeNote` (`sticky-note.tsx`, C P11-20/23's) looks the text up as `body[k].paragraphs[n]`. If that line ever takes `blockAt(body[k], n)` the registry can go; the store's fallback is two lines.
5. **Unlisted files touched:** `lib/notes/anchor-blocks.ts` (new) and `highlight.tsx` (one guard removed) — nobody else's file this round. `note-tags.tsx` changed only a type.
6. **The id of a heading or a caption is positional**, like a paragraph's: a re-read with another paragraph count moves it, and the note finds its words again by its quote (tested). A heading whose section has no paragraph is `p0`.
7. **The base 1093 × 614 clamp** (first line at y 520 → group top 486) is the band's foot, as before; not a regression.
8. **Harness:** my first invented PDF was too short to scroll a mark out both ways, so the final numbers (before and after) are all on the longer one; nothing of the owner's was used. The dev server is stopped (process groups 6051 and 27560 by `kill -- -<pgid>`); the throwaway base worktree used for the base counts is removed.
