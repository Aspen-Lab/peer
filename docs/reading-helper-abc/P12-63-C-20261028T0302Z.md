## 2. The rules as built

**Rule 2 (`reading-partition.ts`, `echoOf`).** `closingPartition(map, body, abstract)` keeps its signature. The words the closing must echo are the abstract's long words (7+ letters) when it has any; else the long words of **the last paragraph of the setup part**: the last row of bucket `introduction` before the row being divided that holds a paragraph, or, where the paper names no introduction, the last row of role `setup` there (a related-work part, say). A part after the row is not read; an introduction is preferred to a related-work part that follows it; a row with no paragraph is passed over. Neither gives a word → no split, as before. The three numbers (4 paragraphs, 20 %, 7 letters), the cue, the figure exclusion and every guard are unchanged. A front text that A1 made the introduction is the setup part too.

**Rule 1 (`page.tsx`).** One memo, `overlayPartition = paragraphPartition ?? (reading?.map ? closingPartition(reading.map, reading.body, reading.abstract.sentences.join(" ")) : null)`, between the guide hook and `shownMap`; `partitionedMap` and `withPartitionMarks` read it in place of `paragraphPartition`. The model's partition wins; the abstract is the reading's own (`reading.abstract.sentences`, the text the page already hands `leadCaption`). The change is the two memos' input and the new memo; nothing after `route` is touched (C P12-53 adds its overlay there).

**Rule 3 (`reading-map.tsx`).** `isPeersPart(row)` = the row has a `part` record and it is not `closing`. The role cell reads `isPeersPart(row) ? MAP.parts.tag : roleLabel(row.role)`, and the legend line (`partShown`) shows while an `isPeersPart` row is on screen. Rows the rules worked out (A1–A5) are plain map rows and were already drawn by their role; a test now pins that. Links, anchors, lines, gists, counts and the open-row carry are as they were.

**Rule 4 (`paper-body.tsx`, `reading-roles.ts`).** `isPlaceholderHeading(heading)` (the extractors' "Body", or empty) is exported from `reading-roles.ts` — the one place that knows the placeholder, now shared by A1 and the body. `Section` prints no `<h3>` when `index === 0 && isPlaceholderHeading(section.heading)`. The section keeps `id="paper-section-0"`, its paragraphs and their anchors; no heading block exists for a heading that is not printed. Only the first section; a later section with the placeholder keeps it. The map's row is untouched ("Introduction", setup, by A1).

**Rule 5 (`reading-roles.ts`, A5).** In `inferRoles`, for a top-level row with role `body` after the anchor: if its **direct** sub-rows that name a role (role ≠ `body`) all name **one** role, the row takes it, before A3's continuation, A2's back-matter and evidence branches (`return` after the patch). Unnamed sub-rows say nothing; two roles → A2/A3 read the part as before; a named part is never touched. Then A4 gives the part's unnamed sub-rows its role (the patch counts as "worked out"). The part does not become an A2 boundary or an A3 continuation (it is not a *named* row). Like A1–A4, nothing happens in a paper with no anchored introduction, nor above it.

**Rule 6.** Named rows, the model partition's own drawing and the route are unchanged: the model-partition map and HTML are byte-identical before and after on the invented trunk paper (§6 b).
