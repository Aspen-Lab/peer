## The manager's reading (binding on every brief until the owner rules otherwise)

The reports are real all the same: the owner is the acceptance above the rubric (reviewing-cycle, "Ending"). **They are investigated first (a B each, or one B for the shared cause), never argued with.** The one hypothesis that explains all three at once, to be tested first: **the owner's window is narrower than 1024 css px** (a 1280-px screen at 125 % browser zoom is 1024 css px; at 150 % it is 853) — below 1024 the paper page is the one-column page (`reader-layout.tsx`, `reader-shell.tsx`; BACKLOG-111), which has **no panes, no dividers, no margin buttons, and parks an opened figure instead of showing it** (`lib/reader/figure-viewer.ts`, P11-41). Second: the dividers exist but are **invisible at rest** (a hairline of 1.26 : 1 contrast, no grip, the cursor hint only on hover — recorded as STILL in every P11 walk). Third: the deployment the owner used served an older `main`. The B measures on the **production build** (`next build && next start`) and on the deployed site, at 1280 × 720, 1366 × 768, 1440 × 900 and 1920 × 1080, at 100 / 110 / 125 / 150 % zoom, with the owner's own PDF when the owner supplies it and with the public papers meanwhile.

1. **The small note's editing tools (sticky note).** `components/notes/sticky-note.tsx` (+ `note-editor.tsx`, `wysiwyg-field.tsx`, `note-toolbar.tsx`). The sticky card gets the same formatting row the notes window has (`notes-window.tsx`: bold / italic / underline, …) **plus a bullet-list control, an indent control and an outdent control** ("行进行退"). Tab / Shift+Tab inside a list item indent / outdent; Enter in an empty bullet leaves the list. The structure is kept in the note's record and in **every export** (Markdown `- ` with two-space nesting; Obsidian; the notes page; the paper's "Open your notes") — PRODUCT_DIRECTION: portable output. Every control is a labelled button, keyboard-reachable, at every reading-scale step, light and dark. Nothing per-user beyond the note itself.

2. **The note pad's blue square.** The small square drawn at a note row (find it: `components/notes/highlight-row.tsx`, `note-editor.tsx`, `app/globals.css` — search "square") is **aligned to the first line of the row's text** (its line box: top of the square = top of the first line's content area, or vertically centred on that line — measure which reads as "aligned" at every step) and **its side equals the text's font size** (1em of that text), at every reading-scale step, on the notes page and in the notes window, light and dark; it scales with the text and never with the row's height.

3. **The three panes' ratio drag "did not appear".** Investigate first (above). If the cause is the width: the manager rules — lower the breakpoint, or give the one-column page the panes (BACKLOG-111) — and tells the owner the trade-off (a column of fewer than ~60 characters per line is the floor the product keeps). If the cause is discoverability: a visible divider at rest (a grip or a 2-px rule at ≥ 3 : 1 contrast), `cursor: col-resize` over the whole hit strip, a hover / focus highlight, and the keys the dividers already have (P11-29) kept. If the cause is the deployment: say so to the owner with the commit the site served.
