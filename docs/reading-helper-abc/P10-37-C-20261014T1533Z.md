- **Rule 2.** `ABBREVIATION_END` (`et al.`, `e.g.`, `i.e.`, `Fig.`, `Eq.`, `Ref.`, a single capital initial, then any closing quote or bracket) makes a full stop at a page's foot not a sentence end in `continuesAcrossPage`; the next page's lower-case, digit or bracket opener goes on in the same paragraph. Accepted trade-off: a sentence that ends in a capital letter ("vitamin C.") followed by a lower-case opener now joins.
