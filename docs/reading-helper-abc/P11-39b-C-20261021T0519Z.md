## 2. What the step does, and which element

`answers-block` is one `<section>` holding every answer, so after the first checkpoint the card was level with the section's first line: 112 px above a selection in the first answer at 1440 × 900, 207.8 above the second answer's, 303 above the third's (the pre-step column below); a Summary block with several paragraphs had the same gap (37 px for a line under its label, 70 to 352 px for the lines below, the largest a caption low in a tall block whose first line had scrolled under the masthead). For a selection in the answers or in a Summary block `levelTopOf` now finds **the paragraph that holds the selection's start** and is level with that paragraph's first drawn line, held for the 250-px floor by `placeInMargin` as before (`explain-box.tsx` only).

* **The element chosen: the nearest block-level ancestor, inside the block, of the element at the selection's first line.** Block-level = computed `display` not `inline`, `contents`, `none` or `ruby` — the same test `block-marks.ts`'s `domTextEnv` and `anchor-blocks.ts`'s text model use for "a line of its own". In this DOM that is an answer's text `<p>` (not the `<li>` around it: the `<li>` also holds the evidence quote), the evidence quote's own `<p>` (a selection that starts in the paper's sentence is level with that sentence's paragraph, not with the answer's claim above it), the question's `<p>`, a "Read next" line's `<p>`; in a Summary block a `<p>`, a **block `<span>`** (the Summary draws its lead lines as `span.font-reading.text-lead`, which a `p, li` tag list would have missed and left at the block's first line), a `<li>`, a `figcaption`. It is the owner's "selected paragraph" read from the page, and it does not depend on which tag the Summary's components happen to use.
* **How it is found.** `document.elementFromPoint` at the left edge of the selection's box (`where.bounds`, measured again on every scroll frame), a pixel or two under the top of its first line: the paragraph its START is in, whatever lines it runs on over (a selection on an answer's later line, or one that runs on into the next answer, is level with the paragraph it started in). It is read once for a selection and kept (a `WeakMap` keyed by the selection object), because the range is not at hand, a click in the card collapses the selection, and the page scrolls under it; the card then follows that paragraph. A kept paragraph that has left the page, a point with nothing of the block at it, a paragraph of another block there, and a page without `elementFromPoint` or `getComputedStyle` are no paragraph: the block's first line, as in the first checkpoint. A body paragraph's, a caption's and a heading's selection is untouched.
