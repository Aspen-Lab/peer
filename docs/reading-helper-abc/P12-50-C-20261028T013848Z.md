## The rules as built

1. **Narrow gutter.** `gutterOf` first runs exactly the old search (a 2 em band slid a point at a time across 35 %–65 % of the text's width; the result is bit-for-bit what it was). Only when that finds nothing, a **1 em** band whose **centre lies in the middle third** of the text's width (33.3 %–66.7 %) is tried with the same acceptance: clear over at least 8 consecutive rows (`GUTTER_MIN_ROWS`), text on both sides in at least 8 rows, each side at least 30 % of the width, the median line filling 60 % of its side. The page still needs 16 baselines. The narrow band is tried at every place an item ends inside the window (a clear band slid left stops at an item's end, so these are all the bands there are) rather than a point at a time: a one-point slide steps over a gutter 1.05 em wide about half the time. `a` and `b` are the real edges of the text, as before.
2. **The prior by position.** `gutterPrior` collects each page's own gutter, then tries every one as the centre of a group: the pages whose right-column edge `b` stands within `max(6 pt, 0.5 em)` of it (`em` = that page's usual text height; for a pair, the larger of the two tolerances). The group with the most pages wins (the first on a tie); fewer than two pages is no prior. The prior is `a` = the smallest left edge of the group (as before: the left edge moves with an overhang), `b` = the median right edge. The rounding buckets (`Math.round(b / 3)`) are gone.
3. Nothing else changes: line assembly within a column, captions, headings, sections, `crossesGutter`, the reading order.
