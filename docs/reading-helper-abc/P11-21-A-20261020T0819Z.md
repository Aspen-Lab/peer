## For the manager

**Defects by mechanism**

1. **The fixed zone's cap is smaller than a briefing paper's fixed content (A7; BACKLOG-100, larger than reported).** Mechanism: `[data-reader-left-fixed]` is capped at 62 % of the pane body (`overflow-y: auto`) and holds, in order, meta line, title (clamped to 4 lines), byline, "and N more", lead affiliation, the command rows (a public paper has four: Read it here, Open the PDF, Save / Skip / Copy, Upload full article PDF), the strip, and a status line or bar while a report runs. C P11-17c measured only the byline (+117 px) on an upload, and an upload has one command row fewer. The budget on `pub` at 1366 x 768: header 287 + commands 184 + strip 46 = 549 against 409; at 1280 x 720 the cut is 170, at 1093 x 614 195; `pub2` (shorter) is still 79 to 135 over from 1366 x 768 down. C P11-20 rule 2 as briefed (byline and affiliation to one line each) frees about 50 px: it will not close this. The tab strip is wholly invisible at 1440 x 900 (the owner's laptop) on a long-title paper and at 1366 x 768 on a short one, so a reader cannot see that Summary exists. **Item:** a C (P11-23) with the budget above as its test (zone never scrolls inside itself, on `pub` and `pub2`, at 1280 x 720, 1366 x 768, 1440 x 900, 1093 x 614, 1024 x 768, report running and settled). Ways to get the room, for the owner to choose: put the strip before the byline; move the byline, the affiliation and the "Upload full article PDF" row into the scroll zone's details (with the DOI and the retention line); fold the second command row into one row of icons below ~800 px high; or let the whole pane scroll as one (`data-mode="whole"`) below a height. The harness cannot show a briefing paper without a layer like mine: see 4.
2. **The sticky pane stops about 8 px short at the foot of the page (X-A1).** Mechanism: P10-21's `margin-bottom: -0.75rem` lets the pane end 12 px below its holder; the holder ends 8 px earlier than that. **Item:** a one-line C (a larger negative margin or the cell's padding), pinned by measuring the title's y at a mid scroll and at the end.
3. **The map's meta wraps with a dot opening the line (X-A2).** Mechanism: the pane's inner width is 284 px at its 320 default (side padding 8 + 16), the old panel's text column was about 320; the meta parts are `whitespace-nowrap` spans with dot separators, so a wrap leaves "· 11 min" on the second line, and at the 280 first-visit width at 1024 all 14 rows break. **Item:** a small C: lay the meta out as two fixed lines in the pane ("role · page" and "words · minutes"), or draw the separator with the part that follows and drop it at a break. The owner may also decide the default left width (360 from 1280 clears every row of this paper; 320 does not).
4. **Harness gap.** The sandbox's home reads "Couldn't reach today's paper sources", so no briefing paper exists for any A. Proposal: commit a public-paper layer (two OpenAlex records and an open-access PDF, as `stub-pub/pubstub.cjs` here) and the partition answer P10 round 6 used (`stub-ext.cjs`) to `docs/reading-helper-abc/harness/`, so BACKLOG-100 and A15's key half can be walked by any reviewer and by C's tests of item 1.
