## The rules as built

**R1, a title size.** `PdfLine.titleSize?` (optional, so hand-built lines in other tests still stand; `titleOf` reads `titleSize ?? size`). `characterSize(runs)` in `lineOf`: each run weighs its characters (white space not counted), sizes within 0.6 pt of each other weigh together, the heaviest wins (the larger on a tie) and is read as the mean of the sizes that weigh with it, rounded to 0.1. A line set in one size reads that size; a 40 pt cap on a line of 9 pt words reads 9; a 24 pt title line with a raised 14 pt mark still reads 24. **A weighted mode, not a weighted mean**: a mean would pull a title line with a smaller raised mark (24 and 14 pt, 3 and 2 characters: 20) out of the 0.6 window that joins the title's lines. `size` is untouched (still the tallest run, round to 0.1). `titleOf` filters page-1 lines above 1.25 times the body by `titleSize`, and a size class made only of rows of one or two letters (a cap alone on its baseline) is never a candidate. The page-1 skip in `buildOutline` (`if (title && line.page === 1 && ... > body.size * 1.25) continue`) compares `titleSize`, so the body line a cap stands on is no longer cut from the text (the manager's addition; "skip only when it is really the title", read as: by the size the title is read at).

**R2, the embedded title as a witness.** `readPages` asks the open pdf.js document for `getMetadata()` (no second parse), takes `info.Title` and the XMP `dc:title`, cleans them (control characters, white space, 400 characters at most, no duplicates) and puts them on `pages[0].embeddedTitles` (absent when there are none; any failure reads as none). `titleOf(lines, body, embedded = [])`: page-1 lines above the bar fall into size classes (anchored at the largest size, 0.6 pt wide, as the old "within 0.6 of the biggest" was). A witness counts when it has 3 or more words, is not a file name (`.pdf`, `.docx` ... anywhere, "Microsoft Word - ...", "Untitled", a single token, two underscores), not a stamp (`STAMP`), and at most 300 characters. A class is eligible when 80% of its words, and at least two, are in the witness; the class with the most words in it wins (the larger size on a tie); inside the class only the lines most of whose words are in the witness are the title (an article-type label at the title's size drops out). The output is the page's own text. With no witness, or none that matches, the first class that is not a masthead, else the largest. A **masthead** is a class that stands over a title block (a class of 3 or more words, below it on the page, set at 70% of its size or more and not itself repeated) and either is a line the running heads repeat (its words contained in a first-or-last-three-rows line of at least two later pages and 40% of them), or has fewer than 3 words and matches no witness. "Not the journal's name" is the same test: a witness that matches only a masthead is ignored. The 70% is a guard I added to the brief: a short title above a smaller authors line, or a title that the running heads repeat above the authors, is not a masthead (two tests fail with the guard off).

**R3, nothing else changes.** `size`, headings, sections and `furnitureOf` are untouched.
