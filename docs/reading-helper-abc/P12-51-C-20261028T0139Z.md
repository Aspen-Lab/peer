## The rules as built (`web/src/lib/papers/pdf-outline.ts`; only `buildOutline`, `titleOf` and helpers I added; `TERMINAL`, `END_MATTER_HEADING`, `FRONT_MATTER_END`, `endsTheArgument` are unchanged)

**O5.** `done` is gone. State in `buildOutline`: `tail` (an end-matter heading has stood), `reading` (the lines under the current heading are read), `resumed` (the current stretch is a part of the argument), `opensTail` (the heading that closed the argument, which stays closed if a wrapped second line joins it), `appendix`.
- The **first** end-matter heading still closes the argument and it, and the text under it, stay out (P7-18; every existing "ends the argument and takes what follows with it" case holds).
- After it, `tailPartOf(heading, canonical, ends)` names each heading: **note** (another end-matter part: `endsTheArgument` true and not bulky, or the `acknowledgments` bucket on a plain heading) opens its own section and its text is read, filed under `acknowledgments` when no bucket names it ("ASSOCIATED CONTENT"); **argument** (a plain heading whose bucket is introduction / related_work / methods / results / discussion / conclusion / limitations) opens its own section, and the sub-headings after it are read as the body's are; **out** (the references) and **appendix** (an `Appendix` / `Supplementary material` heading, or a heading numbered by a letter or a numeral, "B Additional Results", "C.1 Setup") read nothing, and after an appendix no heading opens an argument part again; any other heading reads what its surroundings read (sub-headings of Methods) and, in the notes' company, nothing (the affiliation blocks, a reference entry the page set as a heading).
- "Plain" heading: at most 8 words, no comma, no year, so a reference line that holds the word "method" does not open a section.
- The empty-numbered-group-heading rule in `flush` now reads `reading` where it read `!done` ("never one whose text is not read").

**O6.** When no heading names an abstract or an introduction (`firstNamed < 0`), `openingStart` finds the paper's opening by style: the first run of at least 3 lines, on the first page with text, in the body's size (within 0.6 pt) and face, not a heading and not a caption (furniture, margin print and the dated box are not lines), read back over a drop cap or a mark of two characters or fewer. The lines above it are the cover and are cut (`read = lines.slice(start)`); the opening is then ordinary text before the first heading, emitted by `flush` as **the first `OutlineSection` with `heading` undefined and `canonical` `"body"`, `page` the page of its first line** — the interface C P12-48's rule A1 reads. Nothing is cut when the line above the run is a heading (one or two lines of heading: a real section heading over the first prose; three or more heading lines in a row are a summary paragraph and are cover), when the run is the first thing on the page, or when there is no such run. A paper that names its introduction (`firstNamed > 0`) is not touched: tested byte-identical. `titleOf` ignores a line of two characters or fewer (a drop cap is the largest type on the page and left the paper with no title).

**O8.** `figureTextOf` returns the indices of figure text: a run of 3 or more consecutive lines on one page (furniture, margin print and the dated box are transparent), each at most 25 characters, not ending in sentence punctuation, not a caption or a numbered equation, and either free of any word of 4+ letters or set smaller than 0.95 of the body; the run counts as beside a caption when one of its lines is within 8 body sizes of a caption's first line on the same page. A caption and the lines `continuesCaption` runs it on to are the caption's and never figure text. Those lines are skipped before the caption and heading tests (never a heading, not in `held`, so they no longer fold into a false equation) and in the `lastNumbered` scan.
