## The rule as built (`keep-selection.ts`)
1. **A triple click is counted by the keeper** (pointer events carry no click count; `detail` is 0): a mouse press within `MULTI_CLICK_MS` (1,000 ms) and `MULTI_CLICK_PX` (10 px) of the last, the third in a row, in the text, without Shift, with a `timeStamp`. Three presses a second apart or 10 px apart, a double click, touch and pen, a press outside the text, a Shift+press: not a triple click.
2. **The paragraph is read before the browser moves it**: at the held press's first move (capture phase, before the browser's own drag move) or at its release, `domEnv.paragraph()` reads the standing selection as a `Span` (start, end in document order); null for a collapsed selection, one that began outside the column, or a text field's.
3. **`endParagraphsInColumn`** (new, exported; `endSelectionInColumn` is untouched): the same nearest point and rows as the single press (the column's edge at the pointer's height, the first or last visible line, the legend cleared six pixels at a time), but the paragraph at that point is found with the browser's own `Selection.modify("move", "backward", "paragraphboundary")` then `modify("extend", "forward", ...)`, and the selection is **set whole** from the base: the base's start through the later of the base's end and that paragraph's end, or, for a paragraph wholly before the base, that paragraph's start from the base's end. The triple-clicked paragraph is never left out, and where the browser has taken the selection since (the pane, a collapsed caret, cleared) does not matter. A page with no `modify`: the single press's character correction.
4. **Engaged**: setting a selection by hand ends the browser's paragraph granularity for the rest of the press (measured: a drag taken over the pane and back over the column ended at the pointer's character, 112 to 122 characters). Once a correction has been made for the held triple click, `domEnv` follows a pointer back over the column too, at its own place (`engaged` argument; reset when the next triple click's paragraph is read). A drag that never leaves the column stays the browser's.
5. Unchanged: a single press, a double click, a plain triple click, Shift+click (P11-57), a release over the legend (P11-62), the separators, the notes tab, touch and pen. The source scan test (no `mouse*`/`selectionchange`/`preventDefault` in the file, four listeners exactly) still passes: no listener was added.

## Unsettled, for the manager
- **Chromium only.** `Selection.modify` with `paragraphboundary` exists in Firefox and Safari, but only Chromium was run here.
- **The triple click is inferred**, by timing (1,000 ms, 10 px), because pointer events carry no count and the file may not add a `mousedown` listener (a surviving test pins the four listeners exactly). A system whose double-click interval is longer than a second is not followed as a triple click: it gets today's behaviour, not a worse one. The count is only a trigger: the selection read at the first move must be non-collapsed and in the column, so three fast clicks that selected nothing do nothing.
- **A later paragraph's end** is the paragraph's last position, not the next paragraph's first position as the browser has it: 2 characters shorter in selections that span two paragraphs (no button either way). The same-paragraph case keeps the browser's own base end exactly.
- **A held drag below the window** autoscrolls the page; the paragraph under the pointer changes while it runs. That is the browser's and was the same on the base; the correction follows the pointer's place at each frame.
- The double-click-and-drag was not changed (rule 2) and was measured clean at all five windows. It shares the granularity mechanism (a word), so a drag that goes over the pane and back inside a double click's word granularity is the browser's as before; not measured beyond the 120 gestures above.
