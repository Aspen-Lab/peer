Between that edge and the text's right edge is the shell's **gutter**: the centre's side padding (12 px, `.reader-centre`) and the divider (12 px, `DIVIDER_PX`). The tab was 25.09 px wide (1 px border + 4 + one 16.1 px line of the `eyebrow` face + 4):

## The rule as built

**The tab beside a card is 15 px wide and keeps a 24 px hit area.** In `notes-button.tsx`, when a card stands and a margin is registered (`squeezed`):
- the face: `style={{ left: marginLeft, transform: "translateX(-100%)", width: EDGE_TAB_BESIDE_PX /* 15 */, lineHeight: 1 }}` and `py-0` in place of `py-1`. 15 = 24 (the least gutter) − 8 (clear) − 1 (the tab's `shadow-card` frame, which paints 1 px outside its box). The tab's box is 9 px from the text, its frame's outer edge 8. Its right edge is still the margin's left edge, so it never stands over a card. The label is the same 11.5 px `eyebrow` face, its line set solid (11.5 px) in a 14 px content box.
- the hit area: a transparent strip on the text's side, `before:absolute before:top-0 before:-left-[10px] before:h-full before:w-[9px]` (the offset is the 9 px width plus the 1 px border, because an absolutely placed child is positioned from the padding edge), so face + strip = `EDGE_TAB_HIT_PX` = 24, ending at the text's right edge in the narrowest gutter. Physical `top` and `h-full`, not `inset-y-0`: in `vertical-rl` the logical block axis is the horizontal one, and `inset-y-0` gave the strip no height (found by a hit test: the strip answered nothing until it was changed).
- Unchanged: the tab with no card (`right-0`, `px-3 py-1`, 25.09 px, no style); beside a card with no margin registered (below 1024 px: `right-[min(360px,100vw)]`); the `row` variant; the label, the title, `aria-expanded`, the press; `top`, `z-40`.

**The text column is not touched.** Its rect (text right edge 720 / 760 / 896 / 1023 / 1283 in the grid, before and after), its width and its line breaks are what they were; no file of the column was edited.

### Why this means, and why the others failed (measured at 1024 × 768 unless said)

- **Riding inside the card's left edge** (inside or on its border). 24 across and 8 clear needs 32 px from the text's edge, the gutter is 24, so the tab would stand 8 px inside the card, over the card's own controls: a sticky's and a figure's header button begin **1 px** inside the card (a 40 px row, anywhere in the band: the explain card stands level with its selection), the figure's picture and its pan / zoom button begin **6.6 px** inside, the explain card's close button 17, the notes window's toolbar 13. It would also need a z above the cards (60 to 69). Kept clear of the figure's picture (6.6 px in) the tab can go no more than 5.5 px inside: 16 + 5.5 = 21.5 px across, under the 24 floor, and still over the header rows. Failed.
- **A narrower tab alone.** 15 px across is under the 24 px floor. The strip is what gives the floor back.
- **An offset that uses the gutter's real width.** At the three defect windows the real width is 24 and the column cannot move (rule 2), so there is nothing to offset into. Where the gutter is wide (1440: 57, 1920: 277) the old tab was already clear.
- **A tab that dodges the cards** (stays on the window's edge where no card is level with it). At 1024 × 768 the notes window alone ends at y 422.6, above the tab's 432, so it would work there — but the explain card follows its selection down the page (`useWindows.follow`), the sticky stands level with its selection, and the figure card and the notes window stacked with it fill the whole band (48 to 728): the tab would jump as the reader scrolls, and in the full-band states it has no place at all. Failed.
- **A width read from the page** (a `ResizeObserver` on the centre column's inner box, the tab keeping 25 px where the gutter is wide). It would keep the old look at 1440 and up (the tab is 15 px there too now, 42 px clear at 1440). It adds a DOM read, two observers and a state loop to a presentational view for no defect that can be measured; not done. See "For the manager" 2.

## For the manager

1. **The one judgement: the hit area touches the line ends.** The tab's box and its frame are 9 and 8 px clear of the text (rule 1, as the brief writes it: "its rect"), and the hit area is 24 across (rule 1's floor), but the hit area is the face plus a transparent 9 px strip, and the strip starts exactly at the text's right edge: **0 px clear** at 1024, 1093 and 1280. A hit area that is itself 8 px clear of the text cannot be 24 across at a 24 px gutter without standing 8 px inside the cards (it needs 32 px; the cards' header buttons begin 1 px in and the figure's picture 6.6): by that strict reading rule 4 applies at those three windows. I read "rect" as the visible tab and the floor as the clickable area, and built to that. If you read it strictly, the choices are (a) drop the strip — delete the `before:` classes and the hit-area tests; the hit area is then 15 across, under the floor; (b) leave it; (c) widen the gutter, which is the column's / `.reader-centre`'s file, not this one; (d) inset the cards' left edge by 8 px (`margin-card.tsx`, not this one), which would give the tab 8 more px of its own. Say which.
2. **The tab is 15 px beside a card at every window**, including 1440 and 1920 where the gutter (57, 277) would take the old 25 px. It is sized for the narrowest gutter the shell can have (one number, no DOM read). A tab sized from the measured text edge would keep 25 px at 1440 and up; I did not build it (see the means above). It is a look, not a defect.
3. **Merge note for the other C's `.reader-centre` work.** `notes-button.clear.test.tsx` and its flow test read `.reader-centre`'s `padding-inline` off `globals.css` and `DIVIDER_PX` off `pane-layout.ts`, and `EDGE_TAB_BESIDE_PX` is `gutter − 8 − 1`. If that C changes the centre's side padding or the divider's width, these go red together; the fix is the one constant (and the strip's 9 and 10 px, which are `24 − 15` and that plus the border), not the tests.
4. **Below 1024 px** (no margin registered) the tab beside a card is where it was, the card's 360 px from the right edge: the cards stand over the page's text there and there is no gutter to keep clear; not among the windows measured.
5. **Keyboard focus.** The global ring (2 px, offset 3 px) is drawn 5 px outside the box, so on a keyboard focus it comes to 4 px from the text. It is transient, and the base's ring was over the text.
6. **The key.** The Explain action stands only with a key, so the explain card's measurement needed the stub's placeholder key in a throwaway browser profile, as every round's harness has. No key value, no per-user data and no model is in any commit or in this file.
7. **Not measured:** touch, a window narrower than 1024 px, a divider dragged to its extremes (the arithmetic holds there, by the tests: the gutter does not depend on the pane widths), A / A text sizes (the gutter does not depend on them either: it is padding and the divider).
