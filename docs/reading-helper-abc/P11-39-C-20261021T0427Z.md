## The defect, the rule, the fix

`levelTopOf` (`explain-box.tsx`) returned `DOCK_TOP_PX` (the masthead's foot, P10-08) for a selection with no body paragraph, so a Summary block's or an answer's Explain card stood at 48 px whatever the selection's line: 206 px (the Summary's first block) and 245 px (an answer) above the selection at 1440 × 900, and for a block lower in the Summary a card (48 to 403) that no longer spanned the selection.

**Rule 1 (ruling §1h.138 (b); the owner's P9 words, §1h.49 (a); §1h.128):** a selection in a Summary block, an answer, a figure caption or a section heading that offers Explain this opens the card level (±8 px) with the first line of the block it is in, as a body paragraph's: before and after the answer lands; where the room under it is less than the card's 250-px floor the top rises just enough (`placeInMargin`); the answer scrolls inside. **Rule 2:** a body paragraph's card is byte for byte as today.

**How the block is found (from the selection's anchor).** A selection in a Summary block or an answer reports `paragraphId` — the id of the block it is in, which `placeOf` (`paper-body.tsx`) takes from the block's element: `summary-…` (`paper-words.tsx`'s `SUMMARY_ID`, C P11-17b) or `answers-block` (`for-your-questions.tsx`'s section). `levelTopOf` gates that id with `isPageBlockId` (`anchor-blocks.ts`, C P11-27/28: read, not changed), takes the element with `document.getElementById` (as `highlight.tsx` and the notes' squares do), and reads its **first line**: the top of the first text node with words that draws (a `Range` over it, `getClientRects`; white space and a node that draws nothing are passed over; at most 64 text nodes, so a block hidden whole costs a bounded walk). The block's *box* is not used: on this paper its words stand 56 px below the box's top for "What they found", 16 for the abstract, 8 for "Where it is thin", and 4 to 37 for the skim block (4 with the answers on the page, 37 without: `x01-blocks-s1440-light.json`), the others 0 — outside ±8 for three of the seven blocks. It is read from the page at each render the box already makes for a standing card (the scroll and resize frames of its selection watcher), so the card follows its block; no new effect and no new state (`explain-box.effects.test.ts` still holds). Where no line can be read — the bare kind a block with no id of its own is reported under (`"summary"`, `"answers"`), no such element, no word that draws, no document — the card is level with the selection's own first line (`undefined`: `placePanel`'s and the box's own fallback), never the masthead's foot. The body's paragraph (and, P11-27, a heading's or a caption's own element, where the body reports its box as `paragraph`) goes the way it went: `where.paragraph?.top`.

**A defect in my first fix, found by looking at a picture.** `3cfaf6cc` skipped text inside an `aria-hidden` element, meant for the answers' caret ("▸"). `ScrambleText` draws a fresh report's lines **inside** an `aria-hidden` span (`<span aria-label>…<span aria-hidden>{words}</span></span>`), the Summary's skim line among them, so for the Summary's first block the card stood at the italic evidence quote under the skim line (254.5) and not at the skim line (221.2): 33 px, outside ±8. My harness read "the first line" with the same definition as the code, so every reading agreed; the picture (`B3L-summary-skim-y300-1440x900-light-after.png`) did not. `22f259fc` takes every drawn word, `aria-hidden` or not (the caret is part of the line it stands in; when the answers' panel is open its rotated box is 4.6 px below the label's top, inside ±8); the harness's reference line is now the **topmost drawn text of the block** (every text node with a rect, `aria-hidden` ones included), independent of the code's first-in-document-order reading; and both matrices below were measured again on that reference (the first round's dumps are kept as `dumps-old/`). Re-aimed with it (§1c; tests of mine, committed in 18039f4f / 3cfaf6cc, that had pinned the wrong skip): `explain-box.block.flow.test.tsx` "reads the first line of the block's words: a decoration (aria-hidden) before them…" (now: white space and a node that draws nothing are passed over, a scrambling line inside `aria-hidden` is the line) and the answers' "opens level with the block's first line…" (196, the caret's line, was 200); `explain-box.level.test.ts` "passes over white space, a decoration (aria-hidden) and a node that draws nothing" is split into "passes over white space and a node that draws nothing" and "takes words inside an aria-hidden element for the line" (+1 test).
