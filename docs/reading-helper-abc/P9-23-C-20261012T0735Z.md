## 2. The rules, as built

**1. N2 — the navs keep a gap** (`masthead.tsx`). **Built both halves, and measured which one holds where:**

- `gap-x-4` on the header's grid (`1fr auto 1fr`): 16 px between the three cells wherever they fit. That alone fixes the case the walk measured (a deep-linked upload: the rail is "← Briefing" only): the three need 764 px at 768 (48 padding + 83 wordmark + 16 + 75.9 + 16 + 525, from the measured widths), so the full label would hold at 768 with 4 px to spare.
- **But the rail of a paper opened from the briefing is wider** ("← Briefing · 2 of 5 · k j", 205.5 px), and with it the three need about 894 px. So below `lg` (1024) the Briefing link is its arrow and from `lg` the whole label: two spans in the link, `lg:hidden` "←" and `hidden lg:inline` "← Briefing" (the arrow is taken from `RAIL.back`, so the copy has one source), both `aria-hidden`, and the link carries `aria-label="← Briefing"` — **its accessible name is exactly what it was**. I built **`lg`, not the brief's `xl`**: the measurement does not ask for `xl` — at 1024 with the label the widest rail leaves 146.4 px between the wordmark and the rail (130 over the gap), at 1279 401 px; `xl` would drop a label that fits across 1024–1279. (Font fallback in this dev environment is the only uncertainty; 130 px is 25 % of the nav.)
- Where there is no hover the link is a 44 × 44 target (`[@media(hover:none)]:min-h-11 [@media(hover:none)]:min-w-11`, the shell's rule), as the arrow alone would be 7.6 px wide.
- **Commit 3, not in the brief:** the arrow alone is 7.6 × 16 px to a mouse, so below `lg` an invisible `after` box (`relative after:absolute after:-inset-x-2 after:-inset-y-3 lg:after:hidden`: 8 px each side, the bar's height, ending inside the 16 px gap) is its click area. It takes no room (every gap in §6 is the same with and without it) and is not drawn from `lg`.
- Nothing else in the masthead moves: the header keeps `hidden md:grid sticky top-0 z-50 h-12 px-6 grid-cols-[1fr_auto_1fr] items-center` (pinned), the wordmark, the nav, the rules and the keycaps are untouched.
