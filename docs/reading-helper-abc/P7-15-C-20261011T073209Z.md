## 1. The rule as built

**Placement (`explain-box.tsx`, `explain-dock.ts`).** `placePanel(bounds, viewport)` (the `column` argument is gone) returns:
- width < 640: `{ kind: "sheet", left: 0, width, bottom: 0, maxHeight: 60% }` — unchanged;
- width 640 … 1023: `{ kind: "card", … placeCard(…) }` — unchanged (P3-02's card under or above the selection; the brief said keep whatever the rule did below 1024, and that was the card, not the sheet);
- width ≥ 1024: `{ kind: "dock", right: 0, width: 360, top: 48, bottom: 28, maxHeight: height − 76 }` — flush with the window's right edge, from under the 48-px masthead (`h-12`) to above the 28-px foot legend (`--legend-h`), as tall as the window less the two, independent of the selection and of the text column. It stands against the edge with `right: 0`, not a `left` computed from `innerWidth`: `innerWidth` counts the scrollbar and a fixed box does not stand under it. `explain-dock.test.ts` pins the 48, the 28 and the 360 against `masthead.tsx`, `globals.css`, `key-legend.tsx` and `placeCard`.
- The card component takes `dock`: `data-explain-dock`, a flex column with a line on its left only (`border-y-0 border-r-0`; the masthead's and the legend's own lines stand above and below it), no shadow, and the controls row `mt-auto` (a short thread keeps the input at the dock's foot — measured: controls bottom = dock bottom at all three widths). The card's and the sheet's classes are unchanged. The thread, "Say more", the search toggle, the Tier 0 definition card (it is the dock too at ≥ 1024) and the `e` key are the same component and unchanged in behaviour.
- `ColumnEdge`, the `column` prop, `measureBodyColumn` and its test file are deleted (no caller was left); the page no longer hands the box the column.

**Making room (`explain-dock.ts`, `explain-box.tsx`, `globals.css`).** While the box stands as the dock (open, window ≥ 1024) the box writes two custom properties on `<html>` and takes them off when it closes, when the window narrows below 1024 (it follows `resize`), and when the reader leaves the paper: `--explain-dock: 376px` (the dock and a 16-px gap) and `--explain-dock-width: 360px`. `globals.css`: `.peer-main-content { padding-right: var(--explain-dock, 0px); }` — the shell's `<main>` is padded by it, so the page container (`mx-auto w-full max-w-…`) is given the room that is left and centres itself in it: the cap applies to the remaining width, the panel's track never changes, the column keeps its width where the cap allows and narrows where it does not. Unset, the padding is 0 on every page.

**Holding the selection (`explain-box.tsx`).** The reflow moves the words. A `useLayoutEffect` keyed on whether the box is docked reads the selection's top, changes the layout (sets or removes the properties), reads the top again and `scrollBy`s the page by the difference — before the next paint, in both directions, and for the NEW selection when a new passage closed the box. A passive effect then reads where the selection is again on the next frame, for the button. Leaving the paper only gives the width back (scrolls nothing). The viewport state no longer re-renders when a scroll frame did not change its size.

**A press outside the dock does not close it** (the card and the sheet still close on one). Escape, Close and a new selection close the dock.

**The notes tab (`notes-button.tsx`).** `right-0` → `right-[var(--explain-dock-width,0px)]`: while the dock is open the tab stands against its left edge (z-40 under the dock's z-70 would hide it); with no dock the offset is 0 and nothing changed.
