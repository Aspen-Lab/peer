## 2. The rules as built

1. **The map under the strip, floating (A8).** `ReaderLayout` takes `map?: ReactNode`: on the spread it is in the panel (`reader-panel`: sticky, window-bounded, `overflow-y: auto`), directly after `tabs` and before `terms`, so it floats with the strip and scrolls inside the panel; in one column it stands between the strip and the tabpanel (no stickiness). It is a slot of its own outside the tabpanel: under both tabs, never remounted by a switch, nothing of it duplicated in the column. The page passes `map={hasBody && reading.map ? <ReadingMapView … reveal={reveal} …/> : undefined}` and the `additions` no longer hold it. Because the map now shows under Summary while the body is under Paper digestion, `ReadingMapView` takes the page's `reveal` and its row and paragraph links go through `followSectionLink` (the same path the § links and the terms use): a row clicked under Summary switches the tab, then jumps. No scale wrapper (the panel is chrome, as the strip is). Summary line, "show map / hide map", the heading band: unchanged.
2. **The extractors keep an empty numbered parent (A10).** `html-text.ts`: `headingNumber` exported, new `numbersHeading(parent, child)` (the child's number starts with the parent's number plus a dot: "2." opens "2.1" and "2.1.1", not "3.1", not "20.1") and `dropStrayHeadings(sections)`; `sectionDrafter` no longer drops an empty section, every one of the four HTML builds (LaTeXML, PMC, bioRxiv in both shapes, generic) runs `dropStrayHeadings` over what it collected before `trimToBudget` and `withSectionIds`, so a heading with no text that numbers the next one stays (empty text, its bucket, numbered in order) and one that numbers nothing is dropped as before; end matter as before (dropped by `shouldKeepSection`). PDF: `flush()` keeps a numbered empty heading outside the end matter (`!done`), `buildOutline` runs `dropStrayHeadings` at its return (and `reason` follows the kept list), and `normalize()` in `pdf-text.ts` runs it instead of the `text.length > 0` filter.
3. **The hierarchy.** `readableSections` keeps a section with no paragraphs and no equations when it numbers the section after it (the body shares it, so the body and the map stay one list). `ReadingMapSection` gains `level?: 1 | 2 | 3` and `parentId?: string` exactly as §1h.78 (d) names them; `readingMapOf` fills both: level 1 for "N.", 2 for "N.M", 3 for "N.M.K" and deeper; **an unnumbered heading is level 1** (my choice: a heading with no number says nothing about depth, and "level 2 after a numbered level-1 parent" would nest "Conclusions" or "Supplementary" under whatever numbered section stands above it; a group row is only ever kept when it numbers its next heading, so an unnumbered heading can never directly follow a group row anyway); `parentId` is the nearest preceding row with a smaller level. New `groupTotals(map)`: for a row with no paragraphs and no words that has rows under it (by `parentId`, nested groups included) the summed words and the minutes **from the summed words** (ceil at 180, the rule of the map's own `totalMinutes` and of every row; see §5 for why not the sum of the children's rounded minutes). The view indents `(level − 1) × 0.75rem` (inline padding on the `li`, as before, with `data-level`; a map built before levels falls back to the depth read off the heading), the group `li` has `data-group`, says heading · role · page · summed words · minutes, has no fold, and shows no "not mentioned" fact line (it has no text to mention anything in); this is the one change in the `fact` ternary of the tint region (`group ? null : …`), nothing else of C P10-06's lines was touched. The rows stay one `<ol>`. The summary line counts the group rows as sections and adds the words over the rows' own words (the group's sum is not in it).
4. **The body shows the group heading alone.** `paper-body.tsx` `Section`: a section with no paragraph, equation or figure renders its `<h3>` (same `data-route` / `markedClass` path, no bottom margin) inside its `<section id=paper-section-k>` anchor and no empty text block. Paragraph indices and the reading order unchanged.
5. **Nothing else changes.** Route, tiers, paragraph lines, gists, openings, touch floors, the Summary tab: untouched (the existing tests for each pass unchanged).

Versions (commit 5): `UPLOAD_CONCEPT_EXTRACTION_VERSION` 9 → 10, `PaperReading.version` 10 → 11 (the shape gains the map's `level` / `parentId` and the body's group sections), `READING_VERSION` in `use-reading.ts` and the four pinned tests (`reading.test.ts`, the reading route test, `use-reading.effects.test.ts`) follow; a new `upload-concepts.test.ts` case pins ≥ 10.
