## The rules as built (`web/src/lib/papers/reading-map.ts`, the route only)

1. **A group row never takes a strong slot.** In `routeQuestion` the leaf rows (rows that are not a group row: `!scored` with children) are graded with `gradeScores` — the cap of three is counted over them — and a group row's tier is the strongest tier of its children (settled bottom-up, so a nested group works): strong if a child is strong, medium if the best child is medium, none if none. The group's score, hits, union and evidence are as before (`gradeScores` is unchanged and still exported). A group row is defined as before, by the route's own test: no paragraphs and children present (`parentId` of the children names it); a nested group row (with its own `parentId`) counts too. **`withPartitionMarks` is not changed**: the partition's groups are rows with paragraphs of their own (`partitionedMap` copies the trunk's lines into them) and their tier comes from those paragraphs, with their own `max(1, floor(N / 2))` rule — they are not group rows in rule 1's sense.
2. **A question that asks for parameters or conditions favours the method sections.** `METHOD_BOOST = 1.3` (exported, named); `asksForParameters(terms)`: a specific term equals one of `parameter(s)`, `condition(s)`, `temperature`, `current`, `voltage`, `potential`, `concentration`, `rate`, `time`, `thickness`, `electrolyte`, or is a form of one by `termFormsMatch` (temperatures, rates, times). `pH` is two letters and `tokenize` drops it (it keeps tokens of three or more), so it cannot be a term; `how` is a stop word. A leaf row whose role is `method` or `apparatus` has its score x 1.3 before the group rows take their strongest child's score — so a group row whose children are method sections is boosted through them. Any other question: nothing changes (a test compares the tiers of a paper with a method section and of the same paper with the role `body`).
3. **A term in nearly every section weighs little.** `UBIQUITOUS_SHARE = 0.8`: in `relevanceScores`, a term found in at least 80 % of the scored sections has `idf = 0.1` alone. The paragraph-level df of `topParagraphs` is untouched (the brief says "of the scored sections"). **On the owner's paper this rule does not fire** — see "For the manager", 1.
4. **A function word is never a term (X-A3).** `such`, `various`, `like`, `etc`, `learn`, `want`, `know`, `understand`, `about` join **`FUNCTION_WORDS`** (`FUNCTION_WORDS_BEYOND_TOKENIZE`); `specificTerms` reads both that list and `ROUTE_STOPLIST`, `between` was in both already (`about` was only in `ROUTE_STOPLIST`). The facts line, the route and the preference ledger's question terms all go through `specificTerms`. "learning", "understanding", "knowledge", "likely" are other words and stay (a test).
