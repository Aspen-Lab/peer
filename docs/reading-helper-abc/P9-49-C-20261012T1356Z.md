## 2. The rules as built

**Rule 1 — the slot is held in both paths.** `PaperPlate` has a new prop `lead` (only the reading page's mount passes it). With `lead`, while the figure route has not answered (`resolved.status === "idle"`), nothing is bound and no picture is in hand (`leadFigurePending(figure, bound)`, exported), the component returns the figure's own box and nothing in it: `<div data-plate="figure" data-plate-pending aria-hidden>` with exactly the classes the picture's box has (the mat: `tile-cover cropmarks … aspect-[16/9] @container`, plus the one-column cap). It is the mat of `LoadingMat` ("the plate's mat at the plate's size"), with or without terms on the paper (a 2.4:1 terms plate would grow when the picture lands). No picture, no caption, no word of the paper's own. The page asks the same question of the same state: `hasPlate` now reads `leadPending` (so the swipeable card shell stays in the layout) and the card's frame follows the figure's, not the terms plate's, while pending. Keyed with a cached report the bound figure is there at first paint, as before; keyed without one and with no key the mat stands until the picture lands in it (the `<img>` fades in over the same mat). **If the figure never comes** (the route answers `no_figures`, or fails): with terms on the paper the mat becomes the 2.4:1 terms plate; with none the whole slot goes. In one step, when the route answers (4.6 s in the measurement below, 4-5 s in the walk). How far the h1 moves: §6.3.

**Rule 2 — the caption is whole or absent.** `leadCaption(caption, abstract?)` (exported from `paper-plate.tsx`; `copy.ts`'s `plateCaption` is no longer used by the page):
- null for an abstract block: `isAbstractBlock` — it opens with the word "Abstract" in any case (`^abstract\b`: "ABSTRACT:", "Abstract.", "abstract —", "Abstract" alone; not "Abstractions"), or its first 80 characters stand in the reading's abstract (`reading.abstract.sentences` joined, passed by the page);
- a caption that fits the room (140 characters, `LEAD_CAPTION_CHARS`, the head's `CAPTION_CHARS`) is returned as it is, white space made single;
- a longer one is cut where a sentence ends — the longest run of whole sentences that fits — with no ellipsis (the stop is the end). A sentence end is `.`/`!`/`?` (and closing quotes or brackets) followed by space and a capital, digit or opening mark, except after "Fig.", "et al.", "e.g.", "vs." … (an abbreviation list), after a single capital (an initial), and after a figure's number when the word before it is a label ("Figure 4.", "Fig. 2.", "Table 1."); a decimal ("0.62") has no space after its stop;
- only a sentence longer than the room is cut at a word: `slice(0, 139)` backed up to the last space, trailing stops, commas, colons, dashes and brackets stripped, one "…" (never a stop in front of it).
The plate itself (`lead` only) reads the same filter for the image's alt and the lightbox's caption: an abstract block is not in them (a feed tile's alt is as it was). The page's `<figcaption>` lost `xl:line-clamp-2`: a caption that is whole takes the lines it needs (it is never clipped to a cut). **When the caption is absent the plate has none** — not the paper's title: the feed's tile has no caption either; the title is the line under it on the feed and, on the page, the title block right under the plate.

**Rule 3 — capped in one column.** The lead plate's box (the picture's and the held one) carries `max-xl:max-h-[240px] max-xl:w-full`: the column's width, at most 240 px of height — a wide mat — and the picture stands in it `object-contain` (the `<img>` is `h-full w-full object-contain`, as before). A landscape figure is drawn at the cap's height; **a tall figure (the 9:16 Figure 4) is drawn at the cap's height too, 142 px wide at 768, centred, nothing cropped** (screenshot `run/shots/after-plate-figure-768.png`). The cap bites from a plate width of 427 px (viewport about 467); at 390 (197 px) nothing changes. `w-full` is there because Chrome carries a `max-height` through `aspect-ratio` to the inline axis (measured: without it the mat narrowed to 427 px and the caption stood wider than its plate); the test pins it. **The cap is `max-xl:`, not `max-lg:`** — see §8 item 5. The spread (xl and up: a 320 / 360 px panel, a 180 / 202 px plate) has no cap and does not change.

**Rule 4 — the feed's tiles.** `feed-tile.tsx` mounts `<PaperPlate paper={paper} terms={plateTerms} />` and nothing else; every new behaviour is behind `lead` (the held box, the cap, the alt filter), so a tile's markup is byte for byte what it was. Pinned by exact strings (the terms plate with three terms and with one, no plate for a paper with neither figure nor terms — also when its figure is still on the way — and the figure plate with its alt) and by a pin of the tile's one mount. These strings were rendered on the head (`cfcabbee`) before any change and are green on the head and on the final tree.
