## 2. The rules as built

**L1 (`html-text.ts`, the methods line of `canonicalizeHeading`).** `experimental` followed (optionally by `and <word>`) by section / setup / design / details / procedures / methods / protocols / techniques is methods, before the `^experiment…` → results line. "Experimental results", "Experiments", "Experimental evaluation", "Experimental and numerical results" stay results. Sub-headings inherit the bucket through the existing `bucketInheritor` (tested).

**L2 (`html-text.ts`).** New `END_MATTER_NOTE`, tested right after the methods line (before the `conclusion|summary` line that read "Reporting summary" as a conclusion): the **whole heading** is online content, additional information (and declarations), (Nature Portfolio) reporting summary, peer review information / file, reprints (and|&) permissions, rights and permissions, or keywords. It files under the existing `acknowledgments` bucket (role apparatus, like data availability and competing interests); **no new bucket, `ROLE` is unchanged**. The availability line grew to code / software / materials availability (and "availability of data and materials"). "Online content moderation", "Keyword extraction", "Peer review" stay body.

**A1–A4 (`reading-roles.ts`, `inferRoles(rows, trunkId)`, called once at the end of `readingMapOf`).** Only on rows whose role is `body`; a named role is never overridden; nothing happens unless an introduction is anchored: a top-level row of bucket introduction, or the front text.
- **A1.** The first row, bucket `body`, with no heading of its own (the extractors' placeholder `"Body"` — `pdf-text.ts` `normalize` writes `cleanPaperText(heading) || "Body"` — or an empty heading), in a paper that names no introduction, becomes role setup, bucket `introduction`, heading **"Introduction"** (the model partition's label, held equal to `MAP.parts.labels.introduction` by a test; written as a constant because `copy.ts` imports the reading, which imports the map). Guards I added to the brief's "one large unheaded section is left alone": the row has text; it is not `unheadedTrunk`'s trunk; and something other than end matter stands under it.
- **A2.** Unnamed top-level rows after the introduction and before the first named method / interpretation / apparatus top-level row (or to the paper's end) are evidence; an unnamed row directly before a references row is apparatus. Heading and bucket are kept; only the role is worked out.
- **A3.** Unnamed top-level rows after a references row are apparatus; an unnamed row right after a named method or apparatus row takes that role. Decisions the brief left open: "continues" is **one row**, not a chain (a chain would turn every descriptive results heading after an IMRaD-order Methods into method); the continuation is read **before** "directly before the references" (A2) and before "after the references"; a discussion, a conclusion or a results row hands nothing on.
- **A4.** A sub-row (a row with a `parentId`) that is still `body` takes the role of the row it hangs under, **when that role was worked out by A1–A3** (through sub-sub-rows). The sub-headings of a part that names its role already have its bucket from the extractors' inheritor and are not touched (§3).

**The unheaded rule moved.** `TRUNK`, `Trunk` and `unheadedTrunk` moved verbatim from `reading-partition.ts` into `reading-roles.ts` (so `reading-map.ts` can ask whether the front text is the whole paper without importing the module that imports the map); `reading-partition.ts` re-exports them and imports `unheadedTrunk` for `partitionedMap`. Same code, same numbers; every existing partition test passes unchanged.

**The closing split (`reading-partition.ts`).** `closingPartition(map, body, abstract)` returns a `GuidePartition` (new optional flag `closing: true`): in a map with no row of bucket discussion / conclusion, the first of the last `CLOSING.lastParagraphs` = 4 paragraphs of the **last evidence row** that has (a) a summary cue (`we show|showed|have shown|demonstrate(s|d)|conclude(s|d)`, `in summary`, `taken together`, `our findings`, `overall`, `in conclusion`), (b) no figure / table citation (`Fig.`/`Figs`/`Figure(s)`/`Table(s)` + optional `S`/`ED` + digit), (c) at least `CLOSING.abstractShare` = 20 % of its distinct long words (7+ letters, lower-cased) among the abstract's. No abstract (no long word in it) → null. Never the whole row (the closing starts at paragraph ≥ 1). Null as well when the map already carries a partition, when the row is `unheadedTrunk`'s trunk (the model's), when a row hangs under it, or when map and body are not aligned. `partitionedMap` draws a `closing` partition through a new `closedMap`: `<id>~0` is the row as the paper has it (its heading, bucket, role, page, level, parent) over the paragraphs before the closing; `<id>~1` is the closing under the role's own label ("Conclusion" = `MAP.parts.labels.conclusion`), role interpretation, level 1, no page; both carry a `part` record with `closing: true`; words / minutes counted again from the paragraphs; totals unchanged; body untouched. `closedMap` re-checks the map (role evidence, parts exactly results + conclusion, no named discussion / conclusion, no partition, not the trunk). `withPartitionMarks` folds the route over the two rows with no change (it reads `partOf`). A partition without the flag takes the model's path exactly as before.

**Why the split is an overlay, not inside `readingMapOf`.** `routeQuestion` pairs `map.sections[k]` with `body[k]` by index (`routeQuestion` in `reading-map.ts`) and `buildReading` / `explain` / `deep-report` pass the map and the body together; one extra row in the map would shift every later row against its text. The model partition has the same constraint and is an overlay the page applies (`partitionedMap`); the closing split follows it. The A rules change roles only, so they stay in `readingMapOf`.

**No mark (rule 5).** A1–A4 and L1–L2 add no tag, no flag and no text to a row beyond the role and, for A1, the label. The closing rows carry `part.closing` so a view can draw them as ordinary rows (§7).
