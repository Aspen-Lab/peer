# P7-04 — B (investigator): why a new local user never saw the walkthrough and could not find the key box

Role B, read-only. Date 2026-10-11. Branch checked out: `deep-report-reading-helper-enhancement`, HEAD `bc309034`. `web/` at HEAD is byte-identical to `origin/deep-report-reading-helper-enhancement` (`bde92aaf`), and `origin/main` (`6a702c6f`, PR #34) has the same `first-run.tsx`, `components/briefing/`, and `app/page.tsx`. So a download of either branch behaves the same. Nothing tracked was changed; no commit, no branch.

## Answer in one paragraph

The wizard did not appear because nothing sends a first-time visitor to `/welcome` any more: `FirstRunGate` (mounted in `layout.tsx:151`) lost its redirect and now only backfills `onboardedAt` (`first-run.tsx:60-81`), and the home page reaches `/welcome` only through an 11.5px, 3.1:1-contrast grey link ("is here.") at the bottom-left of the sample card (`starter-strip.tsx:75-83`). What the user saw instead was the home page: the date headline, then a card titled "A SAMPLE, UNTIL YOU SAY OTHERWISE" with six field chips and an "or type your field" box, the sample papers (or "Couldn't reach today's paper sources." offline) below it; one tap on a chip makes the card vanish for good and leaves nothing on the home page that mentions setup or a key. Clicks to the key box from a fresh home page: 3 clicks plus typing one topic via the strip link and the wizard rail (5 clicks via Continue), 2 clicks plus about 1,500 px of scrolling via Profile → Edit, 2 clicks via a paper's "Add a key" link (code-read only), and on every path the API-key input itself appears only after you also choose an AI company in a dropdown. The redirect was removed by `f020a4b3` "feat(first-run): open on a paper, not a form" (2026-09-16, Aspen Labs; the last earlier version is `5ae11d7b`), which reached this branch through merge `87c2049e` (2026-09-24, "…zero-setup first run"); not by PR #33 or #34. Recommended fix: option (b), real "Set up Peer" and "Add a key" buttons in the sample card plus a one-line "Add a key" row that survives the first field tap, not the redirect.

Can the strip be missed? On screen at first paint, no (section 4). Does the sample push it down, or hide it on load? No: the cards render below it (`page.tsx:340` before `:356`). What is easy to miss is the way out of it: the setup link is 11.5px mono, `rgb(142,142,142)` on `rgb(250,250,250)` = **3.14:1** (below the 4.5:1 AA floor for small text), a 55×15 px target, while the six chips (13.5px, 40px tall) and "Use this" are the visible actions.
