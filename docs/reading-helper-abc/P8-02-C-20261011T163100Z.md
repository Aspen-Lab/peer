## 1. The behaviour as built

From a window of **1024 px** (the dock case). The card (640–1023) and the phone sheet (< 640) are as they were.

**Stays open (commit 1).**
- Once the window is open, a new selection in the body does **not** close it: `stale` (the derived "a new selection closes it") now also needs `viewport.width < 1024`. §1h.3's "a new selection closes" is amended for the dock only; the card and the sheet still close on one (pinned at 1023, 800, 640 and 390; the window at 1024 stays).
- "Explain this?" still stands at the new selection. The box now watches two selections: the window's passage (`placed`) and the one on offer (`offered`: the reader's current selection when it is not the passage already in the window). Pressing the button, or `e`, calls `start(selection)`, which **replaces the session in place** — one `setSession` from the old session to the new one, so there is no render in which `open` is null, `docked` never changes, the layout effect that calls `reserveDock` does not run again, and `removeProperty` is never called between two passages (the test spies on it; `setProperty` is called twice — once per property — from the first open to the close).
- The previous passage's thread is replaced in the window and stays the store's, kept for its passage: explaining it again opens its kept answer and thread with no request (`cached` / `cachedTurns` are the page's, derived from the current selection, which is the one being explained). The new passage starts with an empty draft, web search off, and the window scrolled to its top (one new effect, `card.scrollTop = 0` on a change of passage: the window is the same element, so without it the next passage would open where the last was scrolled to).
- `e`: when the current selection is a passage other than the window's (and is worth a button: a key, or a paper definition inside it) it explains it in the same window; otherwise — same passage, or nothing selected — it focuses the input, as before.
- Escape and the Close button close it, and only then are `--explain-dock` / `--explain-dock-width` taken off the document. A press outside does nothing (P7-15). A selection that merely goes away (a click in the window collapses it) leaves it open. All unchanged.
- The late-arrival guards were already keyed on the passage: an answer or a reply that comes back for a passage the window has left is not shown in the new one (two new tests; a mutation that removes the reply guard is caught (M8)).

**Level with the paragraph (commit 2).**
- `paper-body.tsx` reports one more thing with a selection: `ExplainSelection.paragraph` (an optional `ViewRect`), the box of the paragraph's own `<p>` (the first child of the `paper-section-N-pM` anchor; its top is the paragraph's first line). `measure()` reads it again with the rest. It is left out where the element cannot give a box or the box has no size (the selection is still read); `sameSelection` compares it. The body's markup is untouched.
- `placePanel(bounds, viewport, paragraphTop?)` for the dock:

  `top = clamp(paragraphTop, DOCK_TOP_PX = 48, height − DOCK_BOTTOM_PX (28) − DOCK_MIN_VISIBLE_PX (160))` · `maxHeight = max(0, height − top − 28)` · `right: 0` · `width: 360` · **no `bottom`** (the height is the content's, at most `maxHeight`; the thread scrolls inside and the input stays pinned at its foot).

  The clamp numbers: height 900 → the window follows the paragraph from **48 to 712**, stays at **48** above it (height up to 824) and at **712** below it (height 160); height 800 → 48 … **612**; height 768 → 48 … 580; height 600 → 48 … 412. A window shorter than 236 px has top 48 (`clamp` returns the low end). With no paragraph reported the top is the selection's own first line (`bounds.top`) — the nearest honest degradation; in the browser the paragraph is always reported.
- The window **moves when the next passage is explained**, not on a mere selection: the placement reads the open session's selection (`whereOf(open.selection).paragraph?.top`) and that changes only in `start()`. The window follows its own paragraph as the page scrolls or resizes (the same rAF measure as the card's), clamped as above.
- Right edge flush, 360 wide, the page's width kept clear (`--explain-dock` 376 px), the notes tab beside it: untouched.
- Comments: `explain-box.tsx`'s header (the "a new selection closes" sentence and the P7-15 paragraph are amended; "Where it stands" says the new rule), `Place` and `placePanel`'s docs, `ExplainCard`'s `dock` doc; `explain-dock.ts`'s header and `DOCK_TOP_PX`'s ("the highest top"), and the new `DOCK_MIN_VISIBLE_PX = 160`. `copy.ts` is unchanged; nothing new is written on the screen.

## 3. Departures and decisions

a. **`paragraph` is optional** on `ViewRects`, with the fallback above. A required field would have meant touching every `ExplainSelection` literal in five test files; the production reader always reports it.
b. **The new selection's button stays out of the dock** (not in the brief). Before this item the button never stood while the dock was open; now it does, and a selection that ends at the column's right edge would put it under the 360 px window (z-70 against z-70). While the dock is open `placeButton` gets a viewport narrowed by the 376 px the page keeps clear. Measured at 1280: the button's left clamped to 776 (right edge 891.7) against the dock's 920. One mutation removes it and is caught.
c. **The dock's frame.** Level with a paragraph and as tall as its content, the window no longer spans masthead to legend, so P7-15's `border-y-0` (the masthead's and the legend's own lines stood above and below it) would leave a floating panel with a line on its left only. It now carries its own top and bottom lines (none on the right). At the clamp (top 48) its line sits directly under the masthead's; looked at in the screenshots, one line to the eye. **Easy to revert if the owner prefers**; the pin that held the old classes is re-aimed (below).
d. **The window starts a new passage scrolled to its top** (new effect). Not in the brief; the same element is reused, so it was needed for "takes the next passage in place" to read right.
e. **Tests whose subject this item changes — re-aimed, none deleted or weakened (§1c).** Commit 1: `explain-box.flow` "closes when the reader selects something else" and "does not show an answer that arrives after the reader has moved on" → same assertions at 900 px (the card), renamed "(the card, 640 to 1023 px)"; `explain-box.thread.flow` "does not show a reply that arrives after the reader has moved on, and starts nothing again" → at 900 px; "holds the NEW selection when a new passage closes the box, not the one the box was opened for" → "…when the box closes with a new passage selected…", closed by Escape with the new passage selected (same expected scrolls, 800 then −150); `explain-box.brevity.flow` "drops a reply that arrives after the reader has moved on to another passage" → "moved on" is now the press on the next passage's button (valid at both widths). Commit 2: `explain-box.thread.test` placePanel's "is a dock at the window's right edge from 1024 px: 360 wide, under the masthead, above the legend" (→ its top the paragraph's, no bottom), "does not depend on where the selection is" (→ …once the paragraph is known), "is the window's height less the masthead and the legend, and never negative" (→ …when the paragraph is above), and the card test "is marked as the dock, and is a window-high column…" (→ a column against the right edge, a line on its left, top and bottom); `explain-box.thread.flow` "the dock on a window of 1440: flush right, 360 wide, under the masthead and above the legend" and "the dock at 1280 and at 1920 too, and where the selection is changes nothing" (→ the paragraph's top; the words' position within the paragraph changes nothing).
