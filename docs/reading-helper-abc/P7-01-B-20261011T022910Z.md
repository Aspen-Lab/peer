## 0. The answer in short

**Root cause (one sentence).** `linesOfPage` (`pdf-outline.ts:217–255`) gathers every pdf.js item whose baseline is within 2.2 pt of a row into one line across the whole page width and `buildOutline` then reads those rows top to bottom, so on a two-column page the extractor has no notion of a column: the two columns' lines are either welded into one row (when their baselines happen to fall within 2.2 pt: 21 of the 32 lines on page 2, 507 of 1,267 over the paper) or alternate in y order (when they do not), and the 24 pt gutter between them is flattened to a single space (`:237`).

|---|---|---|---|
| real headings detected, of the paper's 19 | **5** | **19** | **19** |
| real headings surfaced as map sections (ceiling 15, §5) | 5 | 15 | 15 |
| fragment / false headings surfaced as sections | 13 | 20 | 14 |
| sections | 21 | 36 | 30 |
| paragraphs | **528** | **265** | **141** |
| page 2: lines mixing both columns | **21 of 32** (all 20 body rows) | **0** of 51 | 0 |
| page 2: left/right switches among the 40 body lines | every row mixed | 1 | 1 |
| whole paper: lines mixing both columns | 507 of 1,267 | 0 of 1,685 | 0 |
| abstract section found | no | yes, 2,076 chars | yes |
| captions: continuation lines taken / caption-size lines left in the body | 0 of 10 / 70 | to the 700-char cap / 4 | to the cap / 0 |
| footer fragments inside paragraphs (page 1) | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| "Received…" box lines in the Introduction | 4, woven line by line | 4, contiguous | 0 |

**Fix, place and size.** In `pdf-outline.ts`: replace the 40-line `linesOfPage` with a column-aware builder and add two small `buildOutline` rules (margin print, wrapped headings); about **190 code lines added, 40 replaced**, plus **~130 lines of tests** and a **one-line bump** of `UPLOAD_CONCEPT_EXTRACTION_VERSION` (`upload-concepts.ts:15`, 2 → 3) so the owner's cached scrambled text is re-read. Pure TypeScript, no model, no new dependency; unpdf already hands over pdf.js's own items. Single-column PDFs and scans are unchanged (§6). The extra "hygiene" tier (V6) is a separate, optional ~20 lines with real side effects on other documents.

**Unsettled** (detail in §7): the hygiene rules change single-column output; the italic author-affiliation block still makes 13 junk sections without a riskier rule; superscripts become digits glued to words; the 700-char caption cap now truncates 6 of 10 captions that used to leak into the body; detection is tuned on one real two-column paper; the owner's already-uploaded copy needs the version bump **and** a re-upload to get its abstract.

## 3. Root cause of the column interleave (page 2, pdf.js coordinates)

`page2-items.json` holds all 204 items of page 2 (`x = transform[4]`, `y = transform[5]`, `width`, `height`, `str`, full transform, font). Page is 607.4 × 800.9 pt. `s/measure-p2.ts` → `p2-measure.json`:

* **Text x-range** 51.5 → 556.9 pt. **Left column** 51.5 → 291.5 (240.0 pt wide), **right column** 315.4 → 555.5 (240.0 pt). **Gutter 23.9 pt = 2.39 em** at 10 pt.
* The two columns **share baselines** on this page: 20 body rows from y = 267.1 down to 48.9 at a 11.4–11.5 pt pitch, in both columns. Three of those rows, geometry only:

| y | left run (x → right edge, width) | right run (x → right edge, width) |
|---|---|---|
| 267.1 | 51.5 → 291.5, 240.0 | 315.4 → 531.3, 215.9 |
| 255.6 | 51.5 → 255.7, 204.2 | 315.4 → 555.4, 240.0 |
| 221.4 / 221.1 | 51.5 → 291.5, 240.0 | 315.4 → 414.2, 98.7 (baseline 0.3 pt off the left's) |

* The branch's `linesOfPage` keys a row by y alone (`:221`), so each of these pairs becomes **one line**, left run then right run, joined by one space (`:237`). Of the **32 lines** the branch builds on page 2: **21 contain runs from both columns** (all 20 body rows, plus the running head, whose "Journal of the American Chemical Society" and "pubs.acs.org/JACS Article" sit on opposite sides); **5 are superscript-only rows** ("17", "9", "18", "10−15", "16"); 4 are the full-width Figure 1 caption rows (these legitimately cross the gutter: their runs are 459–505 pt wide); 2 are footer rows.
* **Page 3 shows why a tolerance cannot fix it.** There the columns drift: left baselines 733.9, 722.9, 711.5, 700.3, 689.0, 677.8, 666.5…, right baselines 733.9, 722.9, 700.3, 679.7, 669.3, 655.3… (the right column opens the "2. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION" heading block and falls out of step). Rows within 2.2 pt merge; rows 2.8–11 pt apart do not and are interleaved by y. Same page, both failure modes.
* **Whole paper** (`s/mixed-all.ts` → `mixed-all.txt`): of the branch's 1,267 lines, **507 (40 %) join runs from both columns**, 78 are superscript-only fragments. Per page: 3 to 56 mixed lines (p. 9, mostly a figure, has 3; p. 10 has 56).
* The pdf.js **item order** is already column-major: walking `getTextContent().items` in array order gives exactly one left→right switch on each of pp. 2–17 — the branch discards it by sorting on y. (Used for the alternative in §5.)

The gutter is the only evidence the branch had and it destroys it twice: a 24 pt gap becomes one space, and rows are keyed without x.

## 5. The fix, prototyped (scratch only)

**Where.** `proto/pdf-outline.ts` = a copy of `lib/papers/pdf-outline.ts` (the only difference outside the new code is one import path); `proto.diff` is the diff; `proto/pdf-text.ts` is an unchanged copy for the tests. Switches (`PROTO.*`) let each rule be turned off; **with everything off the prototype is byte-identical to the branch** on Patra (`identicalToBranch = true`, outline JSON equal).

**Design (what V5 does).**
1. **Columns.** `usualHeight` = the page's modal text height. `gutterOf` scans a 2-em band across the central 35–65 % of the text width for the **longest run of consecutive rows with no ink in the band**; it needs ≥ 8 such rows, ≥ 8 rows with text on each side, each side ≥ 30 % of the text width, and each side's *median line* filling ≥ 60 % of its width ("columns of prose, not cards or table cells"). Tolerates full-width rows (a caption, a title) because they only end a run. `gutterPrior` takes the gutter most pages agree on for a page with too little text of its own (page 9 is a figure).
2. **Order.** `linesOfPage` walks the page's rows top to bottom; a row whose ink **runs unbroken across the gutter** (`crossesGutter`: text on both sides and no gap wider than 0.8 em over the gutter) is a full-width line (title, caption, abstract tail) and ends the current band; all other rows' runs are split by where each run **starts** (a word that overhangs the gutter stays with its column) into a left and a right set; each band is emitted left column top→bottom, then right column top→bottom. Lines are built per column, so the columns' baselines never merge.
3. **Column-relative indents.** The right column's `x` is shifted by (median right start − median left start), so `joinProse`'s `x > left + 6` indent test (`:353`) is unchanged and correct.
4. **Superscripts** (`rowsOf`): runs under 0.75 × the page's text height fold into the nearest row whose baseline is 7.5 pt below to 3.5 pt above, in x order — "1st, 20th, 40th" stays one string.
5. **Margin print** (`buildOutline`): a line under 0.8 × body size above the page's first, or below its last, full-size line is furniture (catches page 1's one-off footer).
6. **Wrapped headings** (`buildOutline`): the next line in the same face and size, one leading below, not numbered, joins the heading ("2.1. … LiCoO2" + "Crystals").
7. **Run-in label** (`linesOfRow`): a first run matching `abstract|summary|key words|highlights` in another face than the rest of the line becomes its own line, a heading.

The first five are V6 (safe on everything I have: only the equation rule moves another document, and there it removes 29 of the printout's 43 "equations", all of the kind shown above). The density rule is what removes the italic author block (13 junk sections → 4) but moves the design spec from 44 to 37 sections: not recommended without a look at that document.

**Content check.** Word-multiset overlap against `pdftotext -raw` pages 1–17 (the reference list on pp. 18–19 is dropped by design): branch recall 92.8 % / precision 98.7 %; V5 89.4 % / 98.5 %; V6 89.1 % / 98.4 %. The recall dip is entirely the existing 700-char `CAPTION_MAX` (`:136`) now truncating captions that used to leak into the body: with the cap lifted V6 is **93.0 % / 98.4 %** (caption lengths 496–1,754 chars; six exceed 700). No other loss shows up in this measure. DOI is still found from `page1Text` in every variant. Outline stage time: 18 ms → 55 ms (pdf.js itself is ~1 s).

**`pdftotext` (reference only).** `pdftotext -layout` does **not** order the columns: it prints each text *row* with the left column's text, a run of spaces, then the right column's text, so the two columns sit side by side and never mix inside a column. What it does differently from the branch is exactly the thing that matters: it keeps the x position, so the 24 pt gutter survives as whitespace, whereas `linesOfPage` collapses it to one space (`:237`, `:243`). Default (reading-order) mode separates the columns with poppler's own block analysis but, on page 2, emits the **right column before the left** (also pp. 5, 6, 17 by a script over the 8 pages it could classify), so it is not a safe oracle either. `-raw` (content-stream order) is correct on pp. 2–17 because this PDF's stream is column-major — the same fact as pdf.js's item order above.

**Alternative tried: trust the stream order (S5).** Group runs of consecutive items on one baseline, keep the stream's order, no geometry. Ordering and headings come out the same as V5 (0 mixed lines, 19 of 19) in ~12 lines — but paragraphs jump to 713 because the right column's raw x = 315 still reads as an indent to `joinProse`, so it still needs a column notion (or a per-column `left`); and it is only as good as each producer's content-stream order (true for this ACS/InDesign PDF and for Skia/Chrome PDFs; unknown for others — I have no second two-column PDF). Geometry works from what is printed, so I recommend it; the stream order is a cheap fallback signal at best.

## 7. Unsettled, risks, and things I did not do

1. **Hygiene rules are behaviour changes on other documents.** The equation rule moves the owner's printout from 132 to 94 paragraphs (it removes 29 of its 43 detected "equations"; the ones I looked at are the same crystallographic notation torn from the paper's sentences, because that printout is the app's own report on this paper — which also makes it a weaker independent test than the other PDFs); the density rule moves the design spec 44 → 37 sections; the margin rule dropped a real (if tiny) label. Each needs its own test and a decision; none is needed for the column fix.
2. **The italic author-affiliation block is still 13 junk sections in V6** (4 with the density rule). `fontName` ids cannot tell italic from bold (here `f12` is the italic of the same family, `f1` the semibold of the headings); a real fix needs the font's actual name from pdf.js (`page.commonObjs`), which I did not try.
3. **Superscripts become plain digits glued to words** ("shape17 and", "MPam1/2)1"). Better to mark them (a `^17` form, or drop pure-numeric citation superscripts); a product call.
4. **The 700-char caption cap** (`:136`) now visibly truncates 6 of 10 captions (880–1,754 chars; the other four are 496–641).
5. **Thresholds are tuned on one real two-column paper** (2 em, 8 rows, 60 % fill, central 35–65 %). Not tested: three-column pages, text wrapped around a figure on one side, ragged-right columns, rotated pages, other producers, full-width tables (a table whose cells look like columns would be read column-major). The one non-paper PDF with side-by-side blocks changed on 3 of 20 pages.
6. **The owner's already-uploaded copy.** Its extracted text is cached under key version 2 (memory for an hour, then `<hash16>.doc.json`), so the fix is invisible until the version is bumped. And `meta.summaryIntro` is frozen at upload time (`route.ts:296`) and never recomputed on read, so the abstract only appears after a **re-upload** (an identical re-upload takes the idempotent refresh path and rewrites the meta) — or a refresh step that nobody has written.
7. **Paragraph count is not "right" at 141.** Geometry gives only 34 indent-opened paragraphs in the body plus one opening per heading; V6's number still includes ~40 paragraphs created by the 21 remaining false equations and a hanging-indent list. Read the table as a trend, not a target.
8. `pdftotext`'s reading-order mode was checked programmatically on only 8 of 17 pages (the others hold captions that confuse the check) plus page 2 by eye.
9. Items not run: the real route (auth/storage), a browser, any model.
