## Choices the brief left to me / flags
- Default unticked (a question is declared intent), as the brief says; nothing in the code or spec pointed the other way.
- Weight 0.2 (a fifth of a like; 0.9 is an upload's floor): one question moves a matching candidate's boost by about 0.013 against a like's 0.05. Tested on a fixture where a like flips the top result and a question does not.
- Evidence kept in its own `questions` record keyed by an opaque per-paper hash, not in `uploads` (whose keys are 64-hex document keys and whose presence labels a row "from your upload").
- Question evidence is not shown in the profile screen's summary (nothing visible moves), and is not an "upload interest" (`uploadInterestTerms` unchanged).
- A term the reader already likes, dislikes or uploaded gets one more piece of positive evidence (0.2) on the same ledger entry; removal takes exactly that piece. A dislike on the same word is eased by 0.2, not erased.
- Removing every question and settling takes the paper's terms out; a removed or edited question's terms leave at the next settle.
- A removal reaches the account by the next sync (the PUT replaces the ledger); sign-in merge is a per-key union and can bring a removed term back from a second device whose copy still has it (the same as an upload's removal today). Not changed.
- The checkbox row sits under its line; not looked at in a browser (no Chromium run).
- `merge.ts` entry timestamps are not bumped by question evidence (as for uploads, so as not to refresh the decay of likes).
