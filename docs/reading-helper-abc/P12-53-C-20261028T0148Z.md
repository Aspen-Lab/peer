## 2. The rules as built

**1. The core (`structure-review.ts`, pure, no `node:` import).**
- `paragraphFeatures(text)` returns `{F,T,C,N,V,W}` with Appendix B's regexes, and `countsText` prints `F2 T1 C3 N4 V2 W1` with zeros left out. Exported so the Tier-0 rules can share it.
- `structureInputOf(map, body, extra)` pairs map row k with body section k and numbers the paragraphs 0 … N−1 over the whole paper (null if the two are not one list). `draftOf(input)` makes the draft from the map's top-level rows (no `parentId`, with their sub-rows): the row's role, confidence `high` (tag `heading:<bucket>`) when the heading names it; a `body` row takes the part before it, `medium` (`inherited`); a row at the very start with nothing before it is `setup`, `low` (`default`); a row with no paragraph is no part; neighbours of one role are one part at the lowest confidence of its rows; tags are rule names and counts only (`rows:n`, `paras:n`; a bucket that is not `[a-z_]{1,24}` is `heading:named`). Lines are `<k> <from>-<to> <role> <conf> <tags>`; `digest` is a cyrb53 of the rules version, N and the lines.
- `buildSkeleton` (grammar of B §2.2): section header `## <id> L<level> p<page|-> <bucket> "<heading ≤ 60>" <from>-<to>`, paragraph line `<i>[ <counts>] | <first 16 words, "…" when cut>`, caption line `fig <label> p<page> | <first 8 words>` (at most 40 of them). Mode A; mode B (heads only for the first two and the last paragraph of each part, every paragraph of a `low` part, the first paragraph of each section; counts everywhere) when the skeleton passes 24,000 characters; mode B with 8-word heads and no `fig` lines after that; `null` (`too_large`, the draft stands) after that. The counts are made once per paper, not per mode.
- `buildReviewPrompt`: Appendix B's system line and user JSON (task, `paper {pages}`, `roles`, `countsKey`, `skeleton`, `draft`, `outputSchema`, six rules); no title, nothing of the reader; 600 answer tokens. The fixed text (2,304 characters) is pinned by length and hash in a test; an edit must bump `REVIEW_PROMPT_VERSION`.
- `sanitizeReview` / `applyCorrections` / `sound` / `reviewStructure`: Appendix A ported (`move_start`, `set_role`, `split`, `unsure`; at most 40 read; indices in range and in order; five roles after trim and lower-case, no aliases, `body` refused; part 0 never moves; one move and one role per part, at most 3 splits per part; starts, then roles, then splits; neighbours of one role merge). An answer that is not an answer, or whose every correction was refused, leaves the draft exactly; so does anything that throws or is unsound.
- The second look: `secondLookTargets` (a correction against a `high` / `medium` part, then a part in `unsure`; strongest first; at most 3; nothing for a correction against a `low` part), `lookParagraphs` (3 either side of the old and the new start of a move, not leaving the two parts; 4 either side of a split; the part thinned — first 3, last 2, every fourth — for a role change or an `unsure` part), `lookWindow` (each paragraph clipped to 600 characters, the whole to 6,000, outermost dropped first, front and back in turn), `buildLookPrompt` (paragraphs `{n,text}`, the two readings as plain statements, `{verdict, role?}`, 60 tokens), `parseLookVerdict`, `resolveLooks`.
- `structureReviewKey(docHash)` = `docHash|rules:…|skel:…|prompt:…`; `createStructureReviewCache` (1 hour, 32 entries); `restoreReview` (reads a kept or fetched entry; another version of the rules, skeleton or prompt is no entry); `StructureReviewEntry` holds indices, role names, look verdicts, the model's id: no word of the paper.
- `applyStructureReview(map, draft, review)`: a row shows the role most of its paragraphs have under the review and changes only where the review changed that; no row added, removed or reordered, no id changed, no field added (**no mark**); a sub-row whose own heading names a role keeps it; the partition's rows (`part.sectionId`) are handled; the very map object comes back for no review, another draft's review (digest), an unsound one, or one that changes no row.

**2. The route `POST /api/papers/[id]/structure`.** Order as the gist route's: malformed claim and `ownedUpload` (404), `requireAiRequest("paper-structure", 20)` (401 / 429 / 503), `resolveProvider(override)` (no provider → `200 {unavailable:true}`, nothing read), `getFullText`, the draft and skeleton from the document as the page holds it, the memory (hit → `{review, cached:true}`, no call), the review call on `reportModelTier()` with a 30 s race (late result ignored), `reviewStructure`, up to 3 looks in parallel (20 s together; a look that fails, times out or cannot be read drops the correction it was asked about), `uploadStillCurrent` (410 and nothing written), the entry written. Failures answer `200 {fellBack: "unusable" | "timeout" | "provider_error" | "too_large"}` and write nothing. Logs: sizes and counts, `err.name` only. Headers `PRIVATE_UPLOAD_HEADERS` for an upload, `no-store` otherwise.

**3. The client.** `use-structure-review.ts` (the gist hook's pattern): `peer-structure-review-v1`, the reading key; asks once per paper when the reading has a body and a draft and the reader has a model (`providerConfigured`, not the deep-report switch, §1h.428 (a)); first tick passes, aborts on unmount, keyed on the paper's key, the reader's key through a ref; an answer is kept 30 days with the digest of the draft it was made against (a kept review of another draft is not laid over this one and the paper is asked again; a fetched review of another draft is kept as a failure); a failure (`fellBack`, `unavailable`, a digest mismatch) 1 hour; an error status, a non-answer or no network keeps nothing; 24 papers; every `localStorage` touch in try/catch. Without a model it returns nothing and sends nothing, even for a paper whose review is kept. `page.tsx` makes `structureDraft` from `reading.map` / `reading.body`, calls the hook, and `reviewedMap = applyStructureReview(shownMap, structureDraft, structureReview)` right after the partition overlay and the route fold; the map view is handed `reviewedMap`.

**4. The stub.** `kindOf` answers `structure` (key `corrections`) and `structure_look` (key `verdict`) before every other kind (a piggybacked prompt would carry `gists` too). Default: `{corrections:[],unsure:[]}`; `stub-control.json` `structure`: `move`, `role`, `split` (one valid correction), `invalid` (refused by the sanitizer), `garbage` (not JSON), `empty` (empty content), `hang` (20 s; run by hand, 20,693 ms then a valid answer); `look`: the verdict (`draft` by default). Other kinds unchanged.

**5. Nothing else changes.** The gist call, its pinned prompt goldens, Tier 0 and the map without a key are untouched (the existing gist and partition tests pass; the only edits to tests are the named re-aims in section 3).

The skeleton depends on the count of paragraphs and sections, not on their length (B §2.3's rule of thumb holds: about 100 characters a paragraph all in). A paper of short paragraphs saves little, as P3's parse did.

Behaviour, each pinned by a test above:
- A draft with one wrong role and one wrong start (a method run under a Results heading; a "Further analysis" bucketed as methods): the double returns `move_start part 2 → 11` and `set_role part 3 → evidence`; applied, the five parts equal the truth. A draft that fused two roles: one `split`, equal to the truth. A right draft: no correction, the draft comes back.
- Eight kinds of unusable answer (part out of range, unknown role, op `delete`, sixty refused corrections, `{}`, prose, `[]`, nothing), a 30 s timeout, a throwing provider: `200 {fellBack}`, the draft unchanged, no entry written.
- A cache hit: the second POST is `cached: true` and the provider's call count does not move (first POST: 1 review + 2 looks = 3 calls). A bumped rules / skeleton / prompt version misses.
- No key: the route answers `{unavailable:true}` having read nothing; the hook with `enabled: false` sends no request and returns nothing even when a review is kept; the map is the object it was.

## 7. For the manager

1. **D6 (the durable owner-scoped sidecar) is not built.** It needs `upload-store.ts` (not mine). The memory is process-only (an hour), so a cold start re-bills one review; the browser keeps an answer 30 days. The owner check precedes the memory read and the privacy test pins it. That is the next item.
2. **Only the map's rows take the reviewed roles.** `readingRoute` / `mergedRoute` / `gistRoute` / `ForYourQuestions` still read Tier 0's `reading.map` roles, so the question-intent weights and "Just get the gist" tiers do not see a correction. Feeding them the reviewed map is a `page.tsx` + `reading-map.ts` change outside this brief.
3. **A boundary inside a row cannot be drawn.** The rows are sections; a row shows the role most of its paragraphs have. The parts that cross sections belong to the generalised `partitionedMap` (B §8.4, C P12-48); `applyStructureReview` already works on the partition's rows (`part.sectionId`). **When the Tier-0 rules land**, `draftOf` should be replaced by their parts (it is built from the map's top-level rows, as §1h.428 (d) said), `STRUCTURE_RULES_VERSION` bumped (its digest then invalidates every kept review by itself), and `paragraphFeatures` shared.
4. **Two decisions of mine to check.** (a) A leading run nothing places is `setup`, **`low`** (tag `default`), not `medium`: the brief gave high / medium and was silent on a row with nothing before it; `low` means a correction against it draws no look. (b) For a part the model listed `unsure`, a look that says `review` and names a different role becomes a `set_role` (B §3.5 says an unsure part "stays as the draft", which would make its look pointless); it is one branch in `resolveLooks` and one test, easy to remove.
5. **Merge risk in `page.tsx`.** My block sits after the `route` memo and before `projectText`; it does not touch the `shownMap` / `route` memos, but C P12-48 may edit lines next to it. The four re-aimed page tests changed only JSX literals.
6. **Not run: the real-key protocol (B §7).** C1 ("every part right, with a key") can be shown only by a real model; the 4-chars-per-token numbers above are estimates (`usage` is the real count). The piggyback-on-the-gist arm is not built (D2).
