## 2. The rules as built

**Rule 1, the tab's rect never meets the divider's strip (`notes-button.tsx`).**
- **Geometry.** The margin divider's 12 px hit strip is the grid track between the centre and the margin (`.reader-shell`: `… 12px minmax(0, 1fr) 12px var(--margin-w)`); it ends where the margin cell begins, which is `rect.left` from `useMarginRect()` in the window's pixels, at every Fit zoom (the strip is `12 x zoom` wide and ends there). The tab stood at that edge with `translateX(-100%)`, its 15 px face over the strip.
- **Place.** Beside a card (a side margin registered) the tab is `left: rect.left + EDGE_TAB_DIVIDER_CLEAR_PX` (3 px: its box 3 px clear, its 1 px frame 2 px clear), 15 px wide (`EDGE_TAB_BESIDE_PX`, unchanged), no translation. Its `z` is `z-[69]` beside a card (the top of the stack, `STACK_Z_MAX`; under the selection's actions `z-[70]` and the lightbox `z-[80]`), `z-40` as before otherwise, because the cards (`z` 60 to 69) stand at the margin's left edge and the tab is now over their left edge.
- **Hit area.** The transparent 9 px strip that gives the tab its 24 px (`EDGE_TAB_HIT_PX`) stood on the text's side (`before:-left-[10px]`), which now would lie over the divider's strip. It is on the card's side: `before:-right-[9px]`.
- **Header controls.** `useHeaderBands` reads the card header rows from the page (`openCards(document, true).headers`, the rows the selection's actions keep clear of: a card's first element, the explain card's top row, the figure's zoom row), a frame after the stack, the margin, the window or a scroll changes, and only while the tab is beside a card at a registered margin. `clearOfHeaders` leaves the tab where it is (the CSS `top`, unchanged) unless it would be within `EDGE_TAB_HEADER_CLEAR_PX` (4 px) of a header row; then it takes, in this order, the smallest clear top below the header it meets (below the last of a stack, each in turn), else the largest clear top above, else the top that covers the least. Tops are held between the masthead's foot (3rem) and 8 px above the legend's top less the tab's length, so E1 (P12-77) is not undone. The `top` is written inline (`clearTop`) only when it moves; the x is the same.
- **Unchanged:** the band and phone placements (`beside` is false there, the right edge, `--tab-band-top`), the row, the press, `aria-expanded`, the drag pass-through, the thumb bar and legend clamps, the 146 / 66 px lengths.

**Rule 2, the strips end at the legend's top (`pane-divider.tsx`).** The strip was as tall as the shell's row (`align-self: stretch`), so the fixed legend (`--legend-h`, 28 px; 28 x zoom under Fit) stood over its foot at every scroll but the last. Each `PaneDivider` (both) now carries `DIVIDER_FOOT_STYLE`: `position: sticky; align-self: start; top: calc(3rem / var(--page-zoom, 1) + 1rem); height: 100%; max-height: calc((100vh - 3rem) / var(--page-zoom, 1) - var(--legend-h) - 1rem)`. The `top` is the left pane's sticky top (its position at scroll 0, so nothing slides) and the stylesheet's `scroll-margin-top` for the separator's focus; the `max-height` is the room between that and the legend's top edge in the zoomed page (painted `100vh - 48 - 44 x zoom`, so the foot is `100vh - 28 x zoom`, the legend's top, `legendHeightPx`); `height: 100%` keeps it no taller than a short paper's row; at the page's foot the row's end carries it up. The strip's width, line (`::before`), cursor, touch action, focus, keys, drag and values are the stylesheet's and the component's, unchanged. The rail's hairline (`reader-divider-rest`) is not a hit strip and is untouched.

## 7. For the manager

1. **A short window leaves no room below a header: the tab goes above it (rule 1).** At 1024 x 576 and 853 x 480 (default step) the tab's usual place is already the lowest the legend's 8 px allow (394, 298), so a header that reaches it ends below that place and there is no clear top under it; the tab stands above the header (224 to 370, 128 to 274) rather than under the legend. 2. **The tab now stands over the left edge of the cards.** A card's text begins 13 px in (its border and `p-3`), the face covers 3 to 18, and its 9 px hit strip 18 to 27: in the tab's own rows the first letters of a card's lines are under it (and a press there is the tab's). A should look at a sticky or the notes window at the tab's height at 1440 x 900 with the real font; this is the cost of "just inside the margin".
3. **Rule 2 shortens the line by 16 x zoom px at its top while the page is scrolled.** The strip begins where the left pane does (64 px at 1x, 48 + 16 x zoom); before, once the page had scrolled, it ran up to the masthead's foot (48).
