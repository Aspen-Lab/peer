## 2. The four rules as built

1. **Cluster C.** `HYPHEN_END = /[-\u2010\u2011\u00AD]$/` (a real hyphen only; an en or em dash at a line end keeps its space). `vocabularyOf(lines)` once per document, from every line: the words seen whole, the hyphenated compounds seen whole (their parts count as words), the prefixes seen hyphenated (every part of a compound but the last); a line's last token before a line-end hyphen and the next line's first token are excluded. `mend(current, next, vocab)`, in this order: (1) digit before and digit after: keep, no space; (2) the next line opens with a non-letter: keep; (2b, added) the right token is `and`/`or`/`nor`: a suspended hyphen, keep the hyphen and the space (`intra- and`); (3) no letter run before the hyphen: after a digit keep (`3-dimensional`, `CO2-free`), otherwise join (`(de)-`); (4) the right token holds a digit, two lower-to-upper steps, or opens with a capital: keep; (5) the joined form occurs whole as a word or compound: join; the hyphenated form occurs whole: keep; (6) the right or the left half is not a word of the document: join; (7) a closed prefix (the 22 of the brief) the document never hyphenates: join; (8) else keep, no space. An empty vocabulary falls to "join" at (6): today's behaviour. The caption fold (`foldLines`) and the heading wrap call the same `joinLines`.
2. **Cluster H.** Ahead of the hyphen branch in `joinLines`: a line ending in `/` (not ` /`), in `||`, in U+2212 after a non-space, or in a digit followed by `- U+2010 U+2011 U+2013 U+2014` with a digit opening the next line, joins with no space and the mark kept. Every other line end joins with a space.
3. **Cluster E.** In `joinProse`: `floatGap = bigGap && gap > pitch × 3 && current unfinished (no . ! ? : ; at its end, a closing quote or bracket after one still finishes it) && the next line opens with a lower-case letter, a digit or ( [`; `breaks` ignores `bigGap` when `floatGap`; a capital after the gap, the indent rule and a block still break. `placeFigures` is unchanged.
4. **Cluster F.** `leadingOf` (median same-page gap ≥ 0.6 × body size, over the lines read) and `foldStacks(held, leading, lift)` in `flush()` before `joinProse`. Row = not a block, ≤ 40 characters, no run of 4 letters, a letter or a digit; stack = two or more consecutive same-page rows each ≤ 0.8 × leading below the one before. Display (the line before is finished or absent, or the line after opens with a capital): one line `{ text: blockMarker(k), block: true }` with `equations.push({ text: rows.join("\n") })`. Inline (unfinished before, lower case after): the rows joined by spaces, a `FlowLine` with `continues: true` (an internal optional field on a type extending `PdfLine`; `PdfLine` itself is unchanged) which `joinProse` never breaks at. Sandwich (unfinished before; fragment row, a non-row text row opening with one of `× = + ÷ − · ∑ Σ /`, fragment row, all tight): one `continues` line `numerator / denominator row-text`. Unfinished above and a digit, bracket or nothing below: left as the page set it. Comments amended on `equationOf` and at the P8-03 describe and the "leaves a whole unnumbered equation…" test (rows at the ordinary leading 12, stays green, unchanged).
