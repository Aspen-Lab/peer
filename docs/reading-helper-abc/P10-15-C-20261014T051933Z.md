1. **One intent table** (`reading-map.ts`). `questionIntent(question)` returns `parameter | mechanism | result | background | none`; `ROLE_WEIGHTS[intent][role]` (exported, one table) multiplies a scored leaf row's score in `routeQuestion` (group rows take their best child's score afterwards, as before). The intent is the one with more cue words, a tie is `none`. Two deviations from the letter of the brief, both forced by it:
   - **`questionIntent` takes the question's text, not `specificTerms`.** The route's terms drop `why`, `result(s)`, `show`, `how`, `what` (ROUTE_STOPLIST / `tokenize`'s list), and the brief's own cue words include `why`, `result`, `show`, `how much`, `how many`, `what is`; read from the terms they could never fire. The question's own words are read (letters only, lower-cased, each distinct word once, a form of a cue word counts — `explains`, `governed`, `temperatures`, `achieved`).
   - **"what is" / "what are" count half a word.** As a full word, "What is the mechanism behind the crack resistance …?" (the brief's own mechanism question) is a tie of one mechanism word and one background phrase and so `none`. As a frame worth 0.5, a content word beside it decides, and "What is LCO?" (no other cue) is background. "how much", "how many", "state of the art" count 1.
2. **The slot rule is generalised** (`keepRoleSlot`, from `keepMethodSlot`; the body is P10-06d's, the role test now `favoured.includes(role)`): `favouredRoles(intent)` = the roles of the intent's largest table weight when that is more than 1 (parameter: method + apparatus; mechanism: interpretation; result: evidence; background: setup; none: none). The best leaf row of those roles at ≥ `STRONG_SHARE` × the best that is not strong becomes strong; the cap still three; the lowest-scoring strong `setup` row, else the lowest strong row, becomes medium. Only `tiers` is touched.
3. **The question pass spends its budget by the route** (`deep-report.ts`, `questionSections`; the prompts, the schema and the cache key untouched). It returns the marked sections ordered by the route's **best tier across the questions** (strong, medium, faint; the paper's order within a tier — a stable sort); when `JSON.stringify` of the list is over `PASS1Q_MAX_BODY_CHARS` (the measure `buildQuestionPrompt` uses), the **faint sections are dropped whole from the paper's end, one at a time until it fits, then the medium ones the same way**; a strong section is cut only when the strong sections alone are over, by `buildQuestionPrompt`'s `fitTexts` exactly as before. With no section marked: all sections in the paper's order, as before. Note: the brief's test line says "the medium ones cut only after the faint"; the rule text says "dropped … then medium, before any cut inside a strong section" — built as the rule text: whole sections, never a cut inside a medium one.
   **How the order reaches the model:** `buildQuestionPrompt` serialises `sections: fitted` as a JSON array in the array's order (`fitTexts` keeps the order), and the prompt's keys and each section's `{ id, heading, text }` are unchanged — a test pins both; the model reads the strong sections first.
