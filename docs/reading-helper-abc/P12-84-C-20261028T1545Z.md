## The rules as built

**R3-C (furniture).** A line or a raw drawing box that is wholly in the page's top band (bottom at most `BAND_FURNITURE + 0.5` = 46.5 pt) and at least 20 pt wide is furniture (`isFurniture`; constants `FURNITURE_MIN_WIDTH`, `FURNITURE_BAND_SLACK`, `FURNITURE_CLEARANCE`).
- `absorbFigureText` never takes a furniture line, so the running head is no figure text, nor the end of a chain of lines.
- `renderRegion` takes a `furniture` list (`furnitureInk(texts, boxes, pageRect)`, read in `readPage` from the page's lines and from the boxes `candidateRegions` returns). Ink whose bottom is at or above the region's top and that overlaps the clip's columns ends the clip's top 1 pt under it: `clip top = max(clip top, min(region top, ink bottom + 1))`. A zero-height rule counts (B: `clusterDrawings` drops it, so the boxes, not the clusters, are read). A clip the padding does not bring near the ink is unchanged; so are the figures that start below the band's reach (B's P1/P2/P4: 0 changed).
- Top band only. `BAND_FURNITURE` knows no foot; a running foot is not built (see "For the manager").

**R3-B (edge labels and marks).**
- Lines: the far edge of a figure-text line may be up to 24 pt past a side of the region (`FIGURE_TEXT_EDGE_REACH`, 6 before); the line must still be within 6 pt of the region on each axis (`FIGURE_TEXT_REACH`, unchanged). A join past the old 6 pt must be the nearest captioned figure's and must not take in another captioned region, a typed caption line or a body line (`contested`), so the wider reach does not cross a gutter. A join within the old 6 pt behaves exactly as before.
- Marks: a vector cluster under `PIECE_MIN_AREA` (3,000 pt2), not wider or taller than half the page, within 6 pt of the region on each axis, on the figure's side of the caption, not furniture, passing `contested`, and at most `MAX_FIGURE_MARKS` = 64 for a figure, joins like a line. Marks are the page's clusters (`candidateRegions` passes `smallMarks(clusters, pageRect)`); a rule of no height is no cluster, so it is no mark. Lines and marks join in turn until the region stops growing, so a legend square 16 pt out joins once the rotated labels between it and the figure have.

Built differently from B's table in two places, both on purpose: (1) the mark's reach is the 6 pt near gap, not 24 pt (a 24 pt mark reach also moved three more tests, below, which B did not measure: it is the 6 pt gap that gives B's four); (2) the `contested` guard, the 64-mark cap and the top-band exclusion for marks are additions of mine. They can only remove a join; they were not in B's measurement.
