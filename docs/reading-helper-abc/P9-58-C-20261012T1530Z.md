## 2. The rule, as built

`TERM_TOUCH` (the term button's touch class, `terms-strip.tsx`) gains **`[@media(hover:none)]:min-w-6`**: where there is no hover, a term button is at least 24 px wide, the word centred in it (the button's own `text-align: center`; computed `center`, measured gaps below). Nothing else in the file changed but the constant's comment.

- **24 (`min-w-6`), not 44 (`min-w-11`), and why.** A's sweep draws its line at 24. A 44 px box would push a two-letter word 11 px in from the left edge that the other terms, the quotes under them and the strip's heading line up on (the buttons are alone on their line, left-aligned in a column), and it would widen a pressed highlight to 44 px. 24 moves a two-letter word 2 px (measured 2.09 left, 2.11 right) and no line moves.
- **A floor, not a width.** `min-w-`, so "retention time" (90.45 px) keeps its own width; a fixed `w-6` would clip it (mutation M3).
- **However short.** The rule is on the class every term button carries, so a one-letter term gets it too (a clone of a real term button with the text "R": 9.73 -> 24).
- **The highlight when pressed** keeps P9-52's rule untouched (`py-[11px]`, `bg-clip-content`, `min-h-11`): it is the word's own line tall (23 px in the pixels, the 22.47 px content box at a 0.25 px grid) and its width is the word's or the 24 px floor, whichever is greater (see "For the manager" 1: a word under 24 px has a highlight of 24 px).
- **A mouse sees no change**: the class is behind `[@media(hover:none)]:`, as every other touch floor of the page (P9-23, P9-52). Measured at 1440 and at 390 with a mouse: computed `min-width: 0px`, the box is the word's.

## 7. For the manager

1. **The pressed highlight's width is max(word, 24 px), not always the word's.** The brief asked for the highlight to stay the word's own box through `bg-clip-content`; the clip holds the height (the word's own line, 23 against 44.47) and stops the highlight spreading into P9-52's padding, but the floor is on the box itself, so its content box is 24 wide for a word under 24. For a two-letter term it is 4.2 px wider than the word in this font (about 1.5 in A's 22.5 px case: 0.75 px each side) — it reads as the same chip. For a one-letter term it is a 24 x 23 chip behind a 10 px letter (measured with a clone; Tier 0 terms are at least two characters, so a one-letter term can come only from the model's terms). An exact word-width highlight would need the colour on an inner span (`group-aria-pressed:` on a `<span>` around `{term.term}`) — a markup change in `terms-strip.tsx` outside the `TERM_TOUCH` class the brief names, so I did not make it. If the owner wants it, it is one span in the one file; it would re-aim P9-52's highlight test ("keeps a marked term's highlight to the word's own line ...", which pins `aria-pressed:bg-…` and `bg-clip-content` on the button) and the third test here, so it is not free of test changes.
