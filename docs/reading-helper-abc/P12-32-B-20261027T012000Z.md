## Measurements

In the first twelve isolated cases there are seven declared captions (including the three non-figure types), three declared body references, one intentionally ambiguous line and one absent-caption case. Baseline produces 11 caption candidates, including all three body references and the ambiguous line; the absent case produces none. For each of those 11, the target line and its immediately following continuation leave outline prose. The extracted label is stored separately, and each candidate's exact ordered text after its label and its continuation appear in that order in its caption field (11/11). Thus this is a **placement and classification** finding for those words, not proof that they were lost from the entire document. In the absent case both tested lines remain in body. This check covers these two inventor-specified lines, not all words or document-wide ordering; it does not prove global source-word preservation. Caption continuation consumes the reference's following line as well, so accepting the opening alone can move more words than the label.

The first nine cases with Figure/Fig labels produce reading figures with page 1 and a generated `page=1&fig=N` URL; the absent case produces none. Each Table, Scheme and Chart caption stays typed in outline and produces no reading figure. A URL is only a requested page/number identity, not evidence that an image exists, is correct, or was served. The above and below cases each produce a caption; this does not prove correct association with the rectangle.

The alias case contains a reference and a declared caption on the same page. Outline retains both `Fig 12` and `Figure 12`; reading retains two entries with the same page/number URL. The repeated-label case contains two declared captions, both `Figure 13`: outline retains two, reading retains one. The latter demonstrates that exact-string dedupe can hide a legitimate repeated label; this test does not establish what identity or image should be chosen. Both examples show why spelling normalization and first-candidate-wins are separate decisions from semantic classification. Continuation is stopped by a new typed label in these cases, but the two-column probe has an extraction failure and is not a continuation-boundary proof.

## Three finite choices and limitations

The following compares three stated decision policies on the first twelve isolated cases. Counts exclude the ambiguous example from true/false labels; there are seven declared positive captions, three references, and one absent case. These are offline evaluations of the controlled inputs, not changes run through the app.

1. **Require punctuation following the number.** Six of seven captions pass; one caption without punctuation fails. Two of three references pass; one unpunctuated reference fails. The absent case has no candidate. False positives 2/3 and false negatives 1/7 in this deliberately small set. Punctuation is visible, but neither necessary nor sufficient for semantic intent.
2. **Require a face or size departure from surrounding body.** One of seven captions passes and one of three references passes; six same-style captions are rejected. False positives 1/3 and false negatives 6/7. Here surrounding font is deliberately known from construction; real PDF font identifiers, headings, column differences and partial font runs make determining a reliable body style itself uncertain.
3. **Abstain when evidence does not uniquely identify semantic role, retaining uncertain lines in original prose position and withholding image/page association.** Applied as a conservative representation decision to these deliberately underdetermined candidates, all 11 opening candidates are marked uncertain: 0 of the seven declared captions become eligible and 0 of the three declared references are promoted; the ambiguous case is also retained without a chosen image. This is a desk-evaluated boundary, not an implemented classifier or measured product output. Its cost is seven missed candidate captions; its benefit is no forced false choice in this set. A system must preserve the line and continuation as source-ordered text, and keep candidate provenance separately, rather than claiming their source words are safely reconstructed from a chosen caption. It may later associate an image only on independent, sufficiently specific identity evidence; geometry and typography alone cannot establish the semantics. Missing/ambiguous image association need not prevent text eligibility. This conservative design also cannot magically identify continuation boundaries and must preserve unclaimed text at its original location.

Candidate identity must include type and provenance; `Figure` and `Fig` may normalize to the same typed number only for matching, never erase distinct candidate occurrences. `Table`, `Scheme`, and `Chart` must not become Figure merely through a shared number. A duplicate identity with multiple plausible occurrences must not select the first page or image automatically. When genuine repeated labels exist, distinct occurrences and uncertainty need representation rather than destructive dedupe. No arbitrary source-specific cutoff, threshold widening, cross-page search, or typography-only semantic proof follows.

## Follow-up: independently checked columns and two-page identities

Five new invented PDFs, each two pages, were run through the same unchanged Node 22 pipeline. They use page-specific continuation text so identical phrases on different pages cannot be mistaken for preservation. For all ten pages, independent generator records of each intended line and its intended column-first reading order were compared to `linesOfPageWith`: **416 expected lines, 416 extracted, 416 exact matches in order**. On all ten pages `gutterOf` found a gap (left edge approximately 272–297pt; right edge 325pt); the full-width candidates have x=45 and right reach 415–470pt, crossing the detected gutter. The two-column candidate case has a caption and a separately typed table caption in the left column while the right column carries independent text. This validates the line-order fixture before assessing classification; it is still a deliberately simple synthetic layout, not a universal column guarantee. The previous v2 attempt clipped eight full-width source lines outside the page and is retained; it is not counted as a success. v3 then exposed a duplicated continuation text that could falsely satisfy a cross-page membership check; it is retained, and v4 uses unique page markers. Raw generator, expected ordered lines, probes and output remain under the same external private directory.

The body check compares each distinct expected line to outline body, and tests the relative order of the surviving lines separately per page; it does not concatenate caption and body into a fictitious original stream. All surviving body lines remained in their expected page-relative order. The caption check separately compares the exact source tail after the typed label and the page-specific continuation in the corresponding caption. The baseline places figures in reading section 0 in these fixtures; its generated URLs are recorded as requests, not verified images. Graphic-region eligibility is outside this investigation.

Across these five files, **390 of 416 inventor-specified lines appear in outline body and 26 do not**, as listed by case above. The 14 full-width opening/continuation lines removed in the first four files occur in their corresponding caption tail and continuation; on the column fixture, the eight ordinary lines are inside the Table caption after its intended continuation. Labels are stored as labels, not reproduced as part of the caption tail. These scoped checks establish relocation and one over-join, not a claim of document-wide source loss or exact placement in the reader for every source word. `extractPdfTextFromBytes` preserves the listed caption labels and pages before `buildReading` applies exact-label dedupe.

For an additional finite comparison of the same three **offline policies**, count only the nine typed candidates in these five new fixtures: six declared true captions (including the column Table), two declared body references, one ambiguous. One absent page has no candidate. Requiring punctuation after the number keeps **6/6 captions and 0/2 references** in this particular set; this does not overturn the earlier seven-caption/three-reference set where it yielded one false negative and two false positives. Requiring a departure from body font or size keeps **0/6 captions and 0/2 references**, since all are deliberately same-face/same-size. The all-abstain representation desk choice declares all nine uncertain, promotes **0/6 true captions and 0/2 references**, and makes no page-image choice for the ambiguous candidate. The latter is a conceptual policy evaluation, not an executed product change and not a solved classifier: it sacrifices all six true captions here. Uncertainty handling must keep source-ordered words available without mistaking a caption for body or asserting that an image belongs to it; a subsequent policy needs evidence of association and must still define continuation boundaries. Exact-label normalization alone cannot disambiguate the two alias orderings and must not collapse repeated legitimate occurrences.

These observations leave an irreducible missing observable: the PDF text layer, line positions, and font do not by themselves label intended semantic role or prove a graphic belongs to a caption. Even with independently verified two-column reading, adjacent same-face prose and a caption continuation can be visually indistinguishable to this path. A future C should first receive a manager-ruled representation and a concrete source-position/identity contract; the existing baseline is demonstrably unsafe for an unconditional first-candidate page choice, but the experiments do not justify a universal lexical/typographic/geometry classifier. Preserve source-position text and candidate provenance separately; distinguish text eligibility from figure/image eligibility; avoid guessing an image/page when occurrences conflict. A manager/A real-input check and existing regression/derived-data coordination remain required. No new C scope, threshold, engine change, actual upload, UI test, or acceptance is authorized by these observations.
