## The rule, and what was built

A drag-select that starts in the paper's text column and is released anywhere outside the column ends at the column's nearest edge, at the pointer's height; it never runs to the document's end.

**The fault** (measured, below): the browser ends a drag-select at the place *in the document* of whatever lies under the pointer. Beside the column there is no text of the paper: the margin's empty aside, an open card (the fixed cards stand at the end of the document), the left pane (its title and map come first), the masthead above, the legend and the window's edge below. P11-45's clamp had covered 12 to 23 px past a line's end (the two separators' own handlers) and nothing else.

**As built.**
- `web/src/lib/reader/keep-selection.ts` (new, sha `9f3f95318f31…`): `endSelectionInColumn` (the decision: the selection's end goes to the nearest point of the column's *visible box* to the pointer — x at the column's edge one pixel in when beside it; y at the pointer's, clamped to the first line under the masthead's foot and the window's last row; a caret that is not the column's, because a fixed overlay stands over that row (the legend at the foot, the masthead), is looked for further in, 6 px at a time, 12 tries); `selectionKeeper` (the controller: a **mouse's primary press that began in the paper's text** is followed until the button is released or the pointer cancelled; each move and the release ask for one correction at the next animation frame — three moves in a frame make one — after the browser's own move of the end and before the paint); `domEnv`, `watchSelection` (four capture-phase listeners on the document: pointerdown, move, up, cancel) and `useKeepSelection(grid)`.
- `web/src/components/reader/reader-shell.tsx` (+5): the import and the hook's one call, `useKeepSelection(grid)`, beside `useKeepPlace(grid)`. base `85c398742ee0…` → `f89bbaa7b0b4…`.
- **The separators: folded in.** `pane-divider.tsx` is back to what it was before P11-45's addition, byte for byte (sha `40358351810d…`): no selection handling, no `endSelectionInColumn` export. The document's one handler owns the rule; a pointer over a separator is a pointer outside the column. Its own drag (the press on it is not "in the text", so it is never followed), keys, focus and 12-px hit area are untouched (measured, below).
- **Why a new file and not `keep-place.ts`:** that file keeps the *scroll place* through a re-flow (a ResizeObserver, a `scrollBy`); this one keeps the *selection's end* through a drag (document pointer events, `Selection.extend`). They share the column's selector and nothing else; each is one call in the shell. `keep-place.ts` is not changed.
- **What is left to the browser:** a press that did not begin in the text (a card's own text, the notes editor, the left pane's title, the map, a separator); a press on a control inside the column (a link, button, field, summary, label, editable block, draggable, and their roles); a touch, a pen, a secondary button; a selection that did not begin in the column; a collapsed one; a pointer over the column's visible box. The notes tab keeps its own pass-through (`notes-button.tsx`, not touched). No `pointer-events` rule, no `preventDefault`: the means is to give the selection's end, in a frame, and nothing else.
- Not touched: `selection-toolbar.tsx`, `paper-body.tsx`, `globals.css`, `notes-button.tsx`, every other component.
