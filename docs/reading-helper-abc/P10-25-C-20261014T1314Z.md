## What was built (`web/src/components/notes/note-editor.tsx` only; `lib/notes/blocks.ts`, `wysiwyg.ts`, the toolbar, the sticky, the store untouched)

- **Rule 1.** `holdsItsType(b)` = an `h3` that carries an `anchor` (exactly what `anchorMap` reads). For such a block: Backspace at its start does nothing (as at a note's first block); the block menu has no "Turn into"; the toolbar's heading / bullets / numbers do nothing (`applyType`); the `/` menu does not open in it (and a pick that arrives anyway only takes the `/` back out); a paste over all of its words keeps the block (the old branch replaced the block by the parsed one, dropping type and anchor); **Delete at the end of the block before it does not join it**. One more guard under all of them: `retype` (below) refuses any type change on it. Its words stay editable; **the paper's tag reads the anchor's `n`, not the heading's words** (pinned by a test: a renamed heading still gives tag 2 on its passage). Delete in the block menu and the paper's × remain the ways to remove a sticky.
- **Rule 2.** A block's undo steps now carry its shape (`type`, `anchor`, `checked`, `indent`). `wysiwyg.ts`'s `recordState` folds a change that leaves the words alone into the step before it (the old type would be lost) and `HistoryState` is not mine, so `recordShape` (exported, pure) pushes a step of its own and the steps carry the extra `shape` field; `remember` stamps every step with the block's shape; `stepHistory` writes `shapePatch(step.shape)` with the words. Every type change goes through `retype`: Backspace demotion of a heading / list, Enter on an empty list item, the block menu's Turn into, the toolbar, the Markdown shortcut (`# `, `- `, … — undo gives back the typed `# ` as text), the slash pick on an empty block.
- **Rule 3.** Nothing else changed: the surface, the hidden title block, the numbering, the exports.
