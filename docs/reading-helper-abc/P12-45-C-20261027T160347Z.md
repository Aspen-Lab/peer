## The rule as built

`readPageStructure` collects its lines as before, then calls `joinLabelOnlyLines(texts)` and sorts the result (the only changed lines of the existing function: the `sort` and the `return`). The new exported helper sits directly under it:

- A **label-only line** is one whose whole text matches `^\s*fig(?:ure)?\.?\s*\d+[a-z]?\s*[.:|]?\s*$` (case-insensitive): "Fig. 1", "Figure 2", "Fig. 3a", "Figure 4:", "Figure 5 |", "FIG. 6.", "Fig.7". "Fig. 3 shows the result", "Figure 4 and 5", "Fig. 3a, b", "Table 1" are not.
- It merges with the line **of the same MuPDF block** (same `blockId`) that is **on the same row** (vertical overlap at least half the shorter line's height) and **starts within 12 pt to its right** (`body.x0 - label.x1` from -1 to 12; the -1 absorbs sub-point glyph-box overlap). Of several, the nearest wins; a body line is used by one label only.
- The result is one `TextBlock`: union rectangle, text "label body", the body line's `blockId`, in the body line's place; the label line is dropped.
- Narrower than B's wording in three places, all to keep "nothing else merges": a body line that is itself a label-only line, a typed caption (`CAPTION_RE`) or a Table/Scheme/Chart caption (`OTHER_CAPTION_RE`) is never the body (so "Fig. 1" beside "Fig. 2. Right caption." stays two lines); lines of other blocks never merge; and at most `MAX_LABEL_ONLY_LINES = 200` label-only lines per page are examined, so a hostile page costs a bounded amount (the lines past the 200th stay as they were). The inner search runs only inside the label's own block.
- Nothing else changed: the floors, region growth, clip, caption merge (`mergeCaptionLines`), `bestCaptionForRegion`, the route.

`FIGURE_EXTRACTION_REVISION` is "4" (was "3") in the same commit.
