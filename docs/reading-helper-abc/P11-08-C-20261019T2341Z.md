## 2. The rules, what each is, and its tests

### 2.1 Rule 1 — the row (`highlight-row.tsx`; `highlight-row.test.tsx`, 37 tests)

- `HighlightRowView` (markup, no state): a 14-px square in the highlight's fill (`aria-label="Go to the passage"`, a button that goes through `goToPassage({ paperId, paragraphId, highlightId })` and flashes the marks); the words on their colour (`highlightPaint(colour, 0)`: fill and the better ink), `line-clamp-2` so two lines at most and an ellipsis where cut (the whole passage stays in the DOM and in the tooltip); a colour button named by the colour's name (`aria-label="Yellow"`, `aria-expanded`) that opens P11-07's `HighlightSwatches` under the row; a ×. A passage the paper no longer holds shows `PASSAGE_LOST_LINE` and no go-to square (`useLostPassages` keyed by the block id, and `storedBodyOf` for the notes page, which has no paper on show); an inert quiet square holds the square's place (the P10-36 precedent) so the rows keep their line. A block with no readable highlight shows "This highlight has no words." and a ×.
- `HighlightRow` (connected): the lost state, the open state of the swatches, the router for the notes page's go-to. `watchOutsideClick` closes the swatches on a click elsewhere (see 2.1a), Escape closes them first (not the window) and gives the focus back to the colour button, and opening puts the focus on the current swatch (a radio group: arrows move the choice).
- The editor (`note-editor.tsx`): `Row` draws `<HighlightRow>` for `b.type === "highlight"`, so the notes window and `/notes/<id>` (both draw the paper's note with the one `NoteEditor`) have it. The colour button and the × act on the editor's own copy of the note (`withHighlightColour`; the × is `blockAction(id, "delete")`, with the "Block deleted · undo" line), which the editor saves half a second later with the rest; the row never writes under the editor. `blockAccepts(type, action)`: a highlight takes Delete and nothing else — `BlockMenu` (exported) draws Delete alone for it, and `blockAction` returns early for any other action, so Duplicate (a second mark over the same words), turn-into and Move leave a highlight alone. The slash menu, the toolbar and `applyType` only act on textual blocks and never saw one.
- Tests: the four parts and their order; each preset's square / words / ink from the table (the Ink preset's words white); two lines and the whole words kept; the colour button, its name, `aria-expanded`; the handlers (square, button, ×), the mouse-down guard that keeps the page's selection; the swatches open (six radios, one checked, a pick recolours); the lost line and no go-to square; the malformed block (never throws); the connected row's first render; `goToHighlight` on fake elements (scroll, flash of this highlight's own and part marks and no other, `FLASH_MS`, the navigate fallback); `highlightIsLost` (held, moved, gone, told); `withHighlightColour` (changes one block, every other the same object, the same array for a no-op); `blockAccepts` and the rendered `BlockMenu` (Delete alone for a highlight; all for a text block); the editor's wiring read from its source; `watchOutsideClick`.
- 2.1a A defect found in the browser and fixed in `65f8e624`: the first version closed the open swatches on `pointerdown`. With the swatches open under one row, a press on another row's square or × closed them, the rows below moved up before the release, and the click landed on nothing (the notes page's go-to square did nothing). `watchOutsideClick` closes on the `click` in the capture phase; its test pins `click` + capture and no earlier event.

### 2.2 Rule 2 — the exports (`export.ts`; `export.highlight.test.ts`, 28 tests)

- Markdown: its own paragraph, `==words==`. Plain text and Copy (both `toPlainText`): its own paragraph, the words. Word and print: `<p><mark style="background:<fill>;color:<ink>">words</mark></p>`, `;mso-highlight:yellow` for Yellow only. A highlight with no readable words writes nothing; an unknown stored colour reads as Yellow (`readHighlight`).
- A sticky's quote and a `quote` block stay out: `export.anchor.test.ts`, `export.quote.test.ts` and `export.formats.test.ts` pass unchanged, and a mixed note (a sticky, a quote block, a highlight) is tested in all four formats. The header comment of `export.ts` and the one comment above `EXPORT_ITEMS` say so (and are pinned by the test).
- Beyond the brief's `==words==`: a star, an underscore and an equals sign in the paper's words are written with the notes dialect's own escapes (`\*`, `\_`, `\=`), so `a==b` does not end the mark early and the words come back from `parseInline` as one mark; plain words are unchanged. Whitespace in the words is collapsed to one line.
- Tests: Markdown (between blocks, every colour, a highlights-only note has a body, order among stickies, the escapes and the round trip, one line, nothing for no words); plain text (the words, no `==`, Copy and .txt are `toPlainText` by the source, nothing for no words); Word and print, both (the exact paragraph, each preset's fill and ink from the table, `mso-highlight` iff Yellow, Ink white, the words escaped, the unknown colour, the inline highlight of a text block unchanged); the sticky's quote and the quote block's words absent while the highlight's are present, in all four; no place of the highlight in the paper in any; the two comments.

### 2.3 Rule 3 — the window's merge keeps a highlight (I11; `notes-window.merge.test.tsx`, +13 tests)

- The cases: a highlight added from the paper while the window holds unsaved words survives, with the words typed (at the end, between two blocks, several, and with a block the window added); a highlight recoloured from the paper keeps the new colour over the stale copy's old one, whatever else the window did (retyped one block, deleted another); a highlight both sides left alone is the same object; a highlight the paper removed does not come back from the stale copy; a stale copy that never had a highlight does not remove it; `reconcileWindow` says `merge` (words unsaved) or `restart` (nothing unsaved) and the merge holds the highlight; and three cases through the real store (`addHighlight`, `setHighlightColour`, and the window's whole write: its stale flush then the merge stored — the highlight and its colour are in the stored note).
- Result: all 13 are green on the head, so `mergeBlocks` / `reconcileWindow` need no change and have none: a highlight's `text` is empty and the window never edits one, so a block only the paper added or changed is already the paper's (`mergeBlock` takes the side that changed the marks, the outside's where both did). Mutations R3a and R3b (below) show the cases can fail.
