## 2. The rule, as built

`MattedFigure` runs the caption it is handed through `figureCaption` (exported from `matted-figure.tsx`) once, and uses that one string for the `<figcaption>`, the picture's `alt`, the lightbox's caption and P9-57's error path (the caption alone, once, no alt). Nothing else in the component changed.

- **The room: `FIGURE_CAPTION_CHARS = 500`, the figure engine's own cap, and why.** The engine's caption is a hard `.slice(0, MAX_FIGURE_TEXT_CHARS)` (`pdf-engine.ts`: `mergeCaptionLines` and the generic-block path, after `normalizeText`). The cap cannot move without a figure-request `v` bump (BACKLOG-60), and `isSameFigure` compares captions exactly, so the view takes the cap as its room: **a caption that reaches 500 may be the engine's slice; one below it never was.** It is also a fair room for a figure under a paragraph of the report (a short paragraph of its own), against the lead's 140 for the one line beside a title. A test reads both constants out of the two source files and fails if they differ.
- **Whole, as it is.** A caption that fits the room (squeezed length <= 500) and ends on a real stop (a stop with its closing quote or bracket) is printed as it is, white space made single. So is a **caption that fits and ends on no stop but is shorter than the engine's cut** (raw length < 500): it was never sliced, it ends where its author ended it ("Figure 2: Overview of the sampling design"), and cutting those would be a regression. A caption already ending in "…" is its own visible cut and is left alone.
- **Cut at a sentence end.** A caption the engine may have sliced (raw length >= 500, ending on no stop) or one longer than the room is cut to the longest run of whole sentences that fits in 500, not otherwise marked: the stop is the end (the lead's rule: "the stop is the end"). A leading "Figure 4." is a label, not a sentence; "Fig.", "et al.", "e.g.", "vs.", an initial ("J. R.") are not sentence ends either.
- **Visibly cut.** If there is no whole sentence in the room, it is cut at a word with one "…" and no stop, comma, colon or dash in front of it; the result is at most 500 characters. A slice whose 500th character was the space after a word (the engine normalizes, then slices, so it can) keeps that word, since it is whole.
- **Blank is none** (as the lead's): a caption of only white space draws no `<figcaption>` and no empty alt; on the error path it draws nothing (the head drew an empty figcaption and its margin).
- **Idempotent**: printing the printed caption changes nothing (a sentence-cut ends on a stop within 500; a word-cut ends in "…" within 500).

## 7. For the manager

1. **`leadCaption` could not be reused as a function, only as a rule.** Its room is the constant `LEAD_CAPTION_CHARS = 140` (not a parameter) and `sentenceEnds` is private to `paper-plate.tsx`, which is not mine to change. So `matted-figure.tsx` carries a copy of `ABBREVIATION`, `LABEL_WORD` and `sentenceEnds` (about 25 lines) and its own `figureCaption`; `matted-figure.caption.test.tsx` imports `leadCaption` and holds the two to the same rule on the cases that tell them apart (a label, an abbreviation, an initial; the same real sentence end). **A shared helper would remove the copy** (e.g. `paper-plate.tsx` exporting `sentenceEnds`, or a `captionWithin(text, room)` both call): it needs that file, so it is left to you.
2. **Reading of "one that does not end on a stop — the engine's slice —".** I took the engine's slice to be a caption that reaches the cap (raw length >= 500) and ends on no stop; a shorter caption with no stop is left whole (rule 2, M2). If you want every no-stop caption cut, that clause is the one line (`mayBeSliced`), but the cost is real: "Figure 2: Overview of the sampling design" would read "Figure 2: Overview of the sampling…". The raw length (before squeezing) is what is compared, because the engine's slice can end on a space that squeezing removes (499 after trim).
3. **A sentence-end cut drops the last, partial sentence whole**, as the lead's does: the measured slice goes from 500 to 361 characters. The better fix remains the one the walk named: carry the whole caption from the engine (a `v` bump, BACKLOG-60, and a look at `isSameFigure`) and let the view cut it at 500; this view then cuts a whole caption the same way (the 2,000 row).
4. **`isSameFigure` is untouched and still right**: it compares the plate's and the bound caption as stored, and `figureCaption` runs only at print, so both sides still compare the engine's raw strings.
5. **Two small changes beyond the cut itself, both the lead's behaviour:** white space made single in the printed caption (the browser collapsed it in the figcaption anyway; the alt and the lightbox now read the same collapsed string), and a blank caption is none (no empty figcaption; on the error path no empty figure and margin). Say if either should go back to the raw string.
6. **Not measured in pixels:** no dev server was run (the brief's measurement is the string). A cut caption is shorter than the engine's 500, so the mat under the Summary's figure is a little shorter than the A walk's 632x505 at 1440; no width or other class changed. A walk of the keyed Summary would show it.
7. **The stop rule inherited from the lead**: a stop is a sentence end only if a capital, a digit, a quote or a bracket follows it, so a caption whose sentences run on in lower case after a stop ("... layer. beneath ...") is treated as one sentence and gets a word cut. Same as under the lead plate; noted so it is not a surprise.
