## The rules as built

1. **Display keeps the glyphs (D).** In `clean.ts`: the six folds (`× → x`, `± → +/-`, `≤ → <=`, `≥ → >=`, `µ → micro`, `° → " degrees"`) are deleted from `normalizeUnicodeSymbols`; the entity map gives `times → ×`, `plusmn → ±`, `le → ≤`, `ge → ≥`, `micro → μ` (U+03BC), `deg → °`; LaTeX `\times` and `\pm` give `×` and `±`; the mojibake repairs give `°` and `±`. U+00B5 still becomes U+03BC, now by one explicit replace at the end of `normalizeUnicodeSymbols` (the old `µ → micro` line was dead code: NFKC had already made it a Greek mu). `Σ Å ∼ ≫` are untouched, as before; `ohm.cm` is the paper's own spelling, nothing done.
2. **The matcher folds instead.** `evidence.ts` gets `foldDisplaySymbols` (`× → x`, `± → +/-`, `≤ → " <= "`, `≥ → " >= "`, `° → " degrees"`), applied in `normalizeForMatch`. Two choices that differ from the brief's wording, both to keep the matching alphabet *identical* to today's:
   - it runs **right after the matcher's own `.normalize("NFKC")`** (which itself follows `cleanDisplayText`), not between the two, because NFKC is what turns `℃` / `℉` (U+2103 / U+2109) into `°C` / `°F`; the old cleaner folded them to `degreesC` / `degreesF` after its own NFKC, and a fold placed before the matcher's NFKC would miss them;
   - the comparison signs are padded (`\s*≤\s*` → `" <= "`) because the old fold ran *before* the cleaner's operator spacing, so `a≤b`, `a ≤ b` and `a <= b` all met at `a <= b`; a bare `≤ → <=` would have split them (`a<=b` against `a <= b`).
3. **Scripts survive (G's gate).** `.normalize("NFKC")` is now `.normalize("NFC")` plus three named constants: `LIGATURES` (U+FB00–FB06 → `ff fi fl ffi ffl st st`; U+FB05 is the long-s t ligature and reads `st`), `SPACE_LIKE` (U+00A0, U+2000–200A, U+202F, U+205F, U+3000 → one space; the `[ \t]+` collapse at the end merges a run; U+200B stays out, the matcher folds it itself), `FULLWIDTH_ASCII` (U+FF01–FF5E → ASCII by code-point offset). The raw ellipsis (U+2026) is spelled `...` by one replace, as NFKC did and as the `hellip` entity and the mojibake repair already do (the brief did not name it; I kept today's behaviour rather than change a feed title's look). The two script replaces (old l.173–174) and the now-unused `SUPERSCRIPT_MAP` are removed; `SUBSCRIPT_MAP` stays because `toPlainSubscript` (the `$_{…}$` LaTeX path) reads it. NFC keeps the canonical singletons: the Angstrom and Ohm signs (U+212B, U+2126) still become `Å` and `Ω`, and `e` + U+0301 still composes. `½` stays `½` (today `12`). The U+2044 fraction-slash tests stay green, untouched.
4. **The capital-plus-digit join narrowed (H; BACKLOG-19).** `/(?<=[A-Za-z])([A-Z])\s+(\d)(?![.,]\d)(?=\b|[A-Z),.:;])/g` → `$1$2`: the capital must follow a Latin letter (the tail of a formula word) and the digit must not open a decimal. Pinned: `CO 2` → `CO2` (joins); `WAS 0.4`, `A 3D`, `A 0.25 mm`, `Figure S 1`, `Type A 2.5 mm` keep their space. One loss I accept because the brief rules it: a spaced-out formula whose capital *starts* a word (`Ti 3 C 2`) now loses its second join (`Ti 3 C 2` stays, where it was `Ti 3 C2`).
5. **Braces stay outside LaTeX.** `normalizeLatex` strips `{` `}` only when the text carries a backslash anywhere (`text.includes("\\")`, checked on the input); the `_{…}` / `^{…}` folds are unchanged and run regardless. `{003}`, `the {003} plane` and `{110} ≫ {101} > {104} ≫ {003}` keep their braces; `\mathbf{x}`, `\text{rate} of $\mathrm{Li}_{2}$O` still lose theirs. The gate is the whole text: `{ab} and \mathbf{x}` → `ab and x` (the braces of a text that has a LaTeX command go with it, as before).
6. **Curly quotes and dashes are left exactly as they are** (`‘’ → '`, `“” → "`, every dash and the minus sign → `-`): not in the owner's complaints, not touched. The em dash still becomes `-`; B P9-02 §5 item 3 flagged it, and it stays the manager's call.
