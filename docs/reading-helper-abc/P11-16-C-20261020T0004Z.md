## The rules, as built

1. **The page uses the shell from 1024.** `reader-layout.tsx`'s spread branch is `<ReaderShell left={{ fixed, scroll }} centre>`; below 1024 the one column is the branch it was, with one addition: `{p.details}` straight after `{p.decision}` (the decision block is two slots now). One column is geometry-identical to the base: 124 text-bearing elements, every rect equal at 1023 × 768, 768 × 1024 and 390 × 844, document height equal (be181842 against this tree, the invented paper, `h/m-dom.mjs`). The DOM order is the visual order; each block is built once and appears once.
2. **The left pane (D3).** Fixed zone: the reading controls, the title (4 lines, the whole in `title=`), the commands, the strip. Scroll zone (the pane's one scroller): the map, then the plate, then the details (sentence, private-PDF line, DOI, progress). A paper with no strip and no map has no zones (`whole`: the pane scrolls as one). `DecisionBlock` takes `part="commands" | "details"` (no `part`: the whole block, as before). `TitleBlock` takes `pane` (clamp, the pane's size at every width, a tighter top). `ReadingControls` takes `spread` (Fit enabled, no top gap; it no longer asks `useSpread`, so the layout imports it without a cycle). `reading-map.tsx`: the `xl:` scroller is gone. The blocks' own gaps are tightened by the layout's wrappers (commands 20 → 12 px, strip 32 → 16, map 24 → 16), not in the blocks; each zone gives its contents 6 px of focus-ring room (`px-1.5`): the zones clip, and the ring is 2 px + 3 px offset (screenshot checked, all four sides show).
3. **The centre.** `common` (the question bar and the answers), the active tab's blocks, Next; a flex column as the old reading column was, the first block's top gap dropped; `scroll-mt-20` kept on the common blocks and the tabpanel. The 38 em cap and the centring are the shell's.

## Found, not fixed (not my files)

- **Escape in a peek also leaves the paper** (28 of 28 runs: the URL goes to `/`). The shell's peek handler (`reader-shell.tsx`, the `document` keydown) closes the peek but neither `preventDefault`s nor `stopPropagation`s, so `keyboard.tsx:306`'s `window` handler runs "Esc = back to the briefing". `explain-box.tsx` (the `onKeyDown` near line 894) does both. The fix is two lines in the shell.
- **A first visit below 1144 css px starts with the left pane as the rail** (the shell's D6), so at 1024 and 1093 the title, the commands, the strip and the map are behind the rail until the reader presses it or rests on it. The pinned numbers are above.
- **The reading controls are not in the pane's header row**: the shell gives its header no slot, so they open the fixed zone on a row of their own, under the pin. A `header?: ReactNode` on `ReaderShell` (after the pin button) would take them up into that row, give the map another 44 px, and remove the 19 px cut at 1093 × 614.
- **The progress bar and its status line are in the details, at the foot of the scroll zone**, as the brief puts the rest of the block. While a model report generates they are below the map (14 rows on the owner's paper), not on screen at any scored width; before, they stood in the panel's head. A third part (`progress`) at the scroll zone's top would keep them in view.
- `spread.ts`'s `PANEL_CLASS`, `PANEL_STICKY_CLASS`, `PANEL_TAIL_CLASS` and `COLUMN_CLASS` have no importer now (and `spread.test.ts` pins them); `SPREAD_GRID` is still the loading mat's and the error states'. `page.tsx` has comments above `useSpread()` that still say "≥ xl" (outside my hunk).
