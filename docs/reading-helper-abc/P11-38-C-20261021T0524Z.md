## 2. The rules as built

1. **A press puts the group away.** The button's press (`onExplain`, then the group's own state) and `e` (the thumb bar's cell is the same action, but under 768 px there is no group) end the group's job; it stays gone until the page hands a different selection object, or a drag that changed the selection leaves one standing (the same words dragged again, before the page has told anyone the first went; a double click). A press that changes no selection (a button, a tab), a press inside a card and a click that collapses the selection do not end the wait. `e` is seen as the Explain card now holding the selection's passage (the card's header text, read with the cards at each stack change), not as a key. **I chose to end the selection with the press** (the browser's ranges removed, as Take notes and Highlight do): first I left it drawn as the card's quote, but P11-40's Escape counts a selection as standing while the page holds `explainTarget`, so a quiet selection would have taken the first Escape. The card keeps its quote and keeps level with its paragraph (the page's selection measures its own copy of the range; measured: card −5 px from the paragraph's first line before and after a 120-px scroll, 0 px apart). Margin only: under 1024 px the pill stays after the press, and the selection with it.
2. **The group never covers a card's header.** `openCards(document)` reads, in one query by the margin's marker, each card's rect, its header (the card's first element: a sticky's, the notes window's and the figure's 40-px row; the explain card's **X, title and quote block**, 122 px at 1440; the figure's zoom row as a second header) and what a header holds (an element with text of its own, a control with none; not the raise button's empty width). `clearOfHeaders` makes the place clear: **level** where the box is over no header; else **on the header's free part**, level with the line, where it fits whole between what the header holds at that height (4 px from each part); else **just below the header** (below the last of a stack of them); else, with no room below in the band, **just above** (my addition, for the foot of the band); else held (flagged). It never leaves the margin sideways (left within margin + 8 … margin − 8), so the centre column's words stay uncovered. `data-header-clear` ("free", "below", "above", "held") is on the group when it was moved. The strip uses the same function (`stripStanding` takes the headers; the host reads them as it places the strip) and now also measures again when the stack of cards changes (a frame is asked for, as for a scroll).
3. **Below 1024 nothing changes.** No margin: the pill above the selection, headers ignored, the press leaves the pill and the selection alone; under 768 px no group (the thumb bar). Pinned by green guards.

## 6. Unsettled

- **The explain card's header is its X, title *and quote*** (the block the card draws over a bottom border, 122 px at 1440): the group never covers the quote's first words either, so a selection at the card's top is moved up to 118 px. Taken as the header's X and title row alone it would move less and cover the quote's head (the walk's X-5 named it). The manager may narrow it (one selector).
- **A line just above a sticky's header** (25–40 px) moves the group to the header's foot, 75 px, more than the header is tall; in a stack the move is the sum of the headers. "Just above the header" would move less there; I kept the brief's "free part, else just below" and used "above" only where there is no room below.
- **The free part is rare and overlaps the header's box** (never what it holds): 32 of 386 selections, beside the figure's Fit button and a card behind another. A reader who measures the header's box, not what it holds, will count them.
- **`e` is seen, not heard:** the toolbar notices the Explain card holding the passage on the next frame, so the group can stand one frame after `e` before it goes; a selection that is a part of the passage the card already explains is not noticed (the button's press always is).
- The strip carries no data attribute (`highlight-strip.tsx` is not mine); the pictures and the rects are the evidence.
- The inline comment above the `explainable` line still says Explain this is not offered for a heading or a caption: left to P11-42 with the line.
