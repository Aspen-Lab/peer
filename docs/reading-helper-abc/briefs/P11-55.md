## The defect

A blind reader of round 4's Theme A pack (`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r4a/`, read-only; the shots `X4-textstep-1024x768-light-paper-step7.png` and its 1093 twin) costed it 0.25.

At text step 1.6, on the 416-px column (1024 × 768 and 1093 × 614), the empty question bar's placeholder ("What should this paper answer?") wraps. The bar stays one row by design (`fitHeight`: "An empty bar is one row, whatever its placeholder"). Its second line is therefore cut through the middle at the bar's bottom border: half a line of glyphs is visible.

The textarea is `font-reading text-body`, which the text steps have scaled since 6-09, so the placeholder grows with the step.

## The rule (ruling §1h.160)

1. **An empty bar shows no clipped line.** At every text step, at every window from 390 up, an empty bar shows its placeholder either:
   - whole on its one row, or
   - on one row cut cleanly at its end with an ellipsis.

   No part of a second line may be visible. Choose the means and say why. For example:
   - the placeholder kept to one line (`::placeholder` with `white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis`, if Chromium honours it on a `textarea`; measure it);
   - or the bar growing to the placeholder's height while empty.

   Prefer the one-row look, which is the bar's design (P10, review 2.2). The gist placeholder (`ASK.chips.gist`) obeys the same rule.
2. **Nothing else changes.** All of these stay as they are:
   - a typed question's own growth (`fitHeight` for a non-empty value);
   - the Submit control;
   - the dropdown;
   - the bar's 48-px minimum at rest;
   - the text steps' scaling of a typed question;
   - the placeholder at scale 1 wherever it fits today.
