## The rules (ruling §1h.149)

1. **No control is covered.** The group and the strip never cover any control of a card, wherever the control stands. That means:
   - the header row;
   - the notes window's formatting row;
   - a sticky's buttons;
   - the figure card's zoom row;
   - an Explain card's input and its buttons.

   Read a card's controls from the DOM (`button`, `[role=button]`, `input`, `textarea`, `[contenteditable]` toolbars), not from a list of selectors that can go stale. Say how you read them.
2. **The free part counts only if the group fits there.** The header's free part is a place only if the whole group fits inside the header row. Otherwise the candidates are:
   - just above the header;
   - just below the header (below the last header of a stack);
   - level, over the card's body, where that covers no control.

   Choose by the smallest move from the line. Over the body, prefer the place that covers the fewest of the card's words. Report the words covered before and after.
3. **Truly nearest.** The move is the nearest clear place the band allows. r2's `excess` cases must reach 0 excess, or you must say why a nearer place is not clear.
4. **Nothing else changes.** The level place with no card at that height is byte for byte as today. So are B2's floor, below 1024, and rule 1 of C P11-38 (the group put away after Explain).
