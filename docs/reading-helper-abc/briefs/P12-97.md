## The rules (the B's package, in this order, one commit each)

1. **R-F, the running foot.**
   - The foot's key ignores which side of the page the folio stands on.
   - The repeat threshold is 40 % of the pages that carry text (15 or more lines), not of all pages.
   - This lands first: without it, the figure rules fold the feet into real paragraphs.
2. **R-B, a small line may be longer.** A line set below 0.95 of the body size counts as a panel line up to 60 characters. At body size the limit stays 25.
3. **R-A, the stretch to the caption.**
   - Today a run of panel lines counts as figure text only when it lies within 8 body sizes of a caption on the same page. The new test: the run is beside a caption when only non-body lines lie between them.
   - A body line is one within 5 % of the body size and of 40 or more characters.
   - The 8-body-size reach stays as one way to be beside a caption; the stretch is the other.

Not built: R-A "any caption on the page", R-C, runs of 2, R-Box. Each moves pinned tests or needs the engine; the B lists them as options.
