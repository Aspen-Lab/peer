## The rule (B P12-80 K1, R1-B)

A caption may change face **once**, anywhere in it, held to the caption's own leading (the gap measured between the caption's own lines, not `linePitch`'s page-wide percentile). After its first line it may change face only to a line set smaller than the body.

This is the cause B found: the bold title run is the line's longest run, so the title's line counts as bold, and the regular line after it reads as another face and ends the caption. It cuts P3's Fig. 1 and Fig. 6 and six captions on P4 and P5.

## Measure (invented input only)

Build captions:
- a bold title run longer than its regular tail, wrapped onto a second line in the regular face;
- a bold title wrapped onto two lines, then the regular face at a smaller size;
- negatives:
  - a caption followed by a body paragraph in the body face and size, which must still end;
  - two face changes in a caption after its first line, at body size, which must end at the second.

Show each caption's length before and after. B measured 0 of 1,586 tests moving; any test that moves is to be named.
