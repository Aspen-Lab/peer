## The symptom

B P12-40: **O5** — at the first end-matter heading `buildOutline` sets `done` and discards everything after it; a paper whose Methods and a second back-matter block come **after** the references (P3's journal) loses them, and a paper whose supporting-information line comes first loses its acknowledgements and statements. **O6** — the paper starts at its first named part (abstract or introduction); a paper that names neither has no row for its unheaded introduction. **O8** — figure-panel text (axis labels, panel letters, short tick strings) becomes rows and prose paragraphs.

## The rules

1. **O5.** An end-matter heading no longer ends the outline: after it, a heading that names an argument part (methods above all) or another end-matter part starts its own section, and reading continues; the reference entries themselves stay out of the argument's prose as they do today.
2. **O6.** In a paper that names no abstract or introduction, the **body-style lines before the first heading, after the cover**, are emitted as the first section with **no heading** (the interface above). The cover — title, authors, affiliations, a summary set in another size or face — is cut as today. A paper that names its introduction is unchanged (test it byte-identical).
3. **O8.** A run of three or more short lines next to a figure caption on the same page — each short (about 25 characters or less), not ending in sentence punctuation, no word of 4+ letters or set smaller than the body — is figure text: it joins no section's prose and is never a heading. **Required negative test:** the short last line of a body paragraph stays in its paragraph.
4. Nothing else changes: captions, headings, columns, the paragraph joins.
