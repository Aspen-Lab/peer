## The questions (each answered with the code path — file, function, line — and the evidence from the paper's lines)

1. **Cluster A — a line whose first glyphs are superscripts or subscripts becomes a line of its own, so the paragraph breaks there and the base line loses its exponents.** Examples in `whole.txt`: "10-15 disconnections…", "33-35 ceramic…", "37,38 group H2O.", "2 2 density (from 15 mA/cm to 30 mA/cm)…", "0-4 μm 4-8 μm coarse crystallite size…", "2 2 st th resistance RCT (43.3 ohm.cm …)", "-13 -14 2 st th diffusivity (4.62 x 10 to 2.44 x 10 cm /s…", "8-15 μm observation…", "2 2 x 3.5 cm and 4 x 3.5 cm", "- due to the repulsion…", "-1 (x = 1-0.95) to 565 cm…", "64-67 been well-studied." Read `web/src/lib/papers/pdf-text.ts` (`linesOfPageWith`, `Row`, `raised`, how a raised glyph is folded into a row) and `pdf-outline.ts` (`CITATION_MARK`, `CITED`, the paragraph-start rules: indent, short line). Show with the dumped rows of p.1, p.3, p.11, p.16 of the paper what the extractor sees for three of the examples: the raised glyphs' y, size, x-range against the base line's. Establish exactly why the raised-only row is not folded (the fold needs base glyphs in the row? the baseline difference exceeds the tolerance? the order of rows?). Then design the fix: fold a row made only of raised/lowered glyphs into the base row it belongs to by x-overlap and vertical proximity (which direction: the base row below for superscripts at a line start? above for subscripts?); within the folded row, drop a citation run (digits with commas/ranges after a word, a sentence end or a bracket), attach an exponent to its base token (after a digit or a unit) as Unicode superscripts (², ⁻¹³, ¹ˢᵗ → keep "1st"/"80th" as plain ordinals), attach a subscript label (after a variable letter such as f, TC) as Unicode subscripts where available else plain. Say which existing tests pin the current behaviour and which would be re-aimed (name them).
2. **Cluster B — citation numbers fused into words**: "morphology-2 (crystal size…", "intra-5,6 and intercrystal", "Li30 and Zn", "NaCoO235", "Bi2Te352", "basal a-b plane 003 and the slowest" (is "003" a citation "3" plus the plane "00"? find the rows). Why does the P7-11 rule (`CITED`, the period test) miss them? Design the rule: a raised digit run (with commas, en-dash ranges) is a citation when it follows a letter, a closing bracket, a comma or a hyphen with no space; an exponent when it follows a digit, a unit (cm, m, s, K, V, mA), a closing parenthesis of a number, or "10". Give the regex and the cases.
3. **Cluster G — chemistry and variable sub/superscripts flattened**: "Co(OH)64-", "DCod2+", "pH2O", "TC101 1.41", "farea", "jlimiting", "A1g". Which of these can be rendered with Unicode sub/superscripts from the glyph rows (raised/lowered flags) at acceptable risk, and which should stay plain? One rule, with its failure cases.
4. **Cluster H — spaces at line joins**: "A3D instantaneous nucleation" (should be "A 3D"), "A0.25 mm diameter Co wire" ("A 0.25 mm"), "demonstratea a lower number", "[Li+]/ [Co2+]", "mA/ cm2", "20- 30 mA/cm2", "4.68- 12.50 μm", "C/ 10". Find the join rule (where consecutive lines are concatenated — `joinProse`?): when does it add a space, when not, and why "A" + "3D" lost its space while "mA/" + "cm2" gained one. Design the rule: a line ending in "/" or a hyphen between digits joins without a space; otherwise a space; a line-end hyphen is cluster C's business (P9-02).
