## The symptom

B P12-40 **O2**: `gutterOf` needs a clear band of 2 em for eight rows; a paper whose gutter is 11 pt at a 9.3 pt body (1.2 em) has no gutter on any page, so the two columns are read welded at one height (headings welded to the other column's line, first paragraphs welded). Another paper finds a gutter on two pages only, at 305.9 and 309.9 pt, and `gutterPrior` votes on `Math.round(b / 3)` — different buckets, so no prior, and its other pages are read welded. A manual prior repaired both papers' rows in B's what-if.

## The rules

1. **Narrow gutter.** A clear band down to **1 em** is a gutter when it lies in the middle of the text width (its centre within the middle third) and is clear over at least 8 rows; elsewhere the 2 em rule stands. A single-column page with a narrow gap (a table, an equation's spacing) must not get a gutter — negative tests.
2. **The prior by position.** `gutterPrior` clusters the pages' gutters by position within a tolerance (about 6 pt, or half an em) and takes the cluster with the most pages; rounding buckets go.
3. Nothing else changes: line assembly within a column, captions, headings, sections.
