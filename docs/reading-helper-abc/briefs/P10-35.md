## The rules

1. **A mark is never drawn on words that are not the note's.** A note's anchor is `paragraphId` (`s<i>-p<j>`, positional) with `start` / `end` and its `quote`. When the paragraph under that id does not hold the quote at `[start, end)`, the note's mark and tag are not drawn there. Tests.
2. **The note finds its passage again.** It searches for the quote (P10-24's `stripTagText` and `locatePassage`) in the same section's paragraphs, nearest first, then the whole body; found once → the anchor is repaired (`paragraphId`, `start`, `end`) and written back to the note once, so the next load needs no search; found more than once → the nearest to the old position; not found → the note keeps its anchor, draws no mark, and its sticky says in one quiet line that its passage is not in this version of the paper. Tests on a body whose paragraph is split in two before the anchored one, and whose section gains a paragraph.
3. **Cheap and quiet.** The search runs only for an anchor whose words do not match; no work for a matching anchor; no network; nothing per-user in shared state.
4. **Nothing else changes**: the numbering, P10-24's selection, P10-25 / P10-28's heading rules, P10-29's merge.
