## The owner's decision (O1)

At 853 × 480 (the default step), the margin divider can move only 1 px. Its range is 280 to 281: the centre sits at its floor (`readingCentreMinimum`, 476 × scale + 24 = 500 px) and the margin at its floor (280).

The owner chose the manager's option 1: **lower the centre's floor to about 50 characters, so the divider has a useful range at narrow widths.**

## The rules

1. **A drag floor.** Add `readingCentreDragMinimum(scale)`: the centre at about 50 characters of the body text at that step, with its 12 + 20 px insets included.
   - Start from `Math.ceil(408 * scale + 32)`.
   - Measure it with the Chromium harness at 853 × 480, at the default and the largest step. Tune the 408 so the mean characters per line at the floor is between 48 and 52, and report the numbers.
2. **The dividers' room uses the drag floor.** Both dividers' ranges (`aria-valuemax`, End, a drag) let the centre go down to the drag floor, never below.
3. **The default layout does not change.**
   - With no width saved by the reader (`marginWidth` / `leftWidth` undefined in the panes store), `fitPanes` keeps today's reading budget (`readingCentreMinimum`). So a first visit at 853 still shows the margin at 281 and the text column at 468 px.
   - A width the reader saved may take the centre down to the drag floor, so a dragged margin is kept and not given back.
   - Pin it with a test: for a sweep of viewports (853 to 1920) and every step, `fitPanes` with nothing saved gives the same result before and after.
4. **Which layout registers does not change.** `railMinimumWidth`, the side margin against the bottom band, still uses `readingCentreMinimum`. W3 at the largest step still registers the band.
5. **Fix the stale comment** on `CENTRE_MIN_PX` ("about 58 characters"), if it no longer holds.
