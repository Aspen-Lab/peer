Its summary line and "show map / hide map" stay. The map's heading band stays. Nothing of the map is duplicated in the column.
2. **The extractors keep an empty numbered parent (A10).** A heading that has no text of its own but is followed by a heading it numbers ("2." then "2.1") is kept as a section with empty text (HTML and PDF alike); an empty heading that numbers nothing (a stray) is dropped as before; end matter as before. `withSectionIds` numbers it in order. The version bumps follow (`UPLOAD_CONCEPT_EXTRACTION_VERSION` 9 → 10; `PaperReading`'s version if the reading's shape is cached).
3. **The map's hierarchy.** `readableSections` keeps a section with no paragraphs; `readingMapOf` gives every row `level` (1 for "N.", 2 for "N.M", 3 for "N.M.K"; a heading with no number: 1, or 2 when it follows a numbered level-1 parent — say what you chose) and `parentId` (the nearest preceding row with a smaller level, if any). A group row (no paragraphs of its own) shows its heading, its role when known, its page, and **the sum of its children's words and minutes** (so "4. EXPERIMENTAL SECTION · method · p.15 · 1,562 words · 9 min"); it has no fold of its own. Children are indented one step per level under their parent (an indent class, not a nested table — the rows stay one list for the keyboard). The map's summary line counts sections as before (the group rows are not double-counted in the words: the sum is over leaf rows).
4. **The body shows the group heading** as a heading alone (its `id` anchor, so the map row jumps to it), with nothing under it before its first child; the heading's tint (C P10-06's) is applied by the same class path as any heading. The reading order of the paragraphs is unchanged (§1a.4).
5. **Nothing else changes**: the route (P10-06 reads `level` / `parentId`; until it lands a group row simply has no mark — fine), the paragraph lines, the gists, the openings, the touch floors, the Summary tab.
