## The rules

1. **The card.** New `web/src/components/reader/figure-card.tsx` (`FigureCardView`, pure for tests). It is a `MarginCard` of kind `figure` that fills the room the stack gives it (the whole margin when it is the only card). It is non-modal (`role="region"`, no `aria-modal`, Tab not trapped).
   - **Header:** 40 px, the label and the caption's head, and the X at the top right.
   - **Zoom row (front only):** minus, readout, plus, Fit.
   - **Surface:** the zoom hook's surface.
   - **Caption:** whole, scrolling, at most 30 % of the card's height.
   - Behind, it shows its header only.
2. **The host.** New `web/src/components/reader/figure-host.tsx`. It listens to the figure store.
   - **Opening:** a figure opens (or is pressed again) → `useWindows.open({ id: "figure", kind: "figure", title })`, brought forward.
   - **Escape:** closes the front figure card wherever the focus is. A modal dialog and `defaultPrevented` win over it, and the page's "back" does not run while the card consumes it.
   - **Closing:** the X or Escape → `closeFigure()` and the window closes.
   - **Paper change or unmount:** call `closeFigure()` there, because the store's key `label:ordinal` repeats across papers.
   - **Below 1024 (no margin rect):** today's overlay `FigureViewer` from `figure-lightbox.tsx`, with nothing reserved. A window that narrows below 1024 closes an open card.
3. **The page.** One line in `web/src/app/papers/[id]/page.tsx`, `<FigureHost key={paper.id} />` right after `<StickyNotes …/>`, plus its import. It is inserted beside the lines `page.sticky.test.ts` and `page.explain.test.ts` match, never editing them. Add a `page.figure.test.ts` pinning that it is mounted exactly once, keyed by the paper.
