## The defect

All three of round 2's Theme-A readers charged the same extra, at 0.25 each:
- The right-edge tab ("OPEN YOUR NOTES", `variant: "edge"` in `web/src/components/notes/notes-button.tsx`) stands flush against the centre column's text whenever a card stands in the margin.
- A card here means the notes window, the explain card or a sticky.
- It happens at 1024 × 768, 1093 × 614 and 1280 × 720.
- It covers no word, but it touches the line ends.

The evidence is read-only, in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r2a/pack/shots/`: `X-noteswindow-1024x768-light.png`, `X-noteswindow-1093x614-dark.png`, `X-noteswindow-1280x720-dark.png` and `A8-Take-notes-1024x768-light.png`.

The mechanism, from the file's own comment (lines 6–10, 85–92): with a card standing, the tab is placed at the margin's left edge (`useMarginRect()`), "beside the cards instead of under them". At these widths the margin's left edge is only the column gutter away from the text's right edge, and the tab is as wide as that gutter.

The tab was put beside the cards on purpose: it opens the owner's notes (item 3, "Open your notes"; P10-09b). It stays.

## The rule

1. **Never touching the text.** Wherever the edge tab stands, its rect is **at least 8 px clear of every line box of the centre column's text**. The cases are:
   - no card;
   - the explain card, a sticky, the notes window or a figure standing;
   - several of them stacked.

   In every case, the tab:
   - never covers a card's or the window's content or controls;
   - stays wholly inside the window;
   - keeps its hit area whole (≥ 24 px across);
   - keeps its label readable.
2. **The text column is unchanged.** Its rect, its width and its line breaks stay exactly as today. The owner's "正文只换行不变大小" holds.
3. **Choosing the means.** You choose it and measure it. Options include:
   - the tab riding the standing card's own left edge (inside or on its border);
   - a narrower tab;
   - an offset that uses the gutter's real width.

   Say what you chose and why, and why the others failed.
4. **Where the tab cannot be placed.** If, at some width, no place meets rule 1 without breaking rule 2, stop and say so in "For the manager", with the measurements. Do not move the column.
