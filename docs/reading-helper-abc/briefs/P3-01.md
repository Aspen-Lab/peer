# P3-01 — terms: the paper's own definitions (Tier 0) and the "Terms to know" strip

Role: C. Ruling §1h.1 (and §1h.7 for order and files; §1f.17 faces; §1a.4 nothing hidden). Acceptance §3d 13. First item of P3.

Setup: `git pull --ff-only origin deep-report-reading-helper-enhancement`; read §1h.1, §1h.7, §1f.17, §3d 13, blueprint §3.5; `lib/feed/term-expand.ts` (the abbreviation logic to reuse), `lib/papers/html-text.ts` (`ExtractedDocument`, sections with `id`, `heading`, `text`, `page`), `lib/papers/reading-map.ts` (`RouteResult`, the route's `read` / `background` marks), `lib/papers/report.ts` (`PaperTerm`, the Pass 2 `terms` that P2-02 sanitises and verifies), `components/reader/paper-body.tsx` (`sectionAnchor`, `paragraphAnchor`, the body's paragraph elements), `components/reader/reading-map.tsx` (where the map renders, so the strip sits under it), `components/reader/evidence-quote.tsx`, `components/reader/copy.ts` (`PEERS_READING`), `app/papers/[id]/page.tsx` (the `additions` slot and the map's slot).

What to build:

1. **`lib/papers/terms.ts`** (pure): `paperDefinedTerms(doc: ExtractedDocument, scope?: { sectionIds: string[] }): PaperTerm[]` — the five patterns of blueprint §3.5 (`X (ABBR)`, `ABBR (X)`, `X, defined as …`, `X refers to …`, `we define X as …`), each hit yielding `{ term, definition (≤1 sentence, the paper's own words), evidence (that sentence), evidenceWhere (the section heading), sectionId, page }`; scoped to `scope.sectionIds` when given (the route's read and background sections), else to the methods and results sections by `canonical`, else the whole body; ≤8, de-duplicated case-insensitively, the first occurrence wins; `term-expand.ts`'s abbreviation logic reused by import, not copied; no term shorter than 2 characters, no stoplist word.
2. **Tier 2 merge** (pure, same file): `mergeTerms(tier0: PaperTerm[], model: PaperTerm[] | undefined): PaperTerm[]` — the Tier 0 ones first, then the model's that are not already present (case-insensitive), still ≤8 in all; a model term with `evidence` stays as verified (P2-02), one without carries `peer: true`.
3. **`components/reader/terms-strip.tsx`**: the strip "Terms to know" under the map (label-face heading through `Band`), one line per term: the term in the reading face, then its definition — a paper-defined one as an `EvidenceQuote` with its section link and page, a Peer one as reading-face prose with the `PEERS_READING` label; clicking a term scrolls to its first occurrence in the body and highlights it with the token `--color-term-mark` (defined in `globals.css` for light and dark, a neutral tint distinct from the three route greens), cleared by the next click or Escape; no external links; the strip is absent with no terms. The highlight target lives in `paper-body.tsx` (a `data-term-mark` attribute on the paragraph and a `<mark>` around the first occurrence, nothing else changed in the body).
4. **The page** computes `paperDefinedTerms(doc, { sectionIds: the merged route's read + background sections })` with `useMemo`, merges `model.report?.terms`, and mounts the strip under the map; copy in `copy.ts` under `TERMS` (`heading: "Terms to know"`); the copy grep scans it.
5. **Tests**: the five patterns on synthetic sections; the scope rule (route sections → methods + results → whole body); the cap and de-duplication; abbreviation reuse; the merge order and `peer: true`; the strip renders only with terms, a paper-defined term as a quote with link and page, a Peer term labelled; the click-to-highlight through a pure helper (`firstOccurrence(doc, term)` → `{ sectionId, paragraphIndex, offset }`) since the body cannot be mounted; the copy grep; the three committed fixtures each yield ≥1 paper-defined term or the test states which has none and why.

Rules: the paper's words only through `EvidenceQuote` (§1f.17); nothing hidden or reordered in the body; commit only your files; the four gates; a Chromium look at the strip and the highlight in light and dark (describe it in the checkpoint); checkpoint `docs/reading-helper-abc/P3-01-C-<UTC>.md`; commit `feat(reader): Terms to know — the paper's own definitions and the model's, with click-to-highlight (P3-01)`; push; one-paragraph report.
