## The rules (each its own commit, test first; the manager's product calls are in bold)

1. **Hyphen and number hygiene in `headingOf`.** (a) An unnumbered "heading" that ends in a hyphen (`-`, `‐`, `‑`, `−`) is a broken line, not a heading. (b) A `NUMBERED_HEADING` match whose number is a decimal with a leading zero or has four or more digits in its first group ("0.661) (Figure…", "2026 was the year") is not a numbered heading (a real section number is 1–3 digits per group). Tests: the two negatives, and the positives "2.1. Electrochemically Tuned Growth", "4.5 Macrotexture Analysis", "10. Conclusion" still headings. Commit: `fix(papers): a line ending in a hyphen or led by a decimal is not a heading (P7-11)`.
2. **A sentence is not a display equation (`equationOf`).** A line with **three or more real words** (letters only, four or more characters, separated by spaces — the same `[A-Za-z]{4,}` idea `equationOf` already uses, counted as words) is prose with notation in it, not an equation, whatever its symbols — except a numbered one ending in "(n)" with a symbol, which stays an equation (a numbered display equation can carry words: "where … (3)"). Tests: a crystallographic line such as "⟨003⟩||ND texture was observed in the films" is prose; "E = mc^2" and "softmax(QK^T/√d_k)V" stay equations; a numbered line with words stays. **The manager accepts that this rule moves other documents** (B's table: the owner's 52-page printout 43 → 14 "equations", all notation torn from sentences; the design spec 4 → 1). Commit: `fix(papers): a line of three or more words is prose, not a display equation (P7-11)`.
3. **The indent's left edge is the 10th percentile (`joinProse`).** `left` becomes the 10th percentile of the section's line starts (sorted `x`, the value at `floor(0.1 × (n − 1))`), not the minimum, so one margin label or one hanging line does not make every other line "indented" (B measured 265 → 188 paragraphs on the paper from this rule alone). A section of fewer than 10 lines keeps the minimum. Tests: 11 lines, one at x = 20 and ten at x = 72 with no other break → one paragraph; the existing `joinProse` tests unchanged. Commit: `fix(papers): the paragraph indent is read against the section's usual left edge, not its one outlier (P7-11)`.
4. **The Received / Revised / Accepted / Published box (`buildOutline`).** A run of **two or more consecutive lines** each beginning with `Received`, `Revised`, `Accepted`, `Published`, `Available online`, `Submitted` (case-insensitive, followed by `:` or a space and a date-like rest) is furniture, dropped wherever it stands (B found it inside the Introduction). A single such line stays. Tests: the run dropped, a single line kept, a body sentence "Published data suggest …" kept. Commit: `fix(papers): the received/accepted date box is furniture (P7-11)`.
5. **The caption cap.** `CAPTION_MAX` 700 → **2,000** (with clean columns a caption now runs to its end; 6 of the 10 on the owner's paper were cut at 700; B measured caption lengths 496–1,754). The test that pins the cap (if any) re-pinned; `continuesCaption` unchanged. Commit: `fix(papers): a figure caption may run to 2,000 characters (P7-11)`.
6. **Citation superscripts (`rowsOf` / `lineOf`).** A folded script that sits **above** its row's baseline (a superscript: its `y` higher than the row's by more than 1 pt) and reads as a citation marker — only digits, commas, en-dashes/hyphens and spaces (`/^[\d\s,–\-−]+$/`), at most 12 characters — is **dropped** when the run before it ends in a letter (`[A-Za-z]{2,}$` after trimming) or in `.`, `,`, `)`, `]` or `;` — the shapes of "…materials.12" and "films,3−5". It is **kept** (glued as today) when the run before it ends in a digit or in a one- or two-letter unit token (`10−3`, `cm2`, `s−1`): those are exponents. Subscripts (below the baseline: "LiCoO2") and lettered scripts ("1st, 20th") are untouched. Count on the owner's paper how many scripts the rule drops and how many it keeps, with five examples of each shape (one sentence each at most) in the checkpoint. **Product call (manager): drop, do not mark** — a citation number carries nothing a reader of the digest needs, and a bracket would litter the sentences. Tests: the four cases above at item level in `pdf-outline.test.ts`, plus one in `pdf-outline.columns.test.ts` if the fixture has a citation. Commit: `fix(papers): citation superscripts are dropped, exponents kept (P7-11)`.
