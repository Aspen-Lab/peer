## The target

On a window of 1024 px or more, when the box is open it stands as a **dock**: fixed at the viewport's right edge, 360 px wide, its top under the masthead, its bottom above the foot legend, scrolling inside; the page **reserves that width** while it is open — the reading spread re-centres in the remaining width (the container's width cap applies to the remaining width; at 1440 the spread moves left by about 184 px and the column keeps its width when the cap allows, else the column narrows — never the panel); the text reflows, the selection is re-measured, and the "Explain this?" button and the highlight follow it. Closing the box gives the width back. Below 1024 px (and on touch) the bottom sheet stays as it is. The thread, "Say more", the search pill, the Tier 0 definition card and the `e` key are unchanged in behaviour. Nothing is ever covered: the dock and the page do not overlap at any width ≥ 1024.
