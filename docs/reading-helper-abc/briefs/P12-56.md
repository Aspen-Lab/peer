## The symptom

B P12-41 on the held-out Nature papers: **M14** — a paper's 10 "Extended Data Fig. N" figures are never listed, so a reader cannot open them, and a body citation of one cannot reach it; **M6** — a body line that opens with a figure citation after a line ending mid-sentence ("…as shown in" / "Fig. 4 and …") is still read as a caption on one paper (C P12-46's separator rule does not catch it: the number is followed by a space and a capital); **M15** — 14 of 15 captions begin with a stray "| " (Nature sets "Fig. 1 | Title"; the bar is a separator, not part of the caption).

## The rules

1. **R14a, Extended Data.** An optional "Extended Data" prefix before Fig. / Figure in `CAPTION_SEPARATED` and in the engine's `CAPTION_RE` / `LABEL_RE`; the label keeps the prefix ("Extended Data Fig. 3"), distinct from "Fig. 3" everywhere (listing, dedupe by label, binding); the figure-image route takes a validated token `fig=ed3` for it (digits only after `ed`; anything else refused as today); `pictureNumber` / `pdfFigureUrl` give it its own URL. Check the whole-document pool's figure cap (12): if it stops a paper's 15th figure from being listed or sized, raise it to cover main plus Extended Data figures (say to what, and the work it adds). A cited "Extended Data Fig. N" in the body reaches its figure, never "Fig. N".
2. **R6b, the line before.** A line that opens with a figure label is not a caption when the line before it in the same column is body text that does not end a sentence (no `.` `!` `?` `:` closing it, same size and face as the body). With R6b in place, C P12-46's refusal of "space + bracket" after the number may be relaxed so that "Fig. 1 (a) Title …" after a finished paragraph is a caption again — do it only if your tests hold both ways.
3. **R15a, the bar.** `|` after the number is a separator, and it leaves the caption's words, like `.` and `:` do.
4. Nothing else changes: headings, sections, the engine's regions, the outline's other functions. Bump `FIGURE_EXTRACTION_REVISION` if pairing or bytes change.
