## The defect

Round 3's Theme-B walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-44-A-20261021T0921Z.md`, weighed (f)) found that a selection longer than 1,200 characters gets no buttons at all: not Highlight, not Take notes. On the owner's paper, a triple-click (the browser's "select this paragraph") offers nothing on 10 of 13 paragraphs.

The cause is `paper-body.tsx`'s `selectionTarget`:
- `if (… passage.length > MAX_SELECTION_CHARS) return null` (1,200);
- `HIGHLIGHT_QUOTE_MAX` (1,200) in `lib/notes/blocks.ts`;
- the explain route's `passageChars: 1200` (`lib/papers/explain.ts`).

The owner's item 5: a selection is offered Explain this / Take notes / Highlight. Item 8: a highlight records the words in the note.

## The rules (ruling §1h.148)

1. **Highlight and Take notes take any length within one block.**
   - A selection inside **one block** (a paragraph, a caption, a heading, a Summary block, an answer) is offered **Take notes and Highlight whatever its length**.
   - A highlight keeps **all** its words: the mark covers them, the note records them, every export carries them, and reload finds them again.
   - A sticky's quote keeps them too.
   - A selection across two blocks still offers nothing (D5).
2. **Explain this on a long selection.** For a selection over the explain route's 1,200-character passage cap, choose one of two options, measure it, and say why:
   - (a) offer it, with the request carrying the passage clipped at the cap on a word boundary, and the card's quote showing the clipped words with "…";
   - (b) don't offer it, and leave a short, plain word on why where the button would stand.

   Prefer (a) if the route explains the clipped passage well with the paragraph as context, and say so from the prompt it builds.

   **The route's own cap is unchanged.**
3. **Short selections are unchanged.** A selection up to 1,200 characters is byte for byte as today.
