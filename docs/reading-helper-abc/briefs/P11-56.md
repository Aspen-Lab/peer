## The rules (ruling §1h.165)

1. **A triple click on any paragraph is offered its actions.** When the browser ends a triple click's selection at the start (offset 0) of the next block, whatever that block is — a paragraph, a heading, a caption, a displayed equation's `code`, a figure, a rule — and the selection's words are the paragraph's alone, the selection is the paragraph's and it is offered Take notes and Highlight, and Explain this where the page offers it (a key, or a defined term). The passage is the paragraph's words (no equation text in it).
2. **Nothing else changes.** A drag that really runs into the next paragraph (its words carry the next paragraph's) still offers nothing (D5); a selection inside an equation offers nothing (the formula rule, `FORMULA_SELECTOR`); C P11-47's long-selection pins and C P11-50's `spansBlocks` pins hold; a triple click on a Summary block and on the answers is as today.
