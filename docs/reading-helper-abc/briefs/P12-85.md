## The symptom (the manager's measure, by shape)

On a Nature-style paper that prints no abstract heading, `echoOf` takes the last paragraph of the setup part. Two situations arise:
- **Today:** that last paragraph is a figure caption's tail that leaked into the body, because the caption was cut short. By chance it echoes the closing well, and the split fires.
- **With C P12-82 (captions whole):** the setup part ends in seven short fragments of figure text (1, 1, 1, 8, 2, 8 and 10 words). The last of these, 10 words, has 3 long words, so the closing's share falls from 0.31 to 0.04 and the split does not fire.

## The rule

`echoOf`'s fallback takes **the last paragraph of the setup part that has at least 40 words** (a constant, `ECHO_MIN_WORDS`), skipping shorter fragments. If no paragraph of the part is that long, it takes the longest one. The abstract, when the paper prints one, is unchanged.

## Measure (invented input only)

- **Fixture 1:** a setup part ending in five short fragments after a 150-word paragraph that shares long words with a closing paragraph. Before the rule there is no split; after it there is one.
- **Negatives:**
  - a paper with an abstract: unchanged;
  - a setup part whose last paragraph is already long: unchanged;
  - a setup part with no paragraph of 40 words: its longest paragraph is the echo.
