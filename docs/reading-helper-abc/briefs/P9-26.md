## The question

The owner wants both stopped: no job or event information fetched, sent to a model, scored, cached, stored or shown. **Careful: `app/api/jobs/*` (`purge-uploads`, `dispatch-digests`, `prepare-dashboards`) are background *jobs* (cron work), not job listings** — establish for each whether it touches job or event information, and leave the ones that do not (the upload purge is the privacy promise and must keep running).
