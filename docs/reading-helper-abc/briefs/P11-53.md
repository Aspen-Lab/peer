## The defect

Round 4's Theme-A walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-51-A-20261021T1423Z.md`, E1, costed 0.5; "For the manager" 1) measured this:
- One paragraph of the owner's paper holds an unbroken run of 43 characters: a chemical name joined by slashes. It is 338 px wide at 17.5 px.
- Since C P11-48 the text steps scale the paper's own text. At steps 1.32 / 1.45 / 1.6 the run is 446 / 490 / 541 px wide, against the 416-px column at 1024 × 768 and 1093 × 614. It passes the column's right edge by 30 / 74 / 125 px, and by 27 px at 1.6 at 1242 × 698.
- The page does not scroll sideways. The word stands over the column's padding, the separator and the margin. With a card standing level with it, the word's tail is under the card.
- At 390 × 844 (one column) the page becomes wider than the viewport from step 1.1 up.

Mechanism: the paper's paragraphs wrap only at spaces and hyphens (`overflow-wrap: normal`). `hyphens: auto` breaks nothing where the browser has no dictionary, as in the walk's Chromium. A run with no break opportunity is as wide as it is.

## The rule (ruling §1h.159)

1. **No word passes its column.** Inside the `.reading-scaled` wrappers, a run wider than its line breaks inside itself, so that no word ever passes the column or its block, at any text step and any window, and no page scrolls sideways.
   - Use `overflow-wrap: break-word` (or `anywhere` if `break-word` does not reach a case you measure; say why).
   - It is inherited, so one rule on the wrapper may be enough. Say where you put it and why.
   - Check that it reaches the paper's body, its headings and captions, the Summary's blocks and the answers.
2. **Nothing else changes.** A line that fits today wraps exactly as today: `overflow-wrap: break-word` breaks a word only when it cannot fit on a line of its own.
   - Compare every line box of the paper's body at scale 1 against the head, on the owner-like invented paper, at 1024 × 768, 1280 × 720 and 1440 × 900. The line count and the line breaks must be identical, unless a word already overflowed at scale 1 (name it).
   - Leave hyphenation, justification (the owner's "两端对齐"), the column's width and the text steps as they are.
