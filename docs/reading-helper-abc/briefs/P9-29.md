## The rules (§1h.59)

1. The figure-image route resolves an `upload:` id for its owner only (the same capability-cookie check as `figure/route.ts`), reads the bytes with `readUploadPdf`, and answers `?page=N&fig=K` with the region whose caption opens "Figure K" on page N (the engine's `onlyPage` + `label`), not "the largest raster"; an upload's answer carries `PRIVATE_UPLOAD_HEADERS` (`private, no-store`), a public paper's keeps its `public, s-maxage`; 400 on a bad page or label, 404 on an unknown paper or a figure not found, 403 / 404 for another reader's upload (as `figure/route.ts` does); `X-Peer-Figure-Engine` passed through. The body is the PNG bytes (`image/png`), never a `data:` URL.
2. `placeFigures` emits the figure-image URL for every "Figure N" caption that has a page — the "exactly one caption on the page" rule and the upload skip go; the URL carries `page` and `fig`.
3. Nothing else in the reading changes; the caption text, its place and the figures' order are as today.
