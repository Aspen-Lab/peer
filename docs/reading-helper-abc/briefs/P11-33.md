## The rule

1. **No wide gaps.** On every non-final line of justified prose, the widest gap between two words is at most **twice the paragraph's normal word space**, measured as rendered. This holds at every centre-column width the layout allows: 416 px, 480, 560, 640 and the default at 1440, Fit off and on.
   - Where justification cannot meet that at a width, the prose there is set ragged right (`text-align: left`), keeping `hyphens` and `text-wrap: pretty`.
   - Where it can, it stays justified exactly as today.
   - The switch follows **the column's width, not the window's**. Use a container query on the centre column (`.reader-centre` or its inner box), or your measured better means; say which and why.
   - Also check whether `hyphens: auto` actually breaks words on the page (it needs a `lang` on the element or an ancestor). If it does not, say why, and whether fixing that alone meets the rule at 416 px.
2. **Nothing else moves.** Adding a container must not change any rect: the panes' widths, the centre column's rect, the margin's rect and the left pane's map must be identical before and after, at 1440 × 900, 1280 × 720, 1093 × 614 and 1024 × 768. The text only re-flows where the alignment changes. Other users of `reading-justify` (the report's sections, quotes, the abstract) follow the same rule in whatever column they stand in.
