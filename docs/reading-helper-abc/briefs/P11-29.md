## The rules

1. **The separator takes focus without moving the page.** At scroll 0, when keyboard focus first reaches the left pane's separator the window scrolls 64 px (the separator is as tall as the grid and Chromium aligns its top to the window's). B's measured fix: `.reader-divider { scroll-margin-top: calc(4rem / var(--page-zoom, 1)); }` — window 0 at 1440 × 900, 1093 × 614, 1920 × 1080 (3 px under Fit on); not `scroll-margin-block` (Tab from the paper's last link to the margin's separator then jumps to 0), not `scroll-padding-top` on `html` (it moves every `scroll-mt-20` landing). Measure at 1440 × 900, 1280 × 720, 1093 × 614 and 1920 × 1080, Fit off and on: the window's `scrollY` before / after focus reaches each separator, from scroll 0 and from mid-page, settled (wait for `scrollY` to be stable for 300 ms before each reading); the pane's top, the map's `scrollTop` and `scrollX` unchanged.
2. **Smooth scrolling respects reduced motion.** `globals.css:865` (`html[data-scroll-behavior="smooth"] { scroll-behavior: smooth; }`, switched on by `layout.tsx:95`) ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`. Wrap it in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; with reduce the computed `scroll-behavior` is `auto` and a focus scroll or an anchor jump is one step; default motion unchanged. Measure both media settings.
