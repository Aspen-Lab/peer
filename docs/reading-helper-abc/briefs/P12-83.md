## The rule (B P12-80 K2, R2-C)

Some PDFs carry a font whose ToUnicode map gives lowercase letters for capital codes. pdf.js follows the map; MuPDF and pdftotext read the capitals. The repair works per document, like `learnLigatures`:
- A short lowercase run (one or two letters) that touches a run beginning with a capital, in the same font, with no space between them, is evidence. Record the font and the letters.
- When a font has **at least 3 such hits over at least 2 different letters**, capitalise that font's short runs of those letters where they touch a capital-led run.
- Every other item comes back exactly as it came. It is pure, deterministic, dependency-free and linear.
