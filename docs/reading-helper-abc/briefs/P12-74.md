## The rules (B P12-73 §2 M1 package A, M6 "block"; ruled §1h.449)

1. **R1** a numbered candidate set smaller than 0.95 of the body is not a heading (`candidateOf`). **R1 and R2 ship together** (R1 alone makes two new extras, B §2 M1).
2. **R2** in `numberedOut`: a numbered line in the body's face and size that follows open prose (the line above is body prose in the same column, one pitch up, not ending a sentence) is not a heading — **unless the line above is itself a heading candidate** (stacked numbered headings in plain fixtures stay headings; without the exemption four tests move, B lists them).
3. **R3** a candidate that holds a lone panel letter b–h (a single letter between spaces or at an end) is figure text, not a heading.
4. **R4** `continuesCaption`: a number-led line ends a caption only when it is set at 0.95 of the body or more (a caption's own number-led middle line goes on).
5. **R5** a candidate with a quoted key and a colon (source code, a settings listing) is not a heading.
6. **M6 block** on the opening page only: small print (under 0.95 of the body) **below the last line in the body's face and size**, set apart by more than a pitch, **opening with a footnote marker** (a digit, or ∗ † ‡ § ¶, directly before a letter), at most 8 rows, and not a caption, is margin print. Keep the marker test (without it `pdf-outline.test.ts:848` moves).
