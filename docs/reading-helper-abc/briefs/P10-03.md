## The rules

1. **One question, one bar (A3, A4).** The box holds exactly one field; no second line ever, no "Up to five questions"; Enter submits (Shift+Enter is nothing special — a newline is folded by `oneLine`); the field is a `textarea` that grows to its text (P7-13 stays) but at rest is one row ≥ 48 px tall at 1440, full width of the column, with the Submit control inside the bar's right end (a button in the bar, not a row under it). The × that cleared a line goes (the reader selects and deletes). The 500-character limit and the counter from 400 stay.
2. **The label (A2)**: the band reads exactly `What you want to learn about this paper:` — `ASK.heading`; "Before you read" appears nowhere (`grep -rn "Before you read" web/src` finds nothing — the standing questions' hint and the privacy page re-worded).
3. **The field is the obvious place to type (A1)**: a 2-px border in the heading ink (`border-heading` or the token the page uses for the strongest border), a surface token that differs from the page's background in light and dark (measure: the field's background ≠ the page's, the border's contrast against the page ≥ 3:1 in both themes — compute from the tokens in `globals.css` and state the numbers), the placeholder in the muted ink. No new colour tokens unless none fit — then one, named, with its light and dark values and contrast.
4. **Suggestions as a dropdown (A5)**: the examples (earlier questions, the profile's, the standing ones) and "Just get the gist" are items of one dropdown (`role="listbox"`, items `role="option"`, Down/Up move, Enter picks, Escape closes; the pointer picks) that opens under the bar while the field has focus and is **not settled** (the text differs from the last submitted question, or nothing was submitted) — and is closed otherwise: after Submit, on blur, with no suggestion to show. Picking an item fills the bar (never submits). "Just get the gist" keeps its meaning (the gist route) as the last item, visibly set apart. Nothing is listed under the bar at rest. The group labels ("From your earlier questions", "From your profile", "Your standing questions") become group headings inside the dropdown.
5. **Submit (A7)**: after a submit the control is disabled until the text changes (`hasNewQuestions` keeps doing this); a submit with the same text does nothing. The settle paths are Submit and Enter only (× and the tick are gone with the lines).
6. **The opt-out moves (A6)**: no "Not for recommendations" checkbox in the box. The ledger still learns a submitted question's words (§1b; `syncQuestionTerms`) **unless** the Profile's new switch is off: `profile.questionsTuneRecommendations?: boolean` (absent = on), a switch on the Profile page beside the standing questions, labelled `Use my questions to tune recommendations` with one line under it (`Peer learns a little from the words of the questions you submit on papers. Turning it off removes nothing already learned (say so in the line? no — keep the line short; the privacy page says it). The stored per-question opt-out (`notForRecommendations`) is read no more; its storage key is ignored, not migrated. The privacy page's sentences on the per-question tick are re-aimed to the switch (the pins re-aimed, named).
7. **The answers under the question (A12)**: `ForYourQuestions` renders, directly under the bar (the `common` block, after the field), one disclosure control — a button reading exactly `Answers generated by AI based on this paper`, `aria-expanded`, collapsed by default on every visit (no memory) — and, open, for each answered question a bullet list: the verdict line first (`Answered` / `Partly answered` / the not-addressed sentence / the unverified line), then one bullet per answer: the answer's text and, under it, the quote through `EvidenceQuote` with its section and page exactly as now; then "Read next" as now.
