## The rules (ruling §1h.155)

1. **Highlight is offered only where it makes a mark.** A selection whose two ends are in two blocks of one Summary or answer container (what `readSelection` computes as `oneBlock: false`) is **not offered Highlight**. It is still offered Explain this and Take notes, exactly as today (the P10-08 pin stands).
   - Why not mark across the blocks: a highlight is one block's words (D5), and in the paper's body a selection across two paragraphs offers nothing at all. Making the Summary the one place where a highlight spans blocks would be a second model of a highlight for one case.
   - Carry the fact on the target the page already hands to `canHighlight` (for example a `spansBlocks: true` field on the `ExplainSelection`, set only when it is true). Say what you chose.
   - `canHighlight` returns false for it. Nothing else reads the new field.
2. **Everything else is unchanged.** Byte for byte, as on the base:
   - a selection inside one block, of any length (C P11-47), on the body, a caption, a heading, a Summary block or an answer;
   - a triple click on a Summary block (the browser ends it at the next block's start; P11-47 reads it as one block);
   - the cross-block offering of Explain this and Take notes;
   - the group's place and the strip.
