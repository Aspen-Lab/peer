## The defect

Round 4's Theme-A walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-51-A-20261021T1423Z.md`, E2, costed 0.25; "For the manager" 2; its probe `r4-wordplace` under `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r4a/h/`, read-only for you) measured this:
- C P11-45's `keep-place.ts` keeps the paragraph at the top in 100 % of re-flows.
- Inside that paragraph it keeps the **fraction** of its height above the line under the masthead.
- A long paragraph's lines are not equally full (a short last line, a long unbroken run, a formula). After a large change of column the same fraction is another word: in 22 of 279 cells the word under the line moved more than one line, worst 4.27 lines (121 px). This happens in the large re-flows: End / Home / Enter on a separator, a collapse or a pin where the column changes by a third, a text step.

Ruling §1h.146 (c) asked for "the paragraph at the top and its offset (±1 line)". The owner's item 3: "需要停在之前的浏览位置" (it must stop at the earlier place).

## The rules (ruling §1h.159)

1. **The word under the line stays under it, to within one line.** Keep a **character anchor**, not a fraction:
   - At the reader's own scroll, read the caret position under the line (`caretPositionFromPoint`, or `caretRangeFromPoint` where that is what the browser has) at the column's text, and keep it as a text node and offset.
   - After a re-flow, put that character's rect (a collapsed or one-character `Range`) back at the same y, by the same one instant `scrollBy` in the same frame as today.
   - Where there is no text under the line (the gap between two paragraphs, a figure, a formula), fall back to today's fraction / gap reading for that paragraph.
   - Where the node has left the page (the paper re-rendered), fall back to the paragraph's fraction.
   - Say what you chose for the x at which the caret is read, and why.
2. **Everything P11-45 holds, still holds.** Specifically:
   - the read only at the reader's own scrolls;
   - the 600-ms hold through the settling;
   - nothing at scroll 0 or the foot;
   - no smooth scroll;
   - every cause with no per-cause code (drag, keys, collapse / pin, Fit, text steps, resize);
   - the cards, the map's scroll, the Back-to-paper return and the arrivals unchanged.

   P11-45's tests stay green. Name any re-aimed one.
3. **No jank.** The correction stays one `scrollBy` per frame, after layout and before paint. Measure the script cost per frame during a drag, against today's +0.12 ms.
