## The questions (each answered with the code path — file, function, line — and the evidence)

Find the dehyphenation (where a line-end hyphen is removed and the halves joined) and why the caption path differs. Design the rule with the document's own words as the evidence: build the set of words seen whole in the document (not at line ends); a line-end hyphen joins when the joined form occurs in the set, or when the right half is not a word on its own; it stays (as a hyphen, no space) when both halves occur as words on their own and the joined form never does ("deposition" + "rate", "low" + "energy", "cross" + "sectional", "solution" + "based"); an en-dash range between digits stays; a hyphen before a bracketed symbol ("non⟨003⟩") stays. Give the cases, the risk (a real compound like "crystallo-mechanical" vs "crystallomechanical" — what does the paper use?), the tests.
2. **Cluster D — symbols folded to ASCII words.** "275 degreesC" (every temperature), "+/-", "x 10" (×), "<=", ">=", "ohm.cm", "0 degrees gun tilt", "approximately"? Find where it happens: does the PDF's text layer carry "°", "±", "×", "≤", "Ω" (dump the glyphs of a line with "275 °C" on p.3 and one with "±" on p.7 and one with "×" in §2.4), and does a normalizer fold them (`cleanDisplayText`? `normalize` in `pdf-text.ts`? a `replace` table in `pdf-outline.ts` or `reading-map.ts`? the rendering component?) — or does the font map "°" to the string "degrees" in the text layer itself? Give the exact site. Design the fix: keep the Unicode symbols; fold to a word only when the glyph is missing (a private-use code point) — and say what the folding was for (a search/tokenizer need? the question route? the gist?) so the fix does not break it; if a tokenizer needs ASCII, fold at the tokenizer, not in the text.
3. **Cluster E — a figure caption inside a sentence at a page break.** §2.2: "…(Figure 5, expanded view of Figure" | [CAPTION Figure 5] | "4C(iii)) reveals that…" — the paragraph that runs across p.7→p.8 is cut by p.8's caption. 4. **Cluster F — multi-line equations torn into one-line paragraphs.** §4.5: ten one-line paragraphs ("I /I", "(hkl) 0(hkl)", "TC = (hkl) 1", "N I /I", "n (hkl) 0(hkl)", "I(hkl)", "PF = x 100", "(hkl)", "I", "n (hkl)"); §2.1's end: "…calculated as I(hkl)" | "x 100, and the quantitative distribution of each as a" | "n I(hkl) three-"phase" system…".
