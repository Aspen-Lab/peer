## The rule (ruling §1h.73 (b))

1. **The opening is the paragraph's first sentence, whole, when it is at most 400 characters** (`OPENING_MAX` 400, the constant's comment saying why: the owner's paper puts 92 % of its first sentences under it, and an opening is read inside a row's fold).
2. **A first sentence over 400 is cut at a word with one "…"** and no stop, comma, colon or dash before it (the `leadCaption` rule), so a cut line never reads as finished.
3. Everything else as it is: where the sentence ends (abbreviations, "Fig. 4", "et al.", initials as the function already handles), the starts (X9: no lowercase start, no reference number), a formula's introduction (a line ending on ":" that introduces an island is whole).
