2. **Resizing:** a visible draggable boundary between each neighboring pair, horizontal resize affordance matching the pane design: ≥3:1 line/grip contrast, full hit-strip cursor row-resize, focus/hover/drag feedback, role separator and orientation horizontal, current value/min/max, appropriate Up/Down/Home/End keys and bounds. 3. **Session and close:** ratios live in the page/session store only, no shared or per-user persistence. Re-render/scroll and window resizing retain shares subject to measured minima. Opening a new composition initially distributes equally; closing a card gives its released share equally to the remaining cards while preserving their remaining differences. Reopening obeys the initial-composition rule. Stable visual card order follows opening order; focus/header activation makes a card active for Escape without relocating its panel. No focus trap, stale drag, event leak, or unbounded update loop. Closing one leaves the other contents and scrolls intact.
