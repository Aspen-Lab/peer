## The defect

C P11-45 found it while measuring: the reading controls' **Larger text / Smaller text** steps (`reading-controls.tsx`, `--reading-scale`) do not scale the paper's own text on the **Paper digestion** tab.

The scale is applied at the use site by three rules in `web/src/app/globals.css` (6-09, about `:895`):
- `.reading-scaled [class~="text-lead"]`
- `[class~="text-body"]`
- `[class~="text-body-lg"]`

The paper's body is drawn with `font-reading text-title …` (`paper-body.tsx:1079`), and its headings with `text-title` (`:1074`). Neither is among the three, so a reader who presses Larger text on Paper digestion sees nothing change.

The controls' own comment says what they are for: "only the article text these buttons control moves".

## The rule (ruling §1h.152)

1. **What the steps scale.** The text-size steps scale **every piece of reading text inside the `.reading-scaled` wrappers**:
   - the paper's body and its section headings on Paper digestion;
   - figure captions;
   - the Summary's blocks;
   - the answers;
   - the question bar's and the report's prose.

   They do **not** scale:
   - the controls;
   - the labels (mono captions, the eyebrow labels);
   - anything outside the wrappers (the left pane, the margin's cards, the masthead, the legend).

   Inventory every `text-*` class used inside the two wrappers (`reader-layout.tsx:195–200`). Give each one a verdict, scaled or not, with the reason. Add the rules the verdicts need, in the same use-site form (`.reading-scaled [class~="text-title"] { font-size: calc(var(--text-title) * var(--reading-scale, 1)); }`), checking that each token exists as a custom property.
2. **Nothing else changes.** At scale 1 every computed font size is identical to the head, on both tabs. The left pane, the cards, the masthead and the legend are identical at every scale. C P11-45's keep-place holds the reader's line across a step, so do not touch it.

**Yours:** in `globals.css`, only the 6-09 `.reading-scaled` rules and their comment; and a test, either `globals.css.test.ts` or a new file.

**Not yours:**
- every other region of `globals.css`;
- every component, including `reader-layout.tsx` and `paper-body.tsx`. These are read-only; if a class must change, stop and say so.
