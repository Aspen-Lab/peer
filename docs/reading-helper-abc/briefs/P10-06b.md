## The rules

1. **Terms meet in a paragraph.** A section's score becomes `0.5 × (the best of its paragraphs' scores) + 0.5 × (the section score as built)`, where a paragraph's score is the same formula over the paragraph's own counts with a **co-occurrence bonus**: `× (1 + 0.5 × (k − 1))` for `k` distinct question terms the paragraph mentions (k ≥ 2). So a paragraph that names the growth parameters, 110 and LCO together outscores a long introduction that names each somewhere. Pure, tested on invented sections (two sections with equal sums, one with the terms in one paragraph → that one strong).
2. **A term's forms match.** The question's term and the paper's word match when they share a stem by a small, explicit rule (no dependency): a common prefix of ≥ 4 letters after stripping the suffixes `-ing`, `-ed`, `-s`, `-es`, `-tion`, `-th` (growing / growth / grown / grow; deposition / deposited; parameters / parameter), never for a term under 4 letters or a number (`110` matches `110` and `(110)` and `{110}` exactly, as now). The mentions line counts the matched forms under the reader's term (`growing ×6` counts growth and grown too — say so in the checkpoint; the facts line stays a count a reader can check by the forms).
