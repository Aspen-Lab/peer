## The defect

Round 7's Theme-B walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-65-A-20261022T1010Z.md`, item 2 and (g); its matrix `z21` — script `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r7b/h/z21-triple-matrix.mjs`, dumps `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r7b/dumps/z21-triple-matrix-*.json`, read-only for you) measured at 1024 × 768, 1093 × 614, 1280 × 720, 1366 × 768 and 1440 × 900:

- A **triple click** on a paragraph (so the paragraph is selected) **followed by a drag released over the LEFT pane's text or a pane control** ends the selection in the pane: **12 of 100** gestures (1,649 characters at 1440; 1,641 at 1280; 4 characters over a pane control), **no button offered**. The same drag after a single press is clean in 100 of 100 (C P11-45b's correction); a double-click-and-drag is clean.
- Mechanism as the walk read it: Chromium keeps the triple click's **paragraph granularity** while the drag is held and extends the selection into the pane's own block, so the selection starts in the paper and ends in a block that is no target (P10 B3), so the three-button group does not appear. `keep-selection.ts` (C P11-45b / P11-57 / P11-62) follows a press that began in the column's text and corrects the selection's end at the release (`endSelectionInColumn`) — for a multi-click press the correction either does not run, or runs and the browser's granularity puts the end back. **Find out which, by execution, before changing anything** (a unit pin and a real-browser probe), and say in the checkpoint.

## The rule (ruling §1h.180)

1. **A triple-click-and-drag released beside the column (over the left pane, the margin, a separator) or beyond the visible box (above the masthead, below the window, over the foot legend) ends the selection at the same place a triple-click-and-drag released at the column's nearest visible point would.** That is: the end follows the pointer's height at the column's edge, exactly as `endSelectionInColumn` does for a single press — the selection is the triple-clicked paragraph through whatever the browser's paragraph granularity gives at that point in the column. The triple-clicked paragraph is never left out. The buttons are offered as for any selection (three with a key, two without; Highlight only where it makes a mark, P11-50).
2. **Nothing else changes.** A single-press drag, a double-click-and-drag, a plain triple click without a drag (E-2, 693 of 693), a Shift+click (P11-57), a release over the foot legend (P11-62), the separators' own drag and keys, the notes tab's pass-through and the one-column page (BACKLOG-111, not yours) are byte for byte as today.
