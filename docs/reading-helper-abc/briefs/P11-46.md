## The defect

Round 3's Theme-B walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-44-A-20261021T0921Z.md`, E-1, costed 0.5) found the problem. C P11-34 gave the notes edge tab a transparent 9-px `before:` hit strip (`EDGE_TAB_HIT_PX`, `notes-button.tsx`). That strip starts at the text's right edge.

With a card open at 1024–1366 px, a drag released 3 px past a line's end lands on the strip. The selection then runs to the rest of the paper (about 48,500 characters), and no buttons are offered. At 1440 and 1920 the strip is clear of the text.

## The rule (ruling §1h.148)

1. **The strip never catches a drag that started in the paper.** It may only be pressed. A pointer that went down outside it, and above all one that went down in the text, passes through it, so the browser's selection ends at the line's end as it would with no tab there.
   - B P11-44's suggestion is `pointer-events: none` on the strip while a pointer is down outside the tab.
   - Alternatives: make the strip catch only a pointer that goes down on it, or drop the strip (the face's 15 px hit area).
   - Say which you chose and why. A hit area of 24 px stays the target if it can be kept.
2. **Pressing the tab is unchanged.** A press on the tab, its face or its strip, opens and closes the notes window as today. Its clearance of the text (≥ 8 px for the face) is unchanged, and so is its keyboard focus.

Yours: `web/src/components/notes/notes-button.tsx`, and its tests. Not yours: anything else.
