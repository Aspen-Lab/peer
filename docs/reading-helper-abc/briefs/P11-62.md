## The defect

Round 5's Theme-B walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-60-A-20261021T2225Z.md`, §6 E-4, costed 0.25; "For the manager" 1; its dump `y20` and `x35` under `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r5b/pack/`, read-only for you) measured this at 1024 × 768, 1093 × 614, 1242 × 698, 1280 × 720, 1366 × 768, 1440 × 900 and 1920 × 1080, light and dark, with and without a key, with Fit on at 1280 and 1440:

- Select down a long paragraph with the mouse and let go at the window's foot, where a real pointer stops on a maximised window. The **foot legend** ("J NEXT … ESC BACK", the bottom 28 px; `DOCK_BOTTOM_PX`, `legendHeightPx(zoom)` under Fit) is under the pointer, and the selection runs **to the end of the paper** (70,018–70,145 characters), the legend's words highlighted, **no button offered** — in the column's x, at every release height over the legend, at 7 of 7 windows. Released 1 px above the legend the same drag is right (128–445 characters, all three buttons).
- Released over the margin or the left pane at the same heights, the selection is corrected to the last visible line (102–761 characters, the buttons offered) — C P11-45b's correction works there.

Mechanism, read by the walk in `web/src/lib/reader/keep-selection.ts`: `domEnv.visible()` returns `bottom: window.innerHeight - 1` ("the window's last row") although the legend covers the last 28 rows, so `endSelectionInColumn` sees a pointer over the legend inside the column's x as "over the column's visible box" and returns false; the browser then ends the selection in the legend's text, i.e. at the document's end. The masthead is already handled the other way (`top: DOCK_TOP_PX + 1`). Confirm the mechanism by execution before you change anything (a pin on `endSelectionInColumn` with y in the legend's rows, red on the base).
