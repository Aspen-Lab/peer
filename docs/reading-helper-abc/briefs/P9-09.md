## The four rules

1. **Cluster C — a line-end hyphen follows the document's own words** (B2 §1.4–1.5). `HYPHEN_END = /[-‐‑­]$/` — only real hyphens; an en or em dash at a line end is punctuation and keeps its space after it (today the class `[‐-―-]` eats U+2013/U+2014 — `typing —` + `everything` reads `typing everything`, B2's design-spec case). Build once per document `vocabularyOf(lines)`: the words seen whole (a token is excluded when it is a line's last token before a hyphen or the next line's first token), the hyphenated compounds seen whole, the prefixes seen hyphenated. `mend(current, next, vocab)`, in this order: (1) digit before and digit after → keep the hyphen, no space (`10-20`); (2) the next line opens with a non-letter (bracket, symbol, digit) → keep (`non-⟨003⟩||ND`); (3) no letters before the hyphen (`(de)-`) → join; (4) the right token holds a digit, two or more lower-to-upper steps, or opens with a capital → keep (`Li-NixCoyMnzO2`, `Lithium-Ion`, `Raman-AFM-SNOM`); (5) the joined form occurs whole as a word or a compound → join; the hyphenated form occurs whole → keep; (6) the right half or the left half is not a word of the document → join (a fragment); (7) a closed prefix (`nano, micro, macro, meso, poly, mono, multi, inter, intra, electro, chemo, hetero, homo, semi, ultra, sub, super, hyper, pseudo, photo, thermo, non`) that the document never hyphenates → join (`nano-` + `wire`); (8) else both halves are words and the joined form never occurs → keep the hyphen, no space (`deposition-rate-limiting`, `low-energy`, `cross-sectional`, `solution-based`). With no evidence (a one-page upload) steps 5–8 fall to "join", today's behaviour. **The caption fold (~l.919, today `join(" ")`) and the heading wrap (~l.926) call the same `mend`** (`Super-` + `saturations` → `Supersaturations`).
2. **Cluster H — no space at a cut token** (B1 §6.2; B2 §5.4, §5.6). Before the hyphen branch: a line ending in `/` (not ` /`), in `||`, in U+2212, or in a digit followed by a hyphen / en dash / em dash with a digit opening the next line, joins with **no space and the mark kept** (`mA/` + `cm2`, `[Li+]/` + `[Co2+]`, `C/` + `10`, `⟨110⟩||` + `ND`, `20−` + `30`, `4.68−` + `12.50`, `LiOH−` + `KOH`, `20–` + `30` → `20–30`, never `2030`); every other line end joins with a space (`either A /` + `B` → `A / B`; a sentence end keeps its space).
3. **Cluster E — a float's gap is not a paragraph break inside a sentence** (B2 §3.2–3.3; the P9-02 brief's premise was wrong: the caption cuts nothing, the figure's own height does). In `joinProse`: `floatGap = bigGap && gap > pitch × 3 && current is unfinished (no . ! ? : ; at its end, a closing quote or bracket after one still counts as finished) && the next line opens with a lower-case letter, a digit or a bracket` → no break; a capital after the gap still breaks (conservative). `placeFigures` then files the figure after the whole paragraph by its own rule — pin that in `reading.test.ts`.
4. **Cluster F — stacked equation rows are one block** (B2 §4.2–4.3, `foldStacks` in `flush()` before `joinProse`). The page leading = the median of the same-page gaps ≥ 0.6 × the body size. A **stack** = two or more consecutive same-page lines, each a short fragment (≤ 40 characters, no run of 4+ letters, at least one letter or digit, not a `block`), each ≤ 0.8 × leading below the one before. **Display** (the line before ends a sentence or the line after opens with a capital): the rows joined by `\n` become **one unnumbered equation** through the existing path (`equations.push({ text })` + `blockMarker`), so `sectionParagraphs` lifts it and the map lists no scraps. **Inline** (the line before is unfinished and the line after opens lower-case): the rows joined by spaces into the sentence. **Sandwich** (a fragment row, a text row opening with an operator `× = + ÷ − · ∑ Σ /`, a fragment row, all within 0.8 × leading): merged as `numerator / denominator row-text` into the sentence; the operator-start requirement keeps this off subscript labels around a prose row. This is the one exception to the cheap rule of §1h.41 (c): a single line of symbols stays prose; a stack of two or more tight rows is lifted. Update the comments at `pdf-outline.test.ts` ~l.723 and on `equationOf` to say so.
