## The rules

1. **"Back to paper" when the notes page was opened from a paper.** Pressing "Open your notes" on a paper records where the reader was: the paper's id, the tab, the scroll position, and the paragraph at the top of the view as a fallback. 2. **Back on the paper, the place is restored once.** The tab is the one recorded. The scroll position is the one recorded, applied after the reading has rendered. If the page's height changed, it lands on the recorded paragraph instead. Then the record is cleared, so a later visit opens normally. The browser's own Back button keeps working as today.
