## The symptom

B P12-39: **M2 / M4** — a figure whose panels include a raster strip or a vector cluster below the size floors (120 / 120 / 20,000) loses those panels (P3 Fig. 2 panels a–c, Fig. 5 panels e–f), because rasters are never joined and sub-floor clusters are never re-attached. **M3** — a drawing's region is its path boxes only, so axis titles, icon strips and panel letters fall outside the crop (P3 Figs. 3, 4). **M5** — the fixed 10 pt padding crosses into the caption: a sliver of the caption's first line shows under every picture of P1 and P2. **M8** — a caption is cut at 500 characters, and a caption can over-join the paragraph below it (P2 Fig. 3, 74 characters).

## The rules (one commit each, in this order; bump `FIGURE_EXTRACTION_REVISION` in every commit that changes regions or bytes)

1. **R2a, satellites.** After the floors and the caption pairing, grow each **accepted, captioned** region by sub-floor items near it: sub-floor rasters adjacent to it (gap ≤ 8 pt, ≥ half overlap on the other axis), and sub-floor vector clusters at least half the floors (≥ 60 × 60 pt, ≥ 10,000 pt²) that pair with the same caption line and share at least half the region's vertical extent. **The floors stay as they are** (ruled §1h.425): nothing below a floor becomes a figure of its own; satellites only join a region that already passed. Repeat until stable; never grow across another captioned region or a caption.
2. **R3a, figure text.** Then absorb short figure-text lines within **6 pt** of the region, repeatedly: at most 60 characters, not a typed caption, not in the caption's block, above the caption, inside the region's horizontal extent widened by 6 pt. **Required negative test:** the short last line of a body paragraph within 6 pt is **not** absorbed (a line whose block has a line over 60 characters is body text).
3. **R5a, the clip.** When the caption's first line lies within the padding, the padded clip stops at the caption's near edge minus 0.5 pt; otherwise the 10 pt padding is unchanged (the existing "box plus 10 pt" test stays green unchanged).
4. **R8a, the caption's end.** A caption ends at the first line whose gap above exceeds 1.4 × the caption's own median line pitch; with that stop in place, raise the 500-character cap to 1,500. The existing "close-body over-join" test in `pdf-engine.caption-pairing.test.ts` (about lines 138–143) records today's over-join on purpose: **re-aim it** to the new rule and name it in the checkpoint with why — do not delete it.
