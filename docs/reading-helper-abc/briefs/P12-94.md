## The rules

1. **No correction against a part the rules are sure about.**
   - A correction whose part has confidence `high` or `medium` is refused with a new reason, `confident-draft`. This covers `move_start` of that part, `set_role` of it, and a `split` in it.
   - It is judged against the draft as given, like the other checks, in the order the reasons are judged today; place it where it reads naturally and say where.
   - An `unsure` listing of such a part draws no look and changes nothing.
   - So a review changes the map only where the rules said they were unsure. A disagreement with a sure part still shows in the logs as a refusal.
2. **A correction against a `low` part needs a second look that agrees.**
   - Such a part draws a look, with a new kind `contradicts-low` ranked after the others. The cap on looks is unchanged.
   - Its corrections stand only on `review`. On `draft`, on a failed look, or when no look happened because the cap was reached, they go.
   - An `unsure` listing of a `low` part keeps today's behaviour.
3. **The lower bound.** The corrections a reply ends with after this change must be a subset of those it ended with before, for every draft, reply and look verdict.
   - Show it with a differential test: randomised drafts, replies and verdicts, at least 20,000 cases, old function against new.
   - Keep the old function's copy in the test file only.
