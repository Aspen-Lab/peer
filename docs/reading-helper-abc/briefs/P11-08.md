## The rules

1. **The row.** New `web/src/components/notes/highlight-row.tsx`, drawn by `NoteEditor` beside the `divider` and `paper` rows in `web/src/components/notes/note-editor.tsx`. It serves both the notes window and `/notes/<id>`. Its parts, left to right:
   - A 14-px square in the highlight's colour that goes to the passage via `goToPassage` and flashes it (`aria-label="Go to the passage"`).
   - The words on their colour, at most two lines, ending in an ellipsis when cut.
   - A colour button, named by the colour's name, that opens P11-07's `HighlightSwatches`.
   - An ×.

   A passage the paper no longer holds shows `PASSAGE_LOST_LINE` and no go-to square (`useLostPassages`, keyed by the block id). The block menu offers Delete only. Duplicate, turn-into and the type menu leave a highlight alone.
2. **The exports** (`web/src/lib/notes/export.ts`; replace the contract's `case "highlight": return null`):
   - Markdown: its own paragraph, `==words==`.
   - Plain text: its own paragraph, the words.
   - Word and PDF: `<p><mark style="background:<fill>;color:<ink>">words</mark></p>`, with `mso-highlight:yellow` for Yellow only.
   - Copy: as plain text.
   - A sticky's quote stays out: `export.quote.test.ts` and `export.anchor.test.ts` stay true. Update the header comment and `notes-window.tsx`'s comment above `EXPORT_ITEMS` (one hunk).
