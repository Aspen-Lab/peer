## The defect

Round 4's Theme-B walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-52-A-20261021T1600Z.md`, §6 E-3, costed 0.25; "For the manager" 2; its dump `y15` under `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-peer/9602921a-6ac5-5952-88ce-8364fcd8f733/scratchpad/p11/r4b/pack/`, read-only for you) measured this at 1024 × 768, 1093 × 614, 1280 × 720 and 1366 × 768:

- Click on a word in the paper, then **Shift+click** just past the end of a line to take the lines between. A Shift+click **12 to 23 px past the line's end** (over the margin's separator) **clears the selection**; one **40 px or more past it** (over the margin) extends the selection **to the end of the paper** (47,868 characters) with no buttons; 40 and 120 px **before a line's start** it ends in the left pane (27,494–27,528 characters) or is cleared (1093). Within 9 px past the end it is right (47–77 characters). 1440 is clean to 40 px, its column being capped.
- A drag released at the same places is right in 500 of 500 cases (C P11-45b).

Mechanism, from C P11-45b's own design (`lib/reader/keep-selection.ts`): the hook follows **a mouse press that began in the column's text** and corrects the selection's end while that press is held and at its release. A Shift+click's press begins **outside** the column, so it is not followed; the browser then extends the standing selection to the place under the pointer (the margin's `aside`, a card, the left pane) or collapses it (the separator). The hook's `endSelectionInColumn` already computes the right end for any (x, y); only the trigger is missing.

## The rules (ruling §1h.165)

1. **A Shift+click outside the column, while a selection that began in the column stands, ends that selection at the column's nearest visible point to the pointer** — the same place a drag's release there gives (`endSelectionInColumn`): the column's edge at the pointer's height beside it; the first line under the masthead or the last above the window's foot above or below. The correction runs after the browser's own extension and before the paint (the next animation frame), so there is nothing to see; the buttons are offered as for any selection.
   - Trigger: a primary mouse `pointerdown` with `shiftKey` whose target is **not** in the column, while `window.getSelection()` has an anchor in the column and is not collapsed. Read the selection at the frame after the press (the browser extends it on the press, not on the release).
   - A Shift+click **inside** the column is the browser's, as today. A Shift+click on a control (the `CONTROL_SELECTOR`) is the control's, as today. A Shift+click outside the column with **no** column selection standing is left alone (the browser's own behaviour, whatever it is).
   - Say how you treat the 12–23 px band over the separator, where the browser **collapses** the selection on the press: the selection must still end at the line's end (the anchor is still known at the frame after; if the browser has collapsed it, restore it from the anchor you read at the press).
2. **Nothing else changes.** A drag (C P11-45b) is byte for byte as today; a press that begins outside the column without Shift is as today; the separators' own drag and keys, the notes tab's pass-through and the one-column page (BACKLOG-111, not yours) are untouched; touch and pen are not followed.
