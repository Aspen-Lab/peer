## The rules (B P12-73 §2 M2 option A, M5, M7 R7a; ruled §1h.449)

1. **M2 (A) the group row.** In `flush`: an unnumbered heading with no text of its own whose whole heading is a part name (B's regex: methods / methodology / experimental …, results (and discussion), discussion, conclusion(s), introduction, with the optional "online / extended" and "materials and" prefixes) is **kept as a row with no words**, at a new optional `level` 1. Later unnumbered headings **set smaller than it by more than 0.3 pt** are its children at `level` 2 and take its bucket (as numbered children do), unless their own bucket is strong (limitations, references, acknowledgments, supplementary, abstract). A heading as large or a strong one ends the group. `level` is carried by `normalize`, kept by `dropStrayHeadings` and `readableSections`, and read by `readingMapOf` as the row's level. A loose trigger by `canonicalizeHeading` is rejected (it breaks P6, B M2-C).
2. **M5 lexical** (before the unanchored `experiment` → results line): a heading with `experiment(s)` **and** one of sample / specimen / preparation / synthesis / fabrication / assembly / measurement(s) / setup / details → methods.
3. **M7 R7a**: `discussion | implication(s) | outlook` → discussion, placed before the methods words.

Nothing else in `canonicalizeHeading` changes (B's note on `model` / `validation` is recorded as backlog, not yours).
