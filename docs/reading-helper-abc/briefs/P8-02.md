## The owner's words (the spec)

> AI窗口应该是一直开启的，而且固定一直在文字的右侧空白处，所有AI窗口应该只会和任何被选中的文字段落的第一行持平

The AI window should stay open; it should always stand in the blank space to the right of the text; and it should be level with the first line of whichever text paragraph is selected. Today (P7-15) selecting the next passage closes the dock (the page widens) and "Explain this?" opens it again (the page narrows) — the churn the owner saw.

## The behaviour to build — from a window of 1024 px (the dock case); the card (640–1023) and the phone sheet (< 640) are unchanged

1. **Stays open.** Once the window is open, a new selection in the body does **not** close it (§1h.3's "a new selection closes" is amended for the dock only). The "Explain this?" button still appears at the new selection; pressing it, or `e`, explains the new passage **in the same window**: the previous passage's thread is replaced (kept for its passage as today — re-explaining a passage opens its kept answer with no request), with **no close and reopen and no reflow**: `reserveDock`'s properties stay on the document from the first open to the close (spy on `removeProperty`: never called between two passages). Escape and the Close button still close it, and only then does the page get its width back. A press outside does nothing (P7-15). `e` while the window is open: if the current selection is a different passage from the open one, it explains the selection; otherwise it focuses the input, as today.
2. **Level with the passage.** The window's top is level with the first line of the **paragraph** the explained passage belongs to (the owner: "被选中的文字段落的第一行" — the paragraph element's top, not the selection's own first line): `top = clamp(paragraphTop, DOCK_TOP_PX, viewport.height − DOCK_BOTTOM_PX − DOCK_MIN_VISIBLE_PX)`, re-measured as the page scrolls and the window resizes (the card already re-measures through `selection.measure()`; the paragraph's rect must be reachable from the selection — add what `paper-body.tsx` must report, nothing more). Clamped so that it never stands above the masthead's foot (`DOCK_TOP_PX`, where it stays while the paragraph is scrolled above) and never so low that less than `DOCK_MIN_VISIBLE_PX` (a new constant in `explain-dock.ts`, 160) of it is visible above the foot legend. Its height is its content, at most `viewport.height − top − DOCK_BOTTOM_PX`, scrolling inside. The window **moves when the next passage is explained** (the press or `e`), not on a mere selection (a selection can be transient; the window belongs to the passage it explains).
3. **Right edge flush, 360 wide, the page's width kept clear** — as P7-15 (`right: 0`, `DOCK_WIDTH_PX`, `--explain-dock` 376 px) — unchanged; the notes tab beside it unchanged.
4. **Comments** in `explain-box.tsx` (the header) and `explain-dock.ts` say the new rule; `copy.ts` unchanged; no new wording on the screen.
