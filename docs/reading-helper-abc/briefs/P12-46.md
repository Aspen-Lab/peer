## The symptom

B P12-39: **M6** — the `CAPTION` pattern (`pdf-outline.ts` about line 178) takes a body-text line that cites a figure in parentheses ("…(Fig. 8)…" shape, at a line start) as a caption, so the figure list gains an entry the paper does not print (an EXTRA). **M7** — `continuesCaption` (about lines 244–266, the face test near 255) ends a caption when the face changes after a bold title line, so a margin caption shows its first line only.

## The rules

1. **R6a.** `CAPTION` matches only when the label's number (and optional panel letter) is followed by a separator: `.` `:` `|` a dash, or whitespace then a capital letter; `)` `,` `;` after the number refuse. Test: a parenthesised citation at a line start is not a caption; the real-caption shapes of the existing tests still are.
2. **R7a.** In `continuesCaption`, one change of face is allowed **directly after the caption's first line** when the next line's size and line pitch match the caption's; a second face change, or a size / pitch mismatch, still ends it (the reason the face test exists — prose under a figure in a mixed-face paper — keeps its test, green unchanged).
3. Nothing else changes: headings, sections, the reading map, the engine.

## Measure

Invented outline input (as the existing `pdf-outline` tests build it): (a) a body line beginning with a parenthesised figure citation — before: listed as a caption; after: not; (b) a caption with a bold first line and regular following lines of the same size and pitch — before: one line; after: all lines; (c) prose under a figure in another face at a different pitch — stays outside the caption before and after.
