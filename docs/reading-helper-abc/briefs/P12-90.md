## The rules

1. **No one-paragraph piece.** P6's damage keeps its shape: in one run of five, the review makes two splits inside the first results part, one paragraph apart ("method" at 11, "evidence" at 12). A one-paragraph piece flips a short row's majority. Phase 2's damage had the same shape with "setup", which C P12-86 now refuses.
   - **Rule:** `sanitizeReview` refuses a split, or a pair of splits on the same part, that would leave any piece shorter than **2 paragraphs**, counted after the part's other accepted splits and its moved start.
   - **The one exception** is a piece that is the last paragraph of the draft's last evidence part, with the role interpretation: a closing, as the rules' own closing split makes.
   - The refusal is counted under a new reason, `piece-too-short`.
2. **A refused correction logs its numbers.** P4 falls back every time on one correction refused as out of range: read 1, rejected 1, the same 95-character reply each run. The log cannot say which number was out of range.
   - **Rule:** for each refused correction (at most 8), the route's log line gives its op, its part, its ordinal (`toParagraph` / `atParagraph`), and the bounds it was judged against (`draft.length`, the paragraph count, the part's from and to).
   - These are numbers only. No role text beyond the five role names, no reply text, no paper text, no key.
