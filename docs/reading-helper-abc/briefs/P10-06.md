## The rules

1. **A score per section per question.** For each of the question's specific terms, `idf = ln((N + 1) / (df + 1)) + 0.1` over the paper's N scored sections (df = the sections mentioning the term — a term everywhere scores little, a term in two sections scores much); the section's score `Σ idf(term) × ln(1 + count(term))`, divided by `ln(2 + words / 200)` so a long section does not win by length alone. Pure, tested on invented sections with known counts. A group row (no paragraphs; `parentId` undefined, `level` 1 — P10-04's fields, when present) takes the max of its children's scores and the union of their hits; a child is scored on its own paragraphs.
2. **Three tiers by the score (A9).** `RouteTier` becomes `"strong" | "medium" | "faint" | "none"` (the gist route's roles map onto them: `read → strong`, `skim → faint`, `background` stays a fourth mark as now): `strong` for a section with score ≥ 0.6 × max **and** among the top three by score (ties broken by document order), `medium` for score ≥ 0.3 × max, `faint` for any hit, `none` for none. With one section only, it is strong. The tokens: `--color-route-strong` (the darkest green), `--color-route-medium`, `--color-route-faint` (the lightest) in light and dark, replacing `read` / `skim`; `ROUTE_TINT` and `markedClass` follow; the facts line reads `strongest · mentions …` / `relevant · mentions …` / `mentions …` (the exact words in `ROUTE.tiers`, yours to choose, short, in Peer's label face — not "read"/"skim", which told the reader what to do); "not mentioned" stays.
3. **Paragraph lines graded the same way** within a section: a paragraph's score by the same formula over its own words (df over paragraphs of the section); the top paragraph(s) of a strong section strong, the rest faint; a paragraph line with no hit untinted.
4. **Selectivity on the owner's question (A11)** — measured, not asserted: on the owner's paper with "I want to learn the parameter for growing 110 LCO", at most three rows strong (expected: the growth / experimental ones — 2.1 and 4.1 or their parents), at least a third of the rows none or faint; and with the owner's other question (screenshot 2.1: "I want to learn the relationship between LCO properties such as crystallography, grain structure, morphology, with various plating variable like temperature, current, etc.") the tint still orders by relevance with ≤ 3 strong.
