On a paper's "Before you read" box they appear as ONE extra chip group; pressing a chip adds that text as a question line (as an example tag does). Never auto-filled: a paper opens with no question the reader did not press or type, ever; with no standing questions the box is byte-identical to today. Same limits as every question: up to 5 standing questions, each ≤ 200 characters, English-only Peer words around them, the reader's own words kept as typed.
