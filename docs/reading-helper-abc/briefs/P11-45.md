## The defect

Round 3's Theme-A walk (`docs/reading-helper-abc/P11-43-A-20261021T0905Z.md`, its note "The place is not kept when a boundary is dragged", and "For the manager" 1) measured this at 1280 × 720, scrolled to 8,000.

| Change | Column width | Page height | Paragraph at the top of the view |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left separator dragged +60 | 552 → 492 px | +3,128 px | `paper-section-2-p6` → `-p5` |
| Margin separator dragged −80 | 552 → 472 px | +4,415 px | → `-p4` |

At 1093 × 614, 3 of 4 drags moved it by 1 to 2 paragraphs. A collapse or a pin re-flows 552 → 646 by the same mechanism.

The browser keeps the scroll offset while the column's width changes, and nothing re-anchors the reader's place. The page already knows how to keep a place: `lib/notes/return-to-paper.ts` `paragraphAtTop` is what the Back-to-paper return uses.

The owner's item 2 ("分界可拖动，正文只换行不变大小"): the text re-wraps, so the reader should not lose their line.

## The rule (ruling §1h.146)

1. **The same paragraph stays at the top.** When the centre column's width changes, the paragraph at the top of the view, and its offset from the masthead's foot, stay where they were, ±1 line, once the layout settles. The changes in scope are:
   - a separator drag, by mouse or by its keys;
   - a collapse or a pin of the left pane;
   - Fit on or off;
   - the Larger / Smaller text steps;
   - a window resize.

   Within a long paragraph, keep the line nearest the top: the same fraction into the paragraph is enough. Say how you do it.
2. **Leave the page alone when the reader is at the top or the bottom.** At scroll 0, or at the page's foot, nothing moves: no re-anchor fights the reader's own scroll.
3. **No jank.** A drag re-anchors at the end of the drag, or on each frame without a visible jump; say which and why. A drag must not run a scroll on every pointer move if that shows. No smooth scroll; reduced motion is respected.
4. **Nothing else changes.** The pane widths, the map's `scrollTop`, an open card's place (it follows its paragraph, as today), the Back-to-paper return and the arrivals stay as today.
