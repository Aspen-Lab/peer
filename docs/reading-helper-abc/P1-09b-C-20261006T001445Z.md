## Choices beyond the brief

1. **`phrasesFromText(text)` is called with its default `max` (8).** The P1-09 calls used `(…, 4)` and `(…, 2)`.
   - `phrasesFromText` returns at most `ceil(max/2)` chunk phrases before its single-keyword tier.
   - So `(…, 2)` could never give the project its 2 multi-word phrases, and `(…, 4)` could never give the challenges their 3.
   - With 8, up to 4 chunk phrases come back, which is more than either cap.
2. **The fallback is one single word, not up to the cap.** The brief says "fall back to a single word" and its test "still yields one". P1-09's observation 1 objected to examples like "Does this help with dendrite?" / "…growth?". Offering three single words would bring those back.
3. **A duplicate does not use a source's slot.** The cap counts examples actually added. If topic 1 duplicates an earlier question, topic 3 can appear. This is pinned by the test "an example already offered does not use up its source's slot".
