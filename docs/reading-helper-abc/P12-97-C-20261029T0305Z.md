## 2. The rules as built (all in `pdf-outline.ts`)

**R-F.** Two parts, both in `furnitureOf` / `isFurniture`.
- *The key.* A new `footKey` = `headKey` (digits to `#`, case folded, spaces collapsed) with every non-letter taken off both ends of the line, unless that leaves nothing (a bare folio keeps its key). `furnitureOf` keys by it, and `isFurniture` asks a `RunningHeads` by it. "12 | Journal ... (2026)" and "Journal ... (2026) | 13" are one head. A plain `Set<string>` (a caller's own, `extended-data.test.ts`) is still asked by `headKey`: its contract is unchanged.
- *The share.* The threshold is `max(3, ceil(0.4 x P))` where P = the pages with 15 lines or more (`pagesWithText`; `FURNITURE_TEXT_PAGE_LINES`). When no page has so many, P is `pageCount` as before, so the small pages of the existing tests are read exactly as they were. P never exceeds `pageCount`.

**R-B.** `isPanelLine`: a line below 0.95 of the body size is held to `PANEL_SMALL_LINE_MAX` = 60 characters; any other line stays at `PANEL_LINE_MAX` = 25. Nothing else in the predicate moved (sentence end, caption, equation, the four-letter word test).

**R-A.** `figureTextOf`: a run is beside a caption when (a) one of its lines is within 8 body sizes of a caption on its page (unchanged) **or** (b) one of its lines is in a caption's *stretch*. A caption's stretch is built from its first line walking up and from its last line (`captionEnd`) walking down, over `read`, on the caption's page. It passes furniture and margin-print lines (`skipped`) and every non-body line; it stops at the first body line (`isBodyLine`: within `BODY_LINE_SLACK` = 5 % of the body size and `BODY_LINE_MIN` = 40 characters), at a line taken by any caption (`taken`), or at a page change.

Not built, as briefed: R-A "any caption on the page", R-C, runs of 2, R-Box.

3. **Two judgement calls inside the B's words.** (a) `footKey` takes off every non-letter at both ends, not only a number next to a bar: the B's "either end, next to a bar" cannot tell a folio from a volume that ends the head ("...Notes 12 | 345" against "345 | ...Notes 12"), and trimming both ends gives the same key to both. Two heads that differ only in what stands beyond their first and last letter are one head; each must still sit in the band on enough pages. (b) When no page has 15 lines the share is of all pages, as before; the B's variant did not say.
4. **A heading can pass the stretch or stop it.** A heading larger than the body, or short, is a non-body line and the stretch goes through it. A heading set at the body's size with 40 characters or more (a long run-in or bold heading in the body's size) is a "body line" by the B's definition and ends the stretch.
5. **What this does not reach, as the B lists it:** the figure whose run the reading order puts after a body paragraph (P4 4, P5 3), lines at or above the body with a four-letter word (R-C), runs of one or two (R-D), formula and code fragments, and the copyright line.
