## 2. The rules as built

- **R1** (`candidateOf`, numbered path): a numbered candidate set under `NUMBERED_SIZE_RATIO` = 0.95 of the body is no heading, whatever its face. The constant sits next to `HEADING_SIZE_RATIO` and is shared with R4.
- **R2** (`numberedOut`): a numbered line in the body's face (`line.font === body.font`) and not set apart (`!isSetApart`, so under 1.12 of the body) that `followsOpenProse` (the line above is body prose in the same column, one pitch up, not ending a sentence, not a paragraph's last line) is out — **unless** `candidateOf(line above)` is not null (stacked numbered headings in plain fixtures). It `continue`s before the numbering is touched, so the refused number does not move the paper's numbering. Pitch: `linePitch(lines)`, computed once.
- **R3** (`candidateOf`, before the numbered path, so numbered and unnumbered candidates alike): `PANEL_LETTER = /(?:^|\s)[b-h](?=\s|$)/` → null. A lone `a` (a word) and `i` are not panel letters.
- **R4** (`continuesCaption`): the numbered-line stop needs `next.size >= body.size * NUMBERED_SIZE_RATIO`; a caption's own number-led middle line, set small, goes on.
- **R5** (`candidateOf`): `QUOTED_KEY = /['"][\w .-]+['"]\s*:/` → null (straight quotes only, as B's).
- **M6 block** (`marginPrintOf`, whose `body` now carries the face; its two callers already passed it; new `footnoteOf`): on the **opening page** (the lowest page number among the lines) only, small print (`size < 0.95 × body`) **below the edge** = the lowest line on that page in the body's face and within 0.3 pt of the body's size (`BODY_SIZE_SLACK`, strict) with at least 30 characters (`PROSE_LINE_MIN`: a folio or "Page 1 of 9" is not prose), that is:
  - set apart: the topmost such line is more than one pitch under the edge (`linePitch`, or 1.2 × body when there is none),
  - **opens with a footnote marker**: `/^[\d∗*†‡§¶]\s?\p{L}/u` (B's regex),
  - at most `MARGIN_PRINT_ROWS` (8) rows (3 pt buckets),
  - and no line of the block opens a caption (`captionOf`) — stricter than "the first line is not a caption": the marker test already rules that one out, so the block is refused when any of its lines is a caption's.

  Lines of that block are margin print; the old 0.8-ratio rule is untouched. If the opening page holds no prose line of 30 characters the set is empty (no `Infinity` edge: B's prototype would have taken every small line of such a page).

## 7. For the manager

1. **`:441` is not what the brief said.** In that fixture the affiliation stays (it stands mid-text); the reference entries at the foot became margin print. Both assertions about the affiliation hold. If you want the entries kept, the cheapest way is to let a body-face line of any length be the edge (drop `PROSE_LINE_MIN`), but then a folio or "Page 1 of 9" at the foot is the edge and a real footnote above it stays (the new folio test pins the 30-character minimum). I kept B's measured shape.
2. **Constants I took from B's prototype, not measured by me:** `PROSE_LINE_MIN` = 30; `BODY_SIZE_SLACK` = 0.3 pt, strict (`< 0.3`). B's prototype used `> 0.3` on a floating-point difference; B reports the Nature foot as 8.0 pt against an 8.3 body, a difference of 0.3, which is exactly at the edge. With the strict `<`, a foot at exactly 0.3 under the body is not the edge (the footnote above it is removed, as B measured); a foot only 0.2 under would be the edge and the footnote above it would stay. I could not measure P3–P6 (no PDF read). **Please re-run B's six maps and look at P5 and P6's introduction (116 and 68 words of footnote) first.**
3. **Stricter than the brief on one point:** the M6 block is refused when *any* line of it opens a caption, not only its first line (the marker test already excludes a first line that is a caption). Mutation-tested.
4. **R2 asks for the body's face on the line itself** (`line.font === body.font`), where B's prototype asked only `!apart`; the two differ only for a small line in another face, which R1 already refuses.
5. **Known costs, as B named them:** R1 — a paper that sets its Methods under 0.95 of the body *and* numbers its sub-headings at that size loses them; R3 — a heading with a lone `b`–`h` token ("Hepatitis b") is no heading; R5 — a heading that quotes a word and puts a colon right after the closing quote. Also the marker test takes `5 μm` as a marker (a digit, a space, a letter) when it is the first small line under the last prose line of the opening page and no caption stands in the block.
6. **Not tested because not observable through `buildOutline`:** the `edge === Infinity` guard in `footnoteOf` (an opening page with no prose line of 30 characters, a cover).
7. **Merging with C P12-75 / C P12-76:** my hunks in `pdf-outline.ts` are the constants after `HEADING_SIZE_RATIO` (top), the `continuesCaption` doc and one line, `marginPrintOf` + `footnoteOf` + four constants (`FOOTNOTE_MARKER`, `PROSE_LINE_MIN`, `BODY_SIZE_SLACK`, `SMALL_PRINT_RATIO`), `PANEL_LETTER` / `QUOTED_KEY` and the `candidateOf` lines, and `numberedOut`. None is in `buildOutline`, `joinProse` or `OutlineSection`. In the tests, `pdf-outline.headings.test.ts` has my two re-aims (the line that was `:376`, and the block that was `:441`); the other two files are mine alone.
8. **R2 reads `followsOpenProse` without `leftOut`** (the lines the reading leaves out of the prose), because `numberedOut` has none; a figure-text line in the body's face directly above a number-led body-face line counts as open prose. Not seen on any fixture; a measure on the real papers would show it.
