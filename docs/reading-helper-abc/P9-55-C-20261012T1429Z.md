## 2. The rule as built, and the choice

**The h1's top does not move after the first paint on a keyed first visit** — because the report no longer has a way into the lead plate. The three candidates, measured:

- *The caption's room held with the box* — rejected without building: it leaves a hole under every plate whose figure has no caption (the invented abstract case, a report that binds no figure), and a room that collapses when no caption comes is a shift the other way.
- *The caption inside the plate's own box* — not built: the picture area would give up the caption's height whenever there is a caption (a third of the 180-px box at 1440: three lines are 60.8 px at the page's caption type), on a plate that is already 320 × 180. See §8 item 3.
- **The plate shows the figure it can know at the first paint — the figure route's answer for this paper — and the report's bound figure is drawn where the report draws its sections. Built.**

*Built.* The page's `Reader` no longer builds a `boundFigure` from the report and no longer hands one to the plate. The lead plate's picture, caption, held slot and frame all read the figure route's answer (`resolvedFigure`) and nothing else:

```
const plateImage = resolvedFigure.imageUrl && !resolvedFigure.hideFigure ? resolvedFigure.imageUrl : null;
const caption = leadCaption(plateImage ? resolvedFigure.caption : null, abstractText);   // P9-49's filter, unchanged
const leadPending = leadFigurePending(resolvedFigure);
const hasPlate = Boolean(resolvedFigure.imageUrl) || plateTerms.length > 0 || leadPending;
const plateIsFigure = Boolean(plateImage);
```

So the plate is the same object with a key and without one, with a cached report and without one — the object the briefing's tile shows (`paper-plate.tsx`'s own header: "the object the reader swipes on `/papers/[id]` is the object they saw in the briefing"). The swap the report used to cause (the picture the reader was looking at replaced 2-3 s in by the model's pick) is gone with the shift.

*The report's bound figure is drawn once, by its section.* The set of pictures already shown (`boundShown`) was seeded with the plate's bound figure; with the plate no longer taking it, that seed would have marked the figure shown and drawn it nowhere — a lost picture. It is now seeded with the plate's own picture (`plateImage`), and `takeBound` (the proposal block's and each result's figure) stands down only for a figure that IS the plate's picture: `isSameFigure(plate, bound)` (new, exported, pure, in `paper-plate.tsx`) — the same URL (an HTML source's own picture), or, for a PDF, the same caption (the route hands the plate a `data:` URL, the binder hands the report the figure-image route's URL for the same region, P9-53; the caption is the extractor's on both sides). Only an exact URL or an exact caption (white space made single, case ignored) counts; a plate with no picture is no match; a false "different" draws one picture twice, a false "same" would lose one, so the test is strict. The plate's picture in the set also stops a section's self-found figure from repeating it.

*What the reader sees (keyed first visit, no cached report; the route answers at 4.5 s, the report at 2.5 s).*
- **Before (the head):** 0-2.55 s the held mat (320 × 180 at 1440, 350 × 197 at 390), no picture, no caption. At 2.55 s the report lands, the plate's picture becomes the report's Figure 4 (9:16, contained) with its caption (137 characters, three lines: `Figure 4. A profile of the crust … drawn from the photographs of the second…`), and the title and the decision block move **+68.8 px at 1440, +68.7 px at 390**. At 4.5 s the route's answer is ignored.
- **After:** 0-4.56 s the held mat. At 2.55 s the report lands and **the plate does not change**: the report's Figure 4 and its caption are drawn by the report's own proposal block on the Summary tab (1440: y 421 in the right column; 390: y 1,380) and stand as the paper's own Figure 4 in the Paper digestion body (the default tab for a paper read in full). At 4.56 s the route's picture (the page-1 graphic) fills the mat; its caption is the abstract block, so none (P9-49's filter). The h1 stands at 252 (1440) / 236.9 (390) from the first paint to the last sample.
- **No picture is lost:** the route's figure leads (the head showed it nowhere on a keyed page that bound a figure), the report's pick is on the Summary tab, the paper's own figures are in the body. Checked with the route made to answer with the very figure the report binds: the plate shows it (picture + caption) and the Summary tab draws it **once**, at 1440 and at 390.

*What it costs.* (1) On a keyed page the lead picture is the route's pick (a page-1 graphic, the first extracted figure), not the report's pick; the report's pick, with its caption, is one tab away (Summary) and no longer on the first view of a paper read in full. (2) A keyed revisit of an upload (cached report): the head drew the bound figure at the first paint; now the held mat fills with the route's picture at the route's answer (4-5 s for an upload — `settled` never remembers an upload's figure; for a public paper opened from the feed the route is settled or edge-cached and the picture is there at the first paint). The box is held, so nothing moves. (3) A paper whose route answers no figure (or hides it) but whose report binds one: the plate is the terms plate or goes (as for no key), the bound figure is on the Summary tab only. (4) The route's own late caption still moves the title when it comes with its picture — §6.2, §8 item 3.

**The dead helpers.** `plateCaption` and `CAPTION_CHARS` in `reader/copy.ts`: `rg -nw "plateCaption|CAPTION_CHARS" web/src web/scripts web/public` finds them only in `copy.ts` itself, in two comments (`paper-plate.tsx`, `lead-caption.test.ts`: "the head's `CAPTION_CHARS`") and in one negative pin (`paper-plate.lead.test.tsx`: the page does not contain `plateCaption`).
