### 2.2 The component: one `SelectionToolbar`, three placements

Keep `selection-toolbar.tsx` (the page imports it, the tests are named after it) and extend it. Its job is unchanged: show the actions for the settled selection, go when the selection goes, never move the caret (`onMouseDown` keeps the selection), publish nothing the page does not already hold.

**Buttons:** "Explain this" (only when `canExplain`, as P10-08b), "Take notes", "Highlight" (always on in the paper body; in the Summary and answers per O-3). `SELECTION_LABELS` gains `highlight: "Highlight"`. The toolbar's `aria-label="Selection actions"` stays; in the margin it is `aria-orientation="vertical"`.

**Placement is a pure function** (`actionsPlace`, beside `toolbarPlace`):

| mode | when | where |
|---|---|---|
| `margin` | viewport ≥ 1024, a margin rect exists and is open, and no open card covers the group's height | `left = margin.left + 8`; `top = clamp(bounds.top, margin.top + 4, margin.bottom − height − 4)`, so the first button is level with the first line; vertical group |
| `above` | 768–1023 (no margin); or ≥ 1024 with the margin collapsed; or an open card covers `[top, top + height]` | exactly today's pill: `toolbarPlace` (above the first line, under it when there is no room), horizontal, three buttons, about 285 px wide |
| `bar` | under 768 | nothing drawn; the thumb bar (2.5) |

It returns `null` (nothing drawn) when the selection is wholly out of view, as now (`bounds.bottom <= DOCK_TOP_PX || bounds.top >= viewport.height`). While the first line has scrolled above the margin's top but the selection is still partly in view, the group is held at `margin.top + 4`.

**Re-measure** on scroll, resize, the margin's own size changes (a `ResizeObserver` on the margin element when it exists) and every change of the window stack (`useWindows.subscribe`) and of `docked`. That fixes defect (b). Hysteresis: a switch between `margin` and `above` caused by a card needs 12 px of travel in either direction, so a selection scrolling past a card's edge does not flicker.

**What it presses.** Explain this → the page's `openExplain` (unchanged: `page.tsx:925`; with the passage already open it moves to the thread's input, `openBox` `:1002`). Take notes → `takeNote(target)` (unchanged: `page.tsx:928`, `sticky-note.tsx:240`). Highlight → a new `onHighlight(target: ExplainSelection)`: P11-04 designs what it does; here only its call. ### 2.6 When the selection is under a card already open

The common case is selecting again in the paragraph the explain card is level with: the card's top is that paragraph's top, so a group at the selection's first line would sit on the card's Close X. Rule (default **D4**): *if any open card's rect intersects the group's rect (4 px slack), the group is drawn in `above` mode, the pill position the owner already has*, and returns to the margin when the card closes or the selection leaves that height. It never covers a card; it reuses the proven pill code; and it is one rule, the same one that serves 768–1023 and the collapsed margin. Alternatives: **D1** overlay the group on the card (transient, but it hides the Close X in the common case above); **D5** a permanent 120 px action lane at the margin's left edge (costs the margin 120 px at every width, and 1280 has 128 to spare). The owner decides (O-2).

An open explain card is unchanged by a new selection (P8-02): it stays, and pressing Explain this takes the new passage into the same card in place. A selection inside a card's own text is no target (`placeOf` needs a paper paragraph or a `data-explain-source`), so the group never appears for card text.

## 3. The card stack (question 3)

### 3.1 What stacks what today

- **Sticky notes and the notes window:** `useWindows` (zustand, `lib/reader/windows.ts`) holds `{id, kind, title, order}`; `stackLayout` (`:119`) and `stackRows` (`:165`) place them as a cascade: oldest at the top, each later one 40 px lower (`WINDOW_HEADER_PX`, `:68`), the last (raised) whole, a header press = `raise`. `StickyNotes` (`sticky-note.tsx:358`) and `NotesWindow` (`notes-window.tsx:234`) each render their own `fixed` cards from those rows.
- **The explain card is not in it.** `WindowKind` already lists `"explain"` (`windows.ts:17`) but nothing opens one; `stackRows` excludes it. The two sides coordinate by reading each other: the column reads `[data-explain-dock]`'s rect every 250 ms (`useColumn`, `:178`; `columnTop`, `:78`) and moves above or below it; the explain card reads the store's need (`stackRoom`, `:153`) and shrinks (`dockInStack`, `explain-dock.ts:224`, used at `explain-box.tsx:1109`). The P10-09c checkpoint names the planned merge as BACKLOG-66; it was never done.
- **Measured at 1440 × 900** (key, two stickies, then Explain): sticky 1 header 48–88, sticky 2 (front) 88–255, the explain card 263–872 (z 70, over the stack's 60–61); with one sticky it is the same shape (`out`: m3; `shots/m3-note2-explain-1440.png`). So today's explain card does *not* show a header-only state: it is always the big card.
- **The figure viewer** is a modal (`figure-lightbox.tsx`: `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, a portal, its own Escape, `:76-79, :156`). P11-05 moves it into the margin as a card; the `MODAL_OVER_PAGE` Escape ordering in `explain-box.tsx:896` then no longer needs it.

### 3.2 The one contract

A **card** is: an `id`, a `kind` (`sticky | notes | explain | figure | highlight`), a `title`, an optional `headline` (the quote head a sticky shows next to its title), a header of 40 px with the title, the headline and the X, and a body. One card is forward and whole; every other shows only its header; a header press brings it forward (`raise`); the X closes it; opening a card brings it forward. The `order` clock in `useWindows` is the one ordering.

- **Store:** `useWindows` as it is, with `WindowKind` extended by `"figure" | "highlight"` and the `explain` kind finally used (`id` fixed, one instance).
- **Frame:** a new `components/reader/margin-card.tsx`, `MarginCard({ id, kind, title, headline?, closeAt: "start" | "end", front, top, height, z, onRaise, onClose, children })`: the header, the behind and front states, the border, the `fixed` box at the margin's frame, `data-margin-card`. `StickyWindowView`'s header block (`sticky-note.tsx:84-106`) is the template. Each kind keeps its own body component.
- **Layout:** `stackLayout` takes a per-kind front height: sticky 260; highlight 140; explain its content, at most the room; notes and figure all the room (today only notes: `NOTES_MIN_FRONT_PX`). The rows are for every kind, so `stackRows` stops excluding `explain`.
- **X side:** a prop, not a rule. Explain keeps its big highlighted X at the top-left (P10 8.4, built); the sticky's X is at the right; the figure's X is at the top-right (the owner's item 1). I would not unify them without the owner (O-7).
- **Mount:** inside `[data-margin-stack]`, a child of P11-01's margin. Until the pane lands the cards stay `fixed` at the margin frame from `readMargin` (so `right-0 w-[min(360px,100vw)]` in `sticky-note.tsx:82` becomes `left`/`width` from the frame).
- **Highlight's card (P11-04):** optional; if it has one it is `kind: "highlight"`, `closeAt: "end"`, front height 140, opened through `useWindows.open`.

### 3.3 Where a card opens: recommendation

**R-open (default).** The first card of an empty margin opens level with the selection that opened it: its top is `clamp(first-line top, margin.top, margin.bottom − its least front height)`, which is the explain card's standing rule (P8-02, P9-03b) extended to every kind. Every later card joins the same cascade, anchored where the oldest card stands, cascading downward, with the front whole; where the cascade does not fit below its anchor the whole column is lifted, never above `margin.top` (the existing `minTop` branch of `stackLayout`, `:119-140`). A lone explain card keeps following its selection's paragraph as the page scrolls; the moment a second card joins, the cascade's origin freezes (a stack that moves with the text could not be a stack of headers).

**A2 (alternative).** Every card opens at the top of the margin; deterministic, no follow logic, and it drops the owner's standing "level with the first line of the selected paragraph" for the explain card. The owner decides (O-6).

Why not "each card at its own selection's height": headers in a cascade must stay visible and reachable (`windows.test.ts`, "every window's header is reachable", P10-18/P10-31); cards hung at independent heights overlap, and a 600 px explain card would push the others off the screen.

Joining the explain card to the cascade (BACKLOG-66) is the riskiest hunk of this item; it matches the P10 rule 7.1a (notes, explain this and the figure "stack like cards") that the manager reads into item 1's cut-off sentence. It is the last hunk of C-2 and has its own gate.
