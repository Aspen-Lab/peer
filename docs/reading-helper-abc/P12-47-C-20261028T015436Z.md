## The rules as built

**R9a + R10a (commit 4; replaces R2a).** Floors unchanged (120 / 120 / 20,000) for a candidate; nothing below a floor becomes a figure of its own. For every `Figure N` typed caption line (`CAPTION_RE`) whose figure is above it:
- *Band*: from the bottom of the nearest body-like line above the caption (a line of at least 70 characters and at least 100 pt wide, or any typed caption line, `Figure` / `Table` / `Scheme` / `Chart`) down to the caption; with no such line, from y = 46 (the top furniture band). A piece belongs to the band when its top is at or under `bandTop - 1` and its bottom at or above `caption.top + 2`.
- *Seed*: the accepted candidate captioned by that line. A caption below a region whose bottom is more than 5 pt under the caption's top is "above its figure" and is skipped. With no candidate the caption is an *orphan*: it seeds on the piece nearest above it, within 220 pt (`MAX_CAPTION_DISTANCE_BELOW`).
- *What is taken*, every piece in the band, **no gap limit**: sub-floor pieces of at least 3,000 pt² with both sides at least 30 pt (R9a), and above-floor pieces that carry no typed caption of their own (R10a; "carries" means `bestCaptionForRegion` says explicit or excluded, so a Table caption counts too).
- *Orphan*: needs 3 or more pieces, a union that passes the unchanged floors, and the union must pair with the same caption line (explicit, not excluded). It becomes a `drawing` candidate. No orphan is made when a candidate already has that caption number.
- A raster that was taken and was a candidate of its own (an uncaptioned above-floor raster) is dropped from the list (85 % overlap, the engine's own duplicate rule), so it is not returned twice.
- Cost checks I kept from the first version: a piece is not taken if taking it would newly cover another **captioned** region or a typed caption line (other than its own caption's); the grown list is re-sorted into page order.
- Two things B's prototype (`engine-proto.ts`, `growCaptioned`) had that the ruling text does not mention, kept because B measured P4–P6 with them: on a page of **two or more typed caption lines** the band is read in columns (a body-like line bounds the band only if it spans the caption's left edge; a piece must reach `[caption.x0 - 15, max(caption.x1, caption.x0 + half a page) + 15]`), and a raster wider than 0.9 of the page is a background, not a piece. B's prototype also carried a 60 pt growth gap (`B41_GAP`); the ruling says none, so none is built.

**R3a, figure text (commit 2).** After the band growth, each accepted, captioned region absorbs, repeatedly, text lines that: are at most 60 characters; are not a typed caption line; are not in the caption's block; sit on the figure's side of the caption (above a caption below; below a caption above); lie within 6 pt of the region on both axes; lie inside the region's width widened by 6 pt (the line may stick out of the region by up to 6 pt, and the region then grows to hold the whole line); and **do not share a block with any line over 60 characters** (that is the body-text test: the short last line of a body paragraph is body). I generalised "above the caption" to "on the figure's side of it" so a caption above a figure also gets its axis title (B's prototype did the same); that is the only reading beyond the brief.

**R5a, the clip (commit 3).** `renderRegion` takes the paired caption line's box. If its top is inside the padded clip (a caption below), the clip's bottom is `max(region bottom, caption top - 0.5)`; for a caption above, the clip's top is `min(region top, caption bottom + 0.5)`. A caption outside the padding changes nothing, so "box plus 10 pt" is as before (that test is unchanged and green). It applies to any paired caption line, typed or the nearest line of an uncaptioned raster (B's prototype did).

**R8a, the caption's end (commit 5).** In `mergeCaptionLines`, a following line ends the caption (and none after it is taken) when its top is more than 1.4 × the **median** of the pitches (top to top) of the caption lines taken so far below the line above it. A one-line caption has no pitch yet, so its second line is joined as before. No same-block exemption (MuPDF puts a body paragraph in its own block when it is set apart, and a pitch jump inside a block is not a thing). **The 500-character cap is NOT raised** — see "For the manager", item 1.
