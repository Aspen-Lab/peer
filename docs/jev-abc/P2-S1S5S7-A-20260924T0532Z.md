**F-A-P2R3-03 [INFORMATIONAL — LOW/VERY LOW SEVERITY] DOI backfill is skipped whenever the survivor's own `metadata.doi` is already present but malformed, not just when it's genuinely missing.**

Reproduced directly (§1p.G CONFORMANCE check 8, informational sub-case). This is a literally-correct reading of §1p.G(4)'s "a DOI **missing** on the survivor" wording, so not scored as a violation, but a generous reading ("missing or unusable") would behave differently.
