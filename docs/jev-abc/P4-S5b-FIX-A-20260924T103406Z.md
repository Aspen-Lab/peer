1. **[RE-DELIVERY RISK, real and likely-common — not previously assigned to this FIX, found via the brief's own requested auth-loading-window probing] `currentOwnerKey()` cannot distinguish "signed in, but entitlement not resolved yet" from "confirmed signed out," and this FIX's arm-time capture happens far too early (after one internal microtask) to wait for a real network round trip.**
