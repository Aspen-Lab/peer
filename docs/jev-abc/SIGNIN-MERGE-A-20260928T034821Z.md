3. *(Severity: informational, not a defect)* — `mergePreferenceLedger`'s recency rule defaults to local whenever local lacks a usable timestamp, even if remote has one. A defensible reading of an ambiguous ruling clause, consistently documented; flagged for awareness only.

### Answer to check 5, one line
